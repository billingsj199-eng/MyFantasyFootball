# Training Camp Report — July 27, 2026 (End of Day)

_First scan of the 2026 preseason cycle — covers camp-opening storylines through Monday evening._

## Top Storylines

- **Chiefs OC Eric Bieniemy steps away from camp** after his wife was allegedly shot by their son at home Sunday night; pass-game coordinator Joe Bleymaier takes over play-calling duties with no return timetable. Worth monitoring for any early-season offensive continuity wrinkles around Mahomes and the KC skill players.
- **Patrick Mahomes (KC) cleared for full practice** to open Chiefs camp, recovering from a torn ACL/LCL — no restrictions reported, on track for Week 1.
- **Jacoby Brissett re-signs with Arizona** on a new 1-year, $15.5M deal, locking him in as camp's presumptive starter over Gardner Minshew and rookie Carson Beck.
- **Vita Vea requests a trade from Tampa Bay** after stalled extension talks — a DT move with no direct fantasy impact, but worth tracking if it signals wider Bucs roster turnover.
- **Micah Parsons (DAL)** remains limited/out with back tightness amid ongoing contract standoff — no fantasy-skill-position angle, but a storyline to watch for Cowboys team-wide implications.
- **49ers HC Kyle Shanahan** will coach in a limited capacity to start camp after a serious July 14 car accident (broken nose/ribs/hand, concussion); assistant Chris Foerster fills in. No word yet on how this affects offensive install pace for Purdy/McCaffrey/Kirk.
- **Zach Charbonnet (SEA) placed on active/PUP** recovering from a torn ACL, clearing the runway for rookie Jadarian Price to open camp as Seattle's lead back.
- **Jonathon Brooks (CAR) took first-team reps** in his first fully healthy camp since back-to-back ACL tears — Dave Canales says he looks like "the guy I remember."
- **Cleveland's QB and WR rooms are wide open**: Shedeur Sanders and Deshaun Watson are splitting first-team reps, while rookie KC Concepcion is pushing veteran Jerry Jeudy for the top receiver job.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Seattle backfield**: Jadarian Price (rookie, Notre Dame) is fully healthy and practicing without restriction — in line to open the season as Seahawks RB1 with Charbonnet on PUP.
- **Arizona QB room**: Brissett's new deal, plus Minshew and 3rd-round rookie Carson Beck, form a four-man rotation early in camp; Brissett is the clear starter for now.
- **Cleveland QB battle**: Sanders and Watson are splitting first-team reps; reporting suggests Watson maintains an edge once pads come on and the evaluation intensifies.
- **Cleveland WR room**: Rookie KC Concepcion (1st round, Texas A&M) is competing directly with Jerry Jeudy for the top target share; both expected to coexist, but early practice reps matter for target hierarchy.
- **Jacksonville backfield**: With Travis Etienne now in New Orleans, HC Liam Coen says he's "expecting a big year" from Bhayshul Tuten, pointing to him as the clear early-down/goal-line option.
- **New Orleans backfield**: Alvin Kamara restructured his deal (agreed July 15) to stay in New Orleans; expect a competitive/split workload with new signee Travis Etienne rather than a bell-cow role.

## Rookie Watch

- **Jadarian Price (RB, SEA)** — healthy, unrestricted, and now the presumptive Week 1 starter with Charbonnet on PUP. ADP (84) hasn't caught up to his opportunity yet.
- **Carson Beck (QB, ARI)** — 3rd-round pick, reportedly sharp in his first NFL camp practice; no near-term fantasy relevance behind Brissett, but a dynasty name to file away.
- **KC Concepcion (WR, CLE)** — 1st-round pick (24th overall) already pushing Jerry Jeudy for the top WR job; going later than Jeudy in early fantasy drafts, camp reps worth tracking.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Jadarian Price (RB, SEA) — Charbonnet's PUP placement hands him the early-down job.
- Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR) — first fully healthy camp, already logging first-team reps.
- Bhayshul Tuten (RB, JAX) — coach's public "big year" endorsement with Etienne gone.

**Fallers**
- Christian Kirk (WR, SF) — calf strain right out of the gate muddies his early role in a crowded WR room.
- Alvin Kamara (RB, NO) — restructured deal (pay cut) alongside Etienne signing signals a real timeshare, not a bell-cow workload.
- Zach Charbonnet (RB, SEA) — PUP placement all but confirms a slow start to his season.
