# Training Camp Report — July 28, 2026 (End of Day)

_Covers the day's practice reports and evening news since Tuesday midday's scan, as the final wave of teams report for camp._

## Top Storylines

- **Kirk Cousins named Raiders starter over rookie Fernando Mendoza** to open camp — HC Klint Kubiak said Cousins "deserves it because he's played really good football." Mendoza will get earned second/third-string-heavy reps as the plan for now. Locks in Cousins' Week 1 value, pushes Mendoza to backup/dynasty-stash territory.
- **Tucker Kraft (GB) starts camp on the PUP list** recovering from a torn ACL, alongside Luke Musgrave, Aaron Banks, Javon Hargrave and Kamal Hadden — but both Kraft and the team expect him to be full-go for the Sept. 13 opener vs. Minnesota. No long-term fantasy concern, but early camp reps go elsewhere.
- **Lamar Jackson's contract standoff remains unresolved** as Ravens veterans report — Jackson has two years left on his deal and a cap hit set to balloon to $84.3M next year; some insiders think he could bet on himself and play out 2026 before signing an extension. Worth tracking for holdout/distraction risk, though no absence expected yet.
- **Baker Mayfield's self-imposed contract deadline passed** with no new Bucs deal — he'll play 2026 on his expiring contract and hit free agency in March. No on-field impact, but adds a "prove-it year" subplot to Tampa Bay's passing offense.
- **Bills WR/TE room banged up entering camp** — Curtis Samuel (hamstring) and Elijah Moore (leg soreness) have each missed practice time, plus TEs Dalton Kincaid and Dawson Knox have been out. Opens up early-camp reps in an already muddled Buffalo pass-catcher pecking order.
- **Kayshon Boutte (NE) turning heads early in Patriots camp** with another highlight-reel deep catch — building on 2025 flashes as a potential WR2 riser behind Stefon Diggs.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Raiders QB room**: Kirk Cousins is the clear Week 1 starter; Fernando Mendoza begins his rookie camp taking reps behind Cousins with the 2s/3s. Per The Athletic, how long Cousins holds the job depends more on Mendoza's development pace than Cousins' performance.
- **Buccaneers QB room**: No QB change — Mayfield's contract limbo resolves into the status quo, he remains the unquestioned 2026 starter on an expiring deal.
- **Packers TE room**: Kraft's PUP stint (with Musgrave also out) muddies early-camp TE snaps, though no threat to Kraft's Week 1 role once activated.

## Rookie Watch

- **Fernando Mendoza (QB, LV)** — No. 1 overall pick begins camp as the backup behind Kirk Cousins; no near-term fantasy value, but a clear dynasty stash if an in-season change happens.
- **Dalevon Campbell (WR, LAC)** and **Andrew Armstrong (WR, KC)** — both rookies drawing praise and earning extra reps early in camp; deep-league/dynasty watch names only at this stage.

## Risers / Fallers
