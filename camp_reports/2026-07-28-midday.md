# Training Camp Report — July 28, 2026 (Midday)

_Covers overnight/morning news since Monday evening's end-of-day scan, updated through midday Tuesday as veterans report to camp league-wide._

## Top Storylines

- **Malik Nabers (NYG) will avoid the PUP list** to open Giants camp — HC John Harbaugh said Tuesday he'll work his way into practice "one way or another," with a Week 1 return vs. Dallas called a "realistic possibility." Big fantasy swing given his WR13-range ADP built around missing time.
- **Cam Skattebo (NYG) trending ahead of schedule** — Harbaugh says the rookie RB will be "ready early on in camp," further along in his ankle/tibia recovery than Nabers is in his knee recovery.
- **Vikings QB battle intensifying, not resolving quickly** — Kyler Murray (signed after his Cardinals release) vs. J.J. McCarthy is expected to run all the way through the preseason per Ian Rapoport, with McCarthy's earlier "playbook command" edge reportedly narrowing as Murray builds chemistry with Jordan Addison. Keeps both out of confident streaming range into August.
- **Jonathan Taylor hold-in speculation** swirls as Colts veterans report, per The Athletic's James Boyd — would let him keep leverage in his contract push without missing game checks; Colts are dangerously thin at RB behind him (Giddens, McGowan).
- **Rashee Rice (KC) cleared to practice** — Andy Reid said Friday Rice is "doing great" following his knee debridement and 30-day jail stint (released June 16); no restrictions reported for the start of camp.
- **Travis Hunter (JAX) looks fully explosive** in his return from last year's knee injury — reportedly clocked 22.6 mph in offseason testing and has been dominant in 1-on-1 reps, including a highlight end-zone grab over CB Jourdan Lewis.
- **Brian Thomas Jr. (JAX) off to a slower start** — through the first several padless practices, he and Trevor Lawrence haven't connected as consistently as their OTA/minicamp chemistry suggested, per beat reporting. Not alarming yet, but worth tracking.
- **Browns WR room getting more crowded** — rookie Denzel Boston is drawing "MVP of our spring" praise from teammates, adding a third real name (with KC Concepcion and Jerry Jeudy) to a target-share battle that was already murky.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Vikings QB room**: Kyler Murray vs. J.J. McCarthy is a genuine, extended competition per Rapoport — no clear leader as camp opens, expect this to drag into preseason games.
- **Jacksonville WR room**: Hunter is winning nearly every 1-on-1 rep early in camp; Brian Thomas Jr. has had a quieter start connecting with Lawrence through the first week of practices. Early-camp noise, not a depth-chart change — but the target hierarchy is worth re-checking as pads come on.
- **Cleveland WR room**: Jerry Jeudy is still labeled the "bell cow" by GM Andrew Berry, but rookies KC Concepcion and Denzel Boston are both pushing for real early-season snaps, making this one of camp's most unsettled target-share situations.
- **New England backfield**: TreVeyon Henderson spent the offseason on pass protection/blitz pickup — the exact skill that limited his rookie-year passing-down snaps. If it sticks in camp, his role (35 catches as a rookie) could expand toward true 3-down duty.
- **Indianapolis backfield**: Extremely thin behind Jonathan Taylor (only Giddens/McGowan) — any hold-in would be a business decision, not expected to cost him game snaps or bell-cow volume once he suits up.
- **New York Giants backfield/WR corps**: Both of the Giants' top offensive skill-position pieces (Nabers, Skattebo) are on encouraging recovery tracks per Harbaugh's Tuesday comments — no committee/timeshare threat currently signaled for either spot once healthy.

## Rookie Watch

- **Cam Skattebo (RB, NYG)** — ahead of schedule in ankle recovery; positioned as Giants' lead back once he's cleared, per Harbaugh.
- **Denzel Boston (WR, CLE)** — teammates calling him the spring's "MVP"; a real name to know in a suddenly crowded Browns WR room alongside fellow rookie KC Concepcion.
- **Jordyn Tyson (WR, NO)** — Saints' No. 8 overall pick; talented complement to Chris Olave, but a hamstring "maintenance" plan has kept him off the field for most of the spring — camp participation is the thing to watch.
