# Training Camp Report — July 29, 2026 (End of Day)

_Covers Wednesday's practice reports and evening news since today's midday scan — a rougher injury day than a big usage-news day._

## Top Storylines

- **Chris Brazzell II (CAR) confirmed with a moderate LCL tear** — up to 8 weeks recovery, which could cost the buzzy rookie WR the start of the regular season. Tests confirm the injury is unrelated to soreness that had already cost him two earlier practices.
- **Ricky Pearsall (SF) knee flares up again** — swelling tied to last year's PCL injury kept him out of Wednesday's practice, with the team calling it a "concern" and "exploring all options." Adds to an already chaotic 49ers WR room (Kirk's calf, Aiyuk's limbo).
- **Adonai Mitchell (NYJ) is this camp's biggest quiet riser** — beating corners deep in 1-on-1s again Wednesday and solidifying himself as Gang Green's clear WR2 behind Garrett Wilson after a buzzy OTA/minicamp stretch.
- **Jonathon Brooks (CAR) took his first padded practice in two years** — no hesitation in his cuts per HC Dave Canales ("looks like the guy I remember"), continuing to build a real threat to Chuba Hubbard's early-down role if he stays healthy.
- **Bijan Robinson (ATL) and Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) hold-ins continue** with no urgency signaled — Lions HC Dan Campbell was noncommittal on a timeline ("it'll come as it comes"). Still no missed-game risk expected.
- **Falcons QB situation unchanged** — Tua Tagovailoa's back tightness is still described as minor with a return expected soon; Michael Penix Jr. remains capped at individual/7-on-7 work.
- **Stefon Diggs remains unsigned** as camps open around the league, with Washington and Baltimore floated as top guesses — a name to watch but zero fantasy value until he lands somewhere.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Jets WR2**: Adonai Mitchell continues to look like the clear No. 2 behind Garrett Wilson — the drops/consistency questions from his Colts tenure are the only thing standing between him and a real breakout role.
- **Panthers backfield**: Brooks getting his first padded reps in two years alongside Hubbard (still the presumptive Week 1 lead back) — a legitimate 1-2 punch brewing if Brooks stays on the field.
- **49ers WR room**: Now three separate question marks (Pearsall's knee, Kirk's calf, Aiyuk's unresolved reinstatement) behind Evans — worth downgrading confidence in any 49ers WR2/3 bet until camp clarifies.
- **Falcons/Lions backfields**: Robinson and Gibbs hold-ins are pure contract leverage, not health — no change to either player's Week 1 outlook expected once deals land.

## Rookie Watch

- **Chris Brazzell II (WR, CAR)** — promising camp cut short by a confirmed moderate LCL tear; shelve dynasty stash expectations for 6-8 weeks.
- **Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR)** — technically a 2024 draftee but effectively a rookie season given back-to-back ACL tears; first padded practice since being drafted is a real milestone.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Adonai Mitchell (WR, NYJ) — camp's clearest ADP riser, cementing a WR2 role next to Garrett Wilson.
- Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR) — first pads in two years, coach praise, real 1B threat behind Hubbard.

**Fallers**
- Chris Brazzell II (WR, CAR) — confirmed LCL tear likely costs him 6-8 weeks and possibly Week 1.
- Ricky Pearsall (WR, SF) — recurring knee swelling adds real uncertainty to his role entering camp.
