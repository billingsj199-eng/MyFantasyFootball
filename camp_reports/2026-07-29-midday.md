# Training Camp Report — July 29, 2026 (Midday)

_Covers overnight/morning news since Tuesday evening's end-of-day scan, as camp chaos hits Atlanta and hold-ins spread to Detroit._

## Top Storylines

- **Falcons QB room implodes overnight** — Tua Tagovailoa is sidelined by a back tightness flare-up (not considered serious, expected back "next week" per Schefter), Michael Penix Jr. still isn't cleared for full team drills recovering from his November ACL tear, and Atlanta released Trevor Siemian and signed veteran Cooper Rush for emergency camp depth — with UDFA Jack Strand taking first-team reps in the interim. No panic move yet, but a name to watch through the week.
- **Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) and Bijan Robinson (ATL) are both staging hold-ins** amid extension talks — present at camp but not practicing, reportedly in a "game of chicken" over which RB resets the position's market first. No missed-game risk expected and Schefter suggests Gibbs' deal could come "relatively quickly," but worth tracking if either drags into August.
- **Chris Brazzell II (CAR) helped off the field with a leg injury** on a padless practice — the rookie 3rd-rounder had been drawing real camp buzz in a thin Panthers WR room; couldn't put weight on it, which is the concerning detail pending team update.
- **De'Zhaun Stribling (SF) is turning heads early**, with Brock Purdy singling him out for praise — a second-round rookie WR working into the mix of an unsettled 49ers receiver room.
- **Brandon Aiyuk (SF) says he won't file for reinstatement** and wants to sign with the Commanders instead — effectively takes him off San Francisco's 2026 board; no resolution timeline yet, watch for a Washington fit if it happens.
- **Eagles WR2 battle heating up** — rookie 1st-rounder Makai Lemon is an early camp frontrunner over incumbent Dontayvion Wicks, despite Wicks' prior familiarity with new OC Sean Mannion from their Packers days.
- **Tucker Kraft (GB) update**: GM Brian Gutekunst said a Week 1 return from his ACL tear is "very much a possibility," a modestly more optimistic timeline than the original PUP designation suggested.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Falcons QB room**: Tua remains Atlanta's Week 1 starter in name, but between his back issue and Penix's still-limited practice status, there's real short-term uncertainty about who's actually taking first-team reps this week. Cooper Rush's signing is pure emergency depth, not a threat to either.
- **Lions/Falcons backfields**: Gibbs and Robinson hold-ins are business decisions, not health concerns — expect both to still open the season as their team's clear lead back once new deals land.
- **Eagles WR2 spot**: Makai Lemon (rookie) reportedly ahead of Dontayvion Wicks early in camp — a real target-share battle to track as pads come on.
- **49ers WR room**: Still unsettled with Aiyuk unresolved and HC Kyle Shanahan sidelined from camp recovering from a car accident/concussion (an assistant-led install for now); Stribling's early buzz and Christian Kirk's calf strain (reported previously) both add to the muddle behind Mike Evans/Ricky Pearsall.
