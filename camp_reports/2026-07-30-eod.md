# Training Camp Report — July 30, 2026 (End of Day)

_Covers the afternoon/evening since today's midday scan — a contract-heavy day (one megadeal, two stalled, one hold-in pair) plus a wave of TE/WR injury notes and camp-battle buzz._

## Top Storylines

- **Chris Olave (NO) signed a 4-year, $132M extension** (~$90M guaranteed, $33M/yr, 6th-highest-paid WR) — locks him in as New Orleans' clear long-term WR1 with zero hold-in risk.
- **Deebo Samuel is reuniting with the 49ers** on a 1-year deal worth up to $7M after one season in Washington — instant target competition in an already banged-up SF WR room (Kirk's calf, Pearsall's knee).
- **Bijan Robinson (ATL) and Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) both missed practice again Thursday** amid extension talks seeking to reset the RB market above Saquon Barkley's $20.6M/yr — Falcons–Robinson talks are trending positive, but Rapoport called the Gibbs situation "concerning." No missed games yet, just hold-ins.
- **Baker Mayfield (TB) says he feels "disrespected" and "undervalued"** after Tampa Bay's extension offer (2 years, well short of his $50M+ AAV ask) — a real distraction risk for the Bucs offense entering his walk year.
- **De'Von Achane (MIA) is fully cleared and a "full go"** for camp with no restrictions after offseason shoulder cleanup — no lingering concern after his 1,838-yard, 12-TD 2025 breakout.
- **Rome Odunze (CHI) says his foot "feels amazing"** — confirms full health entering camp, keeping his Bears WR1 track (next to Luther Burden III) fully intact.
- **Luther Burden III (CHI) is drawing early target buzz**, working with the 1s at a "quicker pace" as he competes to absorb DJ Moore's vacated ~85 targets.
- **Jonathon Brooks (CAR) continued getting 1st-team reps** with positive coach quotes from Dave Canales, keeping the Panthers backfield battle with Chuba Hubbard alive.
- **Taysom Hill confirmed his time in New Orleans is over** (farewell message, no retirement announced, remains unsigned) — closes the book on Saints goal-line/gadget TD-vulture risk for now.
- **Tucker Kraft (GB) and Dalton Kincaid (BUF) both have TE injury notes to track** — Kraft opened camp on PUP (ACL) with GM optimism for Week 1; Kincaid missed a practice with knee soreness tied to a recurring PCL issue.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **49ers WR room**: Deebo Samuel's signing adds to an already crowded mix (Mike Evans, Christian Kirk banged up, Ricky Pearsall's knee, rookie De'Zhaun Stribling) — target shares here are genuinely unsettled heading into camp.
- **Bears WR2**: Burden III getting the bulk of early-camp targets in the race to replace DJ Moore's volume, though 2nd-year WR leaps are historically inconsistent — buzz stock, not a lock yet.
- **Panthers backfield**: Brooks' continued 1st-team run keeps this a real committee question behind Hubbard, CAR's presumptive lead back.
- **Chiefs WR room**: A rough Wednesday full-team practice (Tyquan Thornton drop, Jalen Royals fumble) had new WR coach Chad O'Shea's group labeled the "most disappointing" unit on the day — context for the room behind rookie Cyrus Allen's buzz from the midday report.
- **Falcons/Lions RB contracts**: Robinson's side reportedly more active than Gibbs' in extension talks — a situation to watch for dynasty value, not a workload change yet.
