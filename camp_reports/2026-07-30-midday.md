# Training Camp Report — July 30, 2026 (Midday)

_Covers overnight/morning news since Wednesday evening's scan — a run of positive recovery updates (Nabers, Taylor) alongside a scary but low-fantasy-relevance Bengals injury._

## Top Storylines

- **Malik Nabers (NYG) looked fully healthy in his first camp practice** — ran full routes and cut with no hesitation, months after tearing his ACL/meniscus and a spring setback that had clouded his Week 1 outlook. Biggest green light yet for his recovery.
- **Jonathan Taylor (IND) reported to camp with no hold-in** despite unresolved extension talks — called negotiations "good conversations" and said he has "faith" a deal gets done. Workhorse role and volume are unaffected for now.
- **Bhayshul Tuten (JAX) is pulling away early** — called the "strongest offensive player, period" through Jaguars camp Day 1, while committee rival Chris Rodriguez Jr. is limited to individual work easing back from offseason foot surgery.
- **Jeremiyah Love (ARI) dominated red-zone reps** in the Cardinals' first padded practice, working with the 1st and 2nd team and flashing pass-game/home-run ability — a real threat to eat into the Conner/Allgeier timeshare early.
- **Cyrus Allen (KC), a 5th-round rookie WR, caught a TD from Patrick Mahomes in 7-on-7** and earned 1st-team reps — deep-league buzz stock in an already crowded Chiefs WR room.
- **Tua Tagovailoa (ATL) returned to practice Thursday** for individual drills after Wednesday's back-tightness scare — no lingering concern, Falcons QB1 track intact.
- **Shemar Stewart (CIN, DE) was carted off Wednesday with a hyperextended knee** — not a fantasy skill position, but a lengthy absence would be a real blow to Cincinnati's pass rush and DST value; severity still being evaluated.
