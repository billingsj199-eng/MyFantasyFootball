# Training Camp Report — July 31, 2026 (End of Day)

_Covers Friday's practice reports and evening news since this morning's midday scan — a QB-battle shakeup in Cleveland, a Year 2 breakout brewing at Bears TE, and a quieter-but-real update on the Vikings' QB race._

## Top Storylines

- **Deshaun Watson threw his third interception in as many camp practices** (picked off by LB Quincy Williams), even while taking first-team reps — keeps Cleveland's QB competition alive and Shedeur Sanders in the mix.
- **Colston Loveland is drawing "best TE in the NFL" buzz** — Caleb Williams and Bears beat reporters say the Year 2 tight end has been "unguardable" through Thursday/Friday practices, with some now pegging him a real threat to unseat Bowers/McBride as fantasy TE1.
- **Vikings QB rumors around Kyler Murray have reportedly gone quiet after two good weeks from J.J. McCarthy** — a real shift from earlier in the week when Murray was said to be "closing" McCarthy's edge; the battle is expected to run into the preseason either way.
- **Quinshon Judkins looked fully back at Browns camp**, with RB coach Duce Staley saying "the sky is the limit" and that Judkins can "play all three downs" after last year's ankle/fibula injury — recovery looks not just complete but ahead of schedule.
- **Tua Tagovailoa said his back "feels great"** and that his limited practice work is purely precautionary — a reassuring notch up from the recurrence reported in the midday scan.
- **Brian Thomas Jr. erupted late in Friday's practice** with three 20+ yard grabs off Trevor Lawrence, a clear reversal of the "hasn't fully connected" note from earlier in camp.
- **Denzel Boston made the "catch of camp"** for the Browns, a leaping sideline grab off Shedeur Sanders — building on his buzz from earlier in the week.
- **Rookie TE Eli Raridon (NE) is emerging as the clear No. 2 tight end** behind Hunter Henry after Julian Hill's season-ending IR, with two red-zone TDs in Thursday's practice.
- **Cyrus Allen (KC) continues building real trust with Patrick Mahomes** — Andy Reid: "I think Pat's got some trust there" — as the 5th-round rookie keeps stacking good days.
