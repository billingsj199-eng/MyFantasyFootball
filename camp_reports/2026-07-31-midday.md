# Training Camp Report — July 31, 2026 (Midday)

_Covers overnight/morning news since last night's end-of-day scan — an injury-heavy update out of Atlanta and Carolina, continuing RB hold-ins, and fresh camp buzz for a few Year 2 receivers._

## Top Storylines

- **Chris Brazzell II (CAR) will have season-ending knee surgery** — the torn LCL suffered Wednesday was initially expected to heal without an operation, but a second opinion pushed the Panthers rookie toward surgery instead. Wipes out his entire rookie season and opens real reps in a thin Panthers WR room.
- **Tua Tagovailoa's (ATL) back "flare-up" recurred** and he's out of practice again; the Falcons signed veteran Cooper Rush for QB depth after moving Trevor Siemian to a non-football injury designation. Not considered long-term, but Atlanta's QB room is thinning fast with Michael Penix Jr. also not yet full-go.
- **Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) missed his third straight practice** in his hold-in, and coach Dan Campbell mentioned Gibbs was also dealing with "a little bit of a back" issue during conditioning — Campbell downplayed it, but it's now tangled with the contract standoff.
- **Bijan Robinson (ATL) sat out again too**, with Falcons–Robinson extension talks reportedly more active than the Lions' talks with Gibbs — still no missed-game risk, just a dynasty-value watch.
- **Terrance Ferguson (LAR) is drawing "de facto WR3" buzz** early in camp — he averaged 21 yards per catch as a rookie and the Rams offense is leaning into more 13-personnel looks that get him on the field as a big slot/flex threat.
- **Ladd McConkey (LAC) is a full go** after fully rehabbing a hamstring strain from spring practices, with new OC Mike McDaniel's quick-throw-heavy scheme viewed as a potential bounce-back setup for him.
- **Tre Harris (LAC) is being described as a "legitimate part of the passing offense"** in Year 2, adding a third real target next to McConkey and Quentin Johnston in an increasingly competitive Chargers WR room.
- **Matthew Golden (GB) is an early standout** per beat reporters after a quiet rookie year (29-361-0) — Green Bay's WR room is down to three clear starters (Golden, Christian Watson, Jayden Reed) after moving on from Doubs and Wicks, opening a bigger target share for him.
- **Rookie WR Ja'Kobi Lane (BAL) flashed** with a highlight catch on Day 2 of Ravens camp, part of a rookie pass-catcher group looking to prove it's ready to contribute early.

- **Falcons QB room**: Tua's recurrence plus Michael Penix Jr. still not cleared for full team drills has thinned Atlanta's healthy QB depth to backups; Cooper Rush signing is a direct response.

- **Packers WR room**: Golden stepping into a clearer top-3 role now that Romeo Doubs and Dontayvion Wicks are gone.
