# Training Camp Report — August 1, 2026 (End of Day)

_Covers Saturday's "Back Together Weekend" practices and evening news since this morning's midday scan — the day's headline is a two-WR injury scare in Kansas City, plus updates on the Bucs' and Vikings' QB situations._

## Top Storylines

- **Chiefs WRs Xavier Worthy and Cyrus Allen both left Saturday's practice on a cart within minutes of each other.** Worthy fell shoulder-first on a contested catch — concerning because it's unclear if it's the same shoulder he had labrum surgery on in January. Allen (5th-round rookie) collided with a teammate on special teams; Schefter reports a shin bruise with nothing torn or broken. Worthy is the real fantasy worry until KC confirms it's not structural; Allen sounds like he dodges a bullet.
- **Baker Mayfield's contract situation is resolved for now** — no extension with Tampa Bay, he'll play out 2026 on his expiring deal and hit free agency next spring. Removes any camp distraction, but adds long-term uncertainty to the Bucs passing offense beyond this year.
- **Vikings QB battle still unsettled** — J.J. McCarthy and Kyler Murray split first-team reps for a second straight day. Kevin O'Connell says he has a decision date in mind, likely before Minnesota's joint practices with Baltimore. Don't lock in either QB's redraft price yet.
- **Stefon Diggs remains a free agent** with camps underway; he's said he's "intrigued" by Washington. No fantasy value until he lands somewhere — worth tracking given 1,000+ yards last season as recently as 2025.
- **Panthers LB Nic Scourton tore his ACL** in Thursday's practice and is done for the season — not fantasy-relevant himself, but worth knowing Carolina's defense takes an early hit.
