# Training Camp Report — August 1, 2026 (Midday)

_Covers overnight/morning news since last night's end-of-day scan — a scarier Ricky Pearsall update out of San Francisco, a Cardinals backfield picture coming into focus, and a minor Shedeur Sanders scare in Cleveland on "Back Together Weekend."_

## Top Storylines

- **Ricky Pearsall's (SF) knee situation has escalated** — a top 49ers insider now reports the recurring PCL swelling could require season-ending surgery, up from Wednesday's "exploring all options" language. Bump Deebo Samuel and Jauan Jennings up in San Francisco's WR pecking order until there's real clarity.
- **Jeremiyah Love (ARI) is drawing rave reviews in pads** — Albert Breer named him the headline takeaway of Cardinals camp, but the touch count says committee: Tyler Allgeier has out-carried the rookie 21-18 through the first week. Love's ceiling remains the highest of the three, but early-season touches may be shared with Allgeier and James Conner.
- **Shedeur Sanders (CLE) sat out Saturday's practice with arm/shoulder soreness**, part of a bigger picture where Cleveland's whole QB room is banged up (Dillon Gabriel — hamstring; Kenny Pickett — also limited), leaving Joe Flacco to soak up first-team reps. Sanders said he's fine; not considered serious, but worth a watch given how thin the Browns' QB depth is right now.
- **Kaelon Black (SF) is getting a longer look** behind Christian McCaffrey with Jordan James still out (fractured rib) — a deep-league/dynasty stash if James' recovery drags.
- **Bijan Robinson (ATL) and Jahmyr Gibbs (DET) are both still holding in** for new deals as camp rolls into its second week — no game-status risk yet, but both are dynasty-value watches until something signs (see Chris Olave below for how these usually resolve).
- **Chris Olave's (NO) hold-in is over** — he signed a 4-year, $132M extension ($90M guaranteed) — a signal these RB situations (Robinson/Gibbs) are more about leverage than actual games missed.
