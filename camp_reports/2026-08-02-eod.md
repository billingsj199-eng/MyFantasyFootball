# Training Camp Report — August 2, 2026 (End of Day)

_Quiet close to "Back Together Weekend" — no major injuries since the midday update. The headline is Travis Hunter's best two-way practice of his career, plus a depth-chart add in San Francisco's backfield._

## Top Storylines

- **Travis Hunter had the practice of his career.** In Sunday's Day 4 session, Hunter intercepted a pass off backup QB Nick Mullens, then three plays later hauled in a contested 35-yard catch from Trevor Lawrence. Still the same "wild card" fantasy profile — his offensive snap volume is what will decide his value — but the flashes keep coming.
- **49ers add RB depth behind McCaffrey.** San Francisco signed veteran Khalil Herbert (formerly Bears/Jets) with Jordan James (fractured rib) sidelined indefinitely — Herbert is now the top healthy backup to Christian McCaffrey, worth a deep-league stash if McCaffrey ever needs a breather.
- **Fernando Mendoza had his roughest day of camp.** The Raiders' No. 1 overall pick was picked off in 7-on-7 and underthrew a target during an 11-on-11 session dominated by the Raiders defense — a reminder Kirk Cousins' hold on QB1 isn't in danger yet.
- **Dolphins offense had its best day of camp** in the first fan-attended practice, with backup QB Quinn Ewers hitting two 50+ yard shots — one a 70-yard TD to rookie WR Kevin Coleman Jr. Modest signal for Coleman's camp stock in a crowded slot competition.

## Injury Report

Nothing new since the midday report. Xavier Worthy (shoulder) and Ricky Pearsall (season-ending PCL surgery) remain the notable situations in Kansas City and San Francisco, respectively — no updates since this morning.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Raiders QB1**: Mendoza's rough Sunday doesn't change the picture — Cousins remains the starter to open camp.
- **49ers RB room**: Herbert signing gives McCaffrey a healthy backup while Jordan James (rib) and the room's rookie options work back; keep an eye on reps once pads come off IR-track players.
- **Dolphins WR/slot**: Kevin Coleman Jr. flashing speed with a long TD from Ewers — early camp buzz, not yet a depth-chart change.

## Rookie Watch

- Kevin Coleman Jr. (WR, MIA) — 70-yard TD catch from Quinn Ewers in Sunday's fan practice, building a name in a crowded Dolphins WR room.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**

- Khalil Herbert (RB, SF) — signed into a clear backup role behind McCaffrey.
