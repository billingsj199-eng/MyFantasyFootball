# Training Camp Report — August 2, 2026 (Midday)

_Slow overnight/morning stretch on the second day of "Back Together Weekend" — the headline is good injury news out of Kansas City, a confirmed season-ender in San Francisco, and two QB battles tilting._

## Top Storylines

- **Xavier Worthy's shoulder is believed OK.** After Saturday's cart-off scare, Rapoport and Schefter both report Worthy avoided a serious injury; the Chiefs are being cautious but he could be back on the field before KC's Aug. 15 preseason opener. Downgrade the panic from last night's report — this looks like a non-issue.
- **Ricky Pearsall's season is officially over.** 49ers GM John Lynch confirmed Pearsall will have PCL surgery (6-12 month recovery) and is headed to IR, closing the loop on the swelling story that's been building since July 29.
- **Deebo Samuel got his first practice reps back in red and gold** on a 10-play plan and immediately made plays — he's the clear fantasy beneficiary of the Pearsall injury in SF's receiver room.
- **Shedeur Sanders is winning the Browns' QB battle on the field.** He took first-team reps for the second time this camp and went 13/22 with 4 TDs and 1 INT, while Deshaun Watson has now thrown an interception in three straight practices.
- **Kyler Murray appears to be pulling ahead of J.J. McCarthy** in the Vikings' QB competition based on Saturday's practice, per NBC Sports — first real separation in a battle that had been a dead heat.
