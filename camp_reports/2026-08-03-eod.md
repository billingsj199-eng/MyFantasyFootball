# Training Camp Report — August 3, 2026 (End of Day)

_A relatively quiet padded-practice day league-wide. The headline is the Falcons' QB battle essentially resolving itself — Michael Penix Jr. says he's still ~4 weeks from full clearance on his third ACL recovery, all but locking in Tua Tagovailoa as Week 1 starter. Otherwise it was a minor-injury day: Mike Evans and rookie Kaelon Black both sat out for San Francisco, and Pittsburgh had its first cart-off of camp (a depth WR, no fantasy relevance)._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Penix Jr. (QB, ATL) says he's ~4 weeks from full medical clearance** on his third career ACL recon — meaning he likely can't push for the job before the season starts. This effectively locks in Tua Tagovailoa as Week 1 QB1, which is a stabilizing signal for Drake London and Kyle Pitts as camp winds toward the preseason opener.
- **Mike Evans (WR, SF) missed Monday's practice with a quad strain**, but Ian Rapoport called it minor. Worth a light watch given the 49ers' WR room is already down Ricky Pearsall (season-ending PCL surgery) for the year.
- **Rookie RB Kaelon Black (SF) also missed Monday with an adductor/groin strain.** He'd been stepping into the RB3 role behind Christian McCaffrey while Jordan James (rib) was out — this muddies an already crowded backfield picture, with a timeline anywhere from a week to a couple months depending on severity.
- **Steelers first-team WR Cole Burgess left practice on a cart** in Pittsburgh's first padded session — the first cart-off of camp league-wide. He's a deep camp body with minimal fantasy relevance, but worth flagging as a Steelers roster note.
