# Training Camp Report — August 3, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight/morning window as Back Together Weekend wraps up — no fresh injuries of consequence. The notable items are Arizona's first depth chart (a modest gut-check on Jeremiyah Love's redraft price), a surprise Saints WR retirement, and Roman Wilson expanding his role in Pittsburgh._

## Top Storylines

- **Cardinals release their first 2026 depth chart, and Tyler Allgeier is listed ahead of rookie Jeremiyah Love at RB1.** Allgeier has taken first-team snaps in every 11-on-11 period so far and led Love 10-4 in camp carries; Arizona and Allgeier both describe it as a 1A/1B setup, but it's a signal to temper Love's ADP slightly ahead of Thursday's Hall of Fame Game.
- **Saints WR Ja'Lynn Polk retired at age 24**, less than a week into camp, per team announcement Sunday. No fantasy relevance himself (career backup), but it thins New Orleans' already-shallow WR room behind Chris Olave/Rashid Shaheed.
- **Roman Wilson (WR, PIT) is expanding his role** — continuing to see significant first-team work and now also getting punt-return reps, a new wrinkle that adds a path to a roster-safe/special-teams floor on top of his receiving buzz.

## Injury Report

Nothing new to report since last night. Xavier Worthy (shoulder, KC) and Ricky Pearsall (PCL surgery, SF) remain the notable situations from the weekend — no updates today.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Cardinals RB room**: First depth chart has Allgeier over Love, but touches have been close (10-4) and both sides are calling it a competitive rotation, not a clear pecking order.
- **Steelers WR room**: Wilson stacking first-team reps and adding punt-return work — worth tracking as camp progresses into padded practices.

## Rookie Watch

- Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI) — RB2 on Arizona's first depth chart behind Allgeier; still expected to carve out a real role, but not the unquestioned lead back some redraft rankings assumed entering camp.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Roman Wilson (WR, PIT) — first-team reps plus a new punt-returner role.

**Fallers**
- Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI) — listed RB2 behind Allgeier on Arizona's first depth chart.
