# Training Camp Report — August 4, 2026 (End of Day)

_The afternoon/evening news cycle brought another blockbuster receiver extension — Zay Flowers locked up long-term in Baltimore — while Cleveland's QB battle took a sharper turn against Deshaun Watson after a sixth interception in four practices. Tampa Bay's locker-room tension escalated when Vita Vea requested a trade on top of the Baker Mayfield contract fallout. Elsewhere, a scary-but-brief injury for Browns rookie WR KC Concepcion resolved with good news, and Denver's Pat Bryant kept building on his rookie breakout._

## Top Storylines

- **Zay Flowers (WR, BAL) agrees to a 4-year, $140M extension ($108M guaranteed)** — ties Justin Jefferson as the NFL's 4th-highest-paid WR by APY and becomes Baltimore's No. 2 highest-paid player behind Lamar Jackson. Removes all long-term uncertainty around Lamar's top target.
- **Deshaun Watson (QB, CLE) threw his 6th interception in four camp practices**, including 3 on Saturday alone, as Cleveland's QB battle keeps trending toward Shedeur Sanders. Coach Todd Monken says the two will keep alternating first-team reps for now — bad optics for Watson as a Week 1 QB1 bet.
- **Vita Vea (DT, TB) has requested a trade** after contract talks hit an impasse, adding to a tense Bucs camp already dealing with Baker Mayfield's "disrespected" comments about his own stalled extension. Not a fantasy-relevant player himself, but underscores continued friction in Tampa's building.
- **KC Concepcion (WR, CLE), Cleveland's first-round rookie, avoided a serious injury** after appearing to hurt his right shoulder on a hard fall during Monday's two-minute drill — per Schultz, nothing serious and he's expected to be fine. Keeps his path to a big rookie target share intact.
- **Pat Bryant (WR, DEN) continues building on his strong rookie season**, drawing praise from Sean Payton ("There's a confidence about him, a maturity") and consistently flashing alongside Marvin Mims Jr. — a name to watch for a bigger Year 2 role in Denver's passing game.
- **Kyler Murray (QB, MIN) continues to hold the edge over J.J. McCarthy** in Minnesota's ongoing QB competition, with no clear announcement date yet from Kevin O'Connell.
