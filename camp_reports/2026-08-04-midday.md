# Training Camp Report — August 4, 2026 (Midday)

_The overnight/morning headline is a market-moving one: Bijan Robinson signed a three-year, $66.75M extension (up to $75M) with the Falcons, making him the highest-paid running back in NFL history by APY and ending his hold-in. That immediately turns up the heat on Detroit to get a Jahmyr Gibbs deal done — he's now missed four straight practices — and on the Colts with Jonathan Taylor. Elsewhere, a Jets rookie TE hit a health setback and a couple of second-year WRs (Carolina's Jimmy Horn Jr., Tennessee's Elic Ayomanor) are quietly building camp buzz._

## Top Storylines

- **Bijan Robinson (RB, ATL) signs a 3-year, $66.75M extension (up to $75M), $51M guaranteed** — the richest RB deal in NFL history by average annual value ($22.25M/yr, topping Saquon Barkley). Ends his hold-in and locks him in as Atlanta's long-term workhorse with zero market-share risk.
- **Jahmyr Gibbs (RB, DET) has now missed four straight practices in his own hold-in.** Dan Campbell says Robinson's deal "certainly not gonna hurt" Lions negotiations and downplayed an earlier report of a minor back issue — still reads as a business dispute, not a health or workload concern.
- **Kenyon Sadiq (TE, NYJ), the Jets' Day 2 rookie, hit a setback in his recovery from offseason (sports) hernia surgery.** Coach Aaron Glenn confirmed he'll miss time, with no firm timeline given yet — delays his path to early-camp reps.
- **Jimmy Horn Jr. (WR, CAR), a 2025 sixth-round pick, keeps turning heads** with his speed and route-running — deep-league dynasty buzz building behind Carolina's top WRs.
- **Elic Ayomanor (WR, TEN) is building real chemistry with Cam Ward**, winning 50-50 balls and deep in-breaking routes — still profiles as a WR4 in a crowded Titans room, but camp helium is real.
