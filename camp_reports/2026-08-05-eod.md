# Training Camp Report — August 5, 2026 (End of Day)

_Wednesday's practices delivered a real Browns QB-battle swing, a big recovery milestone for Rashee Rice, and a scattering of minor injury dings across Denver, Minnesota, and LA. George Pickens is now locked in on Dallas's franchise tag for 2026._

## Top Storylines

- **Deshaun Watson had his best practice of camp** — 7 TDs in a red-zone period — the same day **Shedeur Sanders threw his first interception of camp**. The Browns' QB1 job remains a genuine coin flip with Kevin Stefanski/Monken still splitting first-team reps.
- **Rashee Rice (WR, KC) took part in 11-on-11 team drills for the first time this camp**, catching a bubble screen and absorbing contact without issue. A real recovery milestone off his knee scope and suspension — trending toward Week 1 availability.
- **Jaylen Waddle (WR, DEN) exited practice early** with what Sean Payton called "muscle tightness," walking off under his own power. Payton says he's "expected to be fine," but worth a Thursday recheck.
- **Jordan Addison (WR, MIN) sat out team drills with a jammed thumb**; imaging came back clean and Kevin O'Connell doesn't expect a long absence.
- **Jalen Reagor (WR, LAC) missed practice with a finger injury** that Jim Harbaugh says could cost him more time, opening up first-team reps for rookies Tre Harris and KeAndre Lambert-Smith.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane (WR, BAL) keeps stacking highlight reps** — another red-zone TD grab, this one over Marlon Humphrey — continuing his push toward a real WR3/red-zone role as a third-round rookie.
- **George Pickens officially signed his $27.3M franchise tag** after Wednesday's 4pm ET deadline passed with no long-term deal. He's locked in as Dallas's WR1 alongside CeeDee Lamb for 2026.
