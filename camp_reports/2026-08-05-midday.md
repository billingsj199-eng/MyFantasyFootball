# Training Camp Report — August 5, 2026 (Midday)

_A quieter overnight/morning cycle after Monday's blockbuster extensions. The headline injury belongs to a Cowboys backup TE, not a fantasy name, but there's real skill-position movement: a hamstring scare for Eagles rookie Makai Lemon, a hopeful Week 1 timeline for Jets rookie TE Kenyon Sadiq, and Tank Dell finally back on a practice field in Houston. Stefon Diggs' free-agency market is heating up in Washington._

## Top Storylines

- **Tank Dell (WR, HOU) worked on the side during Wednesday's practice**, the next step in his long comeback from a multi-ligament (ACL/MCL/PCL/meniscus) knee injury that cost him all of 2025. No timetable yet for full-team reps, but the trajectory continues to look encouraging — a name to watch climb draft boards if he's full-go by the preseason opener.
- **Kenyon Sadiq (TE, NYJ) will miss time after a "little setback" in his hernia recovery**, but Aaron Glenn says he's "very confident" the rookie will be ready for the Week 1 opener at Tennessee (Sept. 13). Take the dip in current camp reps as noise, not a real threat to his Year 1 role.
- **Makai Lemon (WR, PHI) missed Tuesday's practice with a hamstring injury** alongside DeVonta Smith; the team calls it minor and day-to-day. A second hamstring flare this camp is a mild yellow flag on the rookie's push to win the Eagles' WR2 job over Dontayvion Wicks.
- **Stefon Diggs and the Commanders have "mutual interest,"** per ESPN's Jeremy Fowler, with Diggs (a Maryland native) intrigued by a homecoming — though neither side is rushing a deal. Worth tracking for both Diggs' 2026 outlook and Washington's WR2 picture behind Terry McLaurin.
- **Cowboys TE Princeton Fant is out for the season** with a torn ACL and partial MCL suffered in Tuesday's first padded practice. Minimal direct fantasy impact (special-teamer, not a target-share threat), but it's Dallas's first season-ending camp injury.
- **Aaron Rodgers plans to actually play in the Steelers' first two preseason games** (vs. Packers and Jets) under new coach Mike McCarthy, who wants him for 30-40 snaps — a real departure from the zero preseason snaps he took under Tomlin last year.
