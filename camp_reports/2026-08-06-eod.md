# Training Camp Report — August 6, 2026 (End of Day)

_Hall of Fame Game kicked off the preseason in Canton, with Carson Beck's efficient debut and Bam Knight's workhorse-lite outing (starting over an injured Trey Benson) the two biggest fantasy notes. Pittsburgh's first-round rookie tackle went down in a worrying practice injury, and Cincinnati's WR3 battle got a fresh face pushing to the front._

## Top Storylines

- **Carson Beck (QB, ARI) threw his first pro touchdown** — an 11/14, 148-yard, one-TD first half against Carolina, capped by a fade to Simi Fehoko — in his Hall of Fame Game start over Jacoby Brissett. Efficient, confident debut; still a dynasty stash only behind Brissett for 2026.
- **Bam Knight (RB, ARI) started and led the Cardinals' backfield** (56 yards on 11 carries, all 15 snaps on the opening drive) after Trey Benson was held out with knee discomfort. Reinforces Knight's case for the RB3/handcuff job — a deep-league watch behind Love/Allgeier/Conner.
- **Trey Benson (RB, ARI) was held out of the Hall of Fame Game with knee discomfort** that cropped up earlier in the week — no word yet on severity, but it directly opened the door for Knight's strong showing. Worth tracking before camp practices resume.
- **Steelers rookie 1st-round OT Max Iheanachor left Thursday's practice** with an apparent shoulder/neck injury after a team drill, headed to the locker room for evaluation. Not a skill player, but a prolonged absence would be a red flag for Pittsburgh's offensive line and, by extension, Aaron Rodgers' pass protection.
- **Bengals rookie WR Colbie Young (4th-round, Georgia) is pushing Andrei Iosivas for the WR3 job**, flashing size and contested-catch ability in red-zone reps. Iosivas' recurring drop issues (5 last year, 6 in 2024) have kept the door open — Young is a name to know in Cincinnati's deep passing game behind Chase/Higgins.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Cardinals backfield**: With Benson out and Jeremiyah Love/Tyler Allgeier resting, Bam Knight got every early-down look and looked the part — he remains behind Allgeier/Love/Conner on the depth chart but has clear coaching staff trust as the RB4/handcuff.
- **Bengals WR3**: Colbie Young's rise adds a real name behind Iosivas; worth tracking through the next two preseason games as Cincinnati sorts its third receiver spot.
- **Panthers**: UDFA Haynes King (not Kenny Pickett, who started) threw a TD to Ja'seem Reed in his NFL debut — depth-chart noise only, no fantasy relevance for Carolina's offense.

## Rookie Watch

- Carson Beck (QB, ARI) — sharp Hall of Fame Game debut (11/14, 148 yds, 1 TD in the first half); dynasty name to track, no 2026 fantasy value behind Brissett.
- Colbie Young (WR, CIN) — climbing the Bengals' WR3 depth chart on contested-catch ability; watch his preseason usage.
- Max Iheanachor (OT, PIT) — injury scare Thursday; not fantasy-relevant himself but affects Rodgers' offensive environment if it lingers.
