# Training Camp Report — August 6, 2026 (Midday)

_Contract season keeps rolling — Jonathan Taylor becomes the summer's third top-RB extension — while Jahmyr Gibbs ends his hold-in and returns to the field. Tampa Bay's rookie WR2 and Chicago's backup RB both trend back toward full health, and Carson Beck gets his first pro start tonight in Canton._

## Top Storylines

- **Jonathan Taylor agreed to a 2-year, $44M extension ($39M guaranteed) with the Colts** — the third top-tier RB deal this summer after Bijan Robinson and ahead of a still-unresolved Jahmyr Gibbs. Locks in Indy's workhorse role long-term with zero distraction risk.
- **Jahmyr Gibbs ended his hold-in and returned to practice** for the first time this camp, though he worked with a trainer on a side field after a minor back issue from conditioning tests limited his session. A clear sign extension talks are progressing — worth confirming full participation over the next few days.
- **Emeka Egbuka (WR, TB) was back at practice Thursday** after two days out with lower-body soreness (Bowles called it precautionary, alongside teammate Jalen McMillan). No lingering concern for Tampa Bay's rookie WR2.
- **Kyle Monangai (RB, CHI) is working back into camp reps** on a day-to-day basis after a soft-tissue injury that cost him his first few camp practices; coach Ben Johnson called it short-term.
- **Carson Beck starts tonight's Hall of Fame Game** for Arizona against Carolina — the first extended pro look at the Cardinals' 3rd-round rookie QB, with Jacoby Brissett held out. Panthers counter with Kenny Pickett as Bryce Young also sits. Jeremiyah Love will not play.
- **Falcons LB Jalon Walker tore his ACL in Tuesday's practice** and is out for the season — no direct fantasy impact but thins Atlanta's pass rush.
