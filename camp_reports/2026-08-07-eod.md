# Training Camp Report — August 7, 2026 (End of Day)

_Headline of the night is business, not bodies: Jahmyr Gibbs officially signed his record-breaking extension, and a Stefon Diggs signing that had slipped through the cracks earlier this week gets logged. Otherwise it's a quiet evening — Andy Reid easing off the gas with a healthy Mahomes, Denzel Boston's rookie push in Cleveland getting real GM commentary, and continued signs that James Conner's camp reps are being managed carefully in Arizona._

## Top Storylines

- **Jahmyr Gibbs (RB, DET) officially signed his three-year extension** (up to $75.75M, $51.5M guaranteed) Friday, days after the deal was agreed — it makes him the NFL's highest-paid running back, edging out Bijan Robinson. With David Montgomery traded to Houston this offseason, Gibbs is now a fully-locked-in, no-committee-risk RB1 for years to come.
- **Catching up: Stefon Diggs signed a one-year, $12M deal with the Commanders** earlier this week (missed in prior scans). He slots in as an immediate WR2 behind Terry McLaurin with real weekly target volume in Kliff Kingsbury's offense — a legitimate mid-round fantasy pick, with a minor target-competition drag on McLaurin.
- **Browns GM Andrew Berry publicly called Jerry Jeudy the team's "bell cow receiver,"** but also praised rookies Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion's camps as "excellent" — the front office is backing Jeudy on paper while a real three-man rotation builds behind him. Keeps Boston/Concepcion as strong best-ball/dynasty stashes even without unseating Jeudy yet.
- **Andy Reid said there's a "pretty good chance" Patrick Mahomes (QB, KC) sits out the Chiefs' entire preseason slate**, purely as a precaution off his December ACL/LCL tear — he's been full-go in padded practice since Day 1 of camp. Zero Week 1 concern, just don't expect exhibition reps.
- **James Conner (RB, ARI) remains out of 11-on-11/team drills** in what's being managed as a cautious veteran ramp-up, not a new injury. Keeps the door open for Jeremiyah Love to keep climbing past his "RB2 on paper" designation on Arizona's first depth chart.
