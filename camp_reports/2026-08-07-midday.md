# Training Camp Report — August 7, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news is mostly minor-injury bookkeeping in the wake of Thursday's Hall of Fame Game: a scare on Ja'Tavion Sanders' surgically-repaired ankle, a new knee issue for Jalen McMillan, and Josh Jacobs missing a Packers practice. On the positive side, Chris Rodriguez Jr. and rookie Malik Benson are both building real camp-battle momentum in Jacksonville and Las Vegas._

## Top Storylines

- **Ja'Tavion Sanders (TE, CAR) tweaked the same ankle he had surgically repaired last December**, getting hurt Tuesday, tested, and given the OK to still play every snap of Carolina's opening drive in Thursday's Hall of Fame Game. Not considered serious by the team, but worth a re-aggravation watch on a player already fighting for TE1 reps behind a shaky camp.
- **Chris Rodriguez Jr. (RB, JAX) had his busiest practice of camp Thursday**, with every carry going for positive yardage as he pushes for the short-yardage/goal-line role Jacksonville brought him in for (Travis Etienne struggled there in 2025). Still ramping up off offseason foot surgery, but trending toward real Week 1 touches in a complementary role.
- **Rookie Malik Benson (WR, LV) is emerging as the favorite for Raiders' WR3 job**, outplaying both Jack Bech and Dont'e Thornton Jr. in a rotation none of them had separated from. Coach Klint Kubiak: "He's got a good head start, playing good football." Deep-league/dynasty stash behind Tre Tucker and Jalen Nailor.
- **Jalen McMillan (WR, TB) has been sidelined all week with a knee injury** and has no set return timeline per Todd Bowles, though the team isn't voicing serious concern. Modest near-term bump for Emeka Egbuka and Chris Godwin if it lingers.
- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB) sat out Thursday's practice with a groin injury**, but it's not expected to be a long-term absence — a watch item, not yet a red flag.
- **Colts brass (Steichen, Ballard) still have no clarity on when Alec Pierce (WR, IND) returns** from the PUP list after offseason ankle surgery. Week 1 remains the stated goal, but the lack of a firm date is a mild yellow flag for Indy's WR1.
- **Vikings HC Kevin O'Connell is reportedly targeting Aug. 14 to name a starter** between Kyler Murray and J.J. McCarthy, with reps still being split evenly. Insiders still lean Murray as the favorite, but don't price in either QB until it's official.
