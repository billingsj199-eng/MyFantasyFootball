# Training Camp Report — August 8, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quieter Saturday after a heavy overnight injury cycle: Jaylen Waddle worked to the side and shed his leg sleeve, the clearest sign yet he's nearing a return in Denver, while Texans S C.J. Gardner-Johnson's scary Thursday cart-off resolved with an intact ACL. Bryce Young keeps building real camp momentum in Carolina, and two Day 2/Day 3 rookies — Jonah Coleman (DEN) and Bam Knight (ARI) — flashed enough to nudge into deeper-league conversations._

## Top Storylines

- **Jaylen Waddle (DEN) worked to the side of Saturday's practice and shed his leg sleeve**, the clearest sign yet he's tracking toward the 4-5 day return window Sean Payton laid out for his leg strain — still Denver's clear new WR1 once fully back.
- **Texans S C.J. Gardner-Johnson's Thursday cart-off scare resolved with an intact ACL**, and he could be ready as soon as Week 1 — not a fantasy-relevant position, but a real breather for Houston's defense/IDP formats.
- **Bryce Young (CAR) is building genuine camp momentum**, with coach Dave Canales saying he's "taken full command" of the offense and praising his timing with Tetairoa McMillan and Jalen Coker — a better Young raises the passing-game ceiling for both.
- **Rookie RB Jonah Coleman (DEN) earned notable praise from Sean Payton** ("you don't feel a rookie with Jonah"), pushing for early-down/short-yardage work behind J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey — worth a deep-league stash watch in a crowded backfield.
- **Cardinals rookie RB Bam Knight (12 carries, 60 yards, incl. a 15-yard breakaway) flashed in the Hall of Fame Game** with starters resting — he sits behind Conner/Allgeier/Benson on the depth chart but strengthens his case as the handcuff to own.
- **Steelers 1st-round rookie OT Max Iheanachor sat out Friday and Saturday practices** with a minor upper-body issue; coach Mike McCarthy expects him back Monday with no real disruption — an O-line note more than a skill-position one, but worth tracking for Pittsburgh's run-blocking outlook.
- **Vita Vea (TB) and Tuli Tuipulotu (LAC) remain the last unresolved veteran contract "hold-ins" league-wide**, still limiting practice reps as of Friday — a slow-developing situation, low direct fantasy relevance.
