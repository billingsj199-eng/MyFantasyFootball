# Training Camp Report — August 8, 2026 (Midday)

_A grinding, injury-heavy overnight stretch: Broncos WR Marvin Mims Jr. left Friday's practice with a leg injury and was flown home for evaluation, Jets RB Isaiah Davis will miss a couple weeks with a knee issue, and Detroit's Miles Kitselman is done for the year. On the brighter side, Seahawks rookie Jadarian Price and Dolphins rookie Caleb Douglas both continue to look like real Week 1 roles are forming, and Malik Nabers had his best rehab practice of the summer._

## Top Storylines

- **Broncos WR Marvin Mims Jr. left Friday's practice with a leg/soft-tissue injury** and was flown back to Denver for evaluation; severity is still unclear, and the team called in a workout WR (Kyrese Rowan) as a precaution. Worth a firm re-check before locking in his Week 1 role.
- **Jets RB Isaiah Davis (knee) is expected out "a couple weeks"** per Aaron Glenn, but the team is confident he's ready for the regular season — low fantasy relevance himself, but a note on the Jets' RB3 competition.
- **Seahawks rookie Jadarian Price continues to look like the clear lead back**, with coaches using him all over the offense as a receiver too — with Kenneth Walker III gone to Kansas City and Zach Charbonnet's ACL timeline still uncertain, Price's path to a true Week 1 RB1 role in Seattle keeps solidifying.
- **Dolphins rookie WR Caleb Douglas is drawing "best receiver on the team" praise** through the first several padded practices, including a one-handed TD grab — with Tyreek Hill and Jaylen Waddle both gone, he has a genuinely open runway to real target share.
- **Malik Nabers (NYG, recovering from ACL/meniscus) had his best-looking practice of the summer Friday**, cutting hard off the surgically repaired knee in individual work — no target return date yet, but the trajectory keeps trending toward Week 1 availability.
- **Lions placed TE Miles Kitselman on injured reserve with a significant leg injury** (out for the season) and re-signed WR Tarik Black in the corresponding move — minimal fantasy impact either way, but thins Detroit's TE depth behind Sam LaPorta.
- **Browns OC Todd Monken laid out the preseason plan for the Watson/Sanders QB battle**: each gets a start in the first two preseason games, with a decision not expected until after the Aug. 22 game vs. Buffalo — don't price in either QB's Week 1 outlook yet.
