# Training Camp Report — August 9, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quieter Sunday evening after the Tunsil bombshell from earlier: Panthers rookie QB Haynes King's strong Hall of Fame Game debut was interrupted by a hamstring injury, the Vikings' night practice failed to settle the Murray-McCarthy battle (if anything, McCarthy had the better night), and a couple of camp-battle WRs — Savion Williams and Isaac TeSlaa — made noise in different ways._

## Top Storylines

- **Panthers rookie QB Haynes King hurt his hamstring on the game-winning TD run** that capped his strong Hall of Fame Game debut (21/34, 180 yds, 2 TD); he's considered week-to-week, and Carolina signed veteran Kyle Trask for QB depth behind Bryce Young/Kenny Pickett.
- **Vikings night practice at TCO Stadium didn't settle the QB race** — Kevin O'Connell still isn't ready to name a starter after Murray and McCarthy have now split roughly 300 reps; in tonight's red-zone 7-on-7, McCarthy actually out-TD'd Murray 3-for-2, tempering this morning's "heavy favorite" framing for Murray.
- **Packers WR Savion Williams erupted for two touchdowns**, including a 60-yard bomb off a coverage bust from Kyle McCord, as Green Bay's banged-up WR room opens a bigger role for him.
- **Lions rookie WR Isaac TeSlaa scrapped with safety Chuck Clark** in a post-whistle shoving match — a sign he's playing physical and competing hard in Detroit's wide-open WR3 battle.
- **Jaguars WR Brian Thomas Jr. is building real chemistry with Trevor Lawrence** through Day 10, a notable reversal after camp reports in late July flagged the two as not fully connecting yet.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Vikings QB battle**: Still unresolved after the night practice; O'Connell continues to decline naming a starter even as reps stay split near 50/50.
- **Packers WR corps**: With other receivers banged up, Williams (2025 3rd-rounder) is seeing a real snap surge and cashing in on it.
- **Lions WR3 job**: Remains wide open; TeSlaa's physicality (including the Clark scuffle) is part of his case for the role.
- **Jaguars passing game**: Lawrence-Thomas chemistry trending up entering the stretch run of camp.

## Rookie Watch

- Haynes King (QB, CAR) — Flashed real rushing/game-winning upside in the Hall of Fame Game before the hamstring injury interrupted his momentum.
- Isaac TeSlaa (WR, DET) — Competing physically for Detroit's open WR3 spot.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Savion Williams (WR, GB) — Two-TD day, including a long bomb, as he steps into a bigger role with others banged up.
- Brian Thomas Jr. (WR, JAX) — Chemistry with Lawrence is visibly improving after a slow start to camp.
- J.J. McCarthy (QB, MIN) — Won tonight's red-zone efficiency battle with Murray, keeping the Vikings' QB1 race genuinely open.

**Fallers**
- Kyler Murray (QB, MIN) — This morning's "heavy favorite" framing looks less certain after McCarthy's better night practice.
- Haynes King (QB, CAR) — Hamstring injury halts a promising camp debut just as he was gaining buzz.
