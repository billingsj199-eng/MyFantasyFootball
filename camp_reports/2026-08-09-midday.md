# Training Camp Report — August 9, 2026 (Midday)

_Saturday's biggest fallout: Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil tore his triceps and will miss most of the season, a real hit to Jayden Daniels' pass protection. Elsewhere it was a quieter overnight/morning stretch — Kyler Murray's grip on the Vikings' starting job tightened further, and Titans rookie WR Carnell Tate flashed with a 65-yard bomb from Cam Ward._

## Top Storylines

- **Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil tore his left triceps in Saturday's practice** and is expected to need surgery and miss most of the 2026 season — a major blind-side downgrade for Jayden Daniels, who was built up around elite left-tackle protection; Andrew Wylie is the likely next man up.
- **Kyler Murray's hold on the Vikings' QB1 job is tightening** — he's now taking roughly two-thirds of first-team reps and is described as the "heavy favorite" to start Week 1, with J.J. McCarthy stuck running the second unit.
- **Titans rookie WR Carnell Tate (4th overall) hauled in a ~65-yard bomb from Cam Ward** for a touchdown that made social media rounds, adding to a fast start that has him drawing Offensive Rookie of the Year buzz.
- **Vita Vea's hold-in standoff drags on** — Bucs GM Jason Licht says there are "no plans" to trade him, but Vea's own agent told the Tampa Bay Times he doesn't see it ending in anything but a trade; Vea is still expected to travel with the team Monday for joint practices with the Jets.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Vikings QB battle**: Murray's rep share and consistency have him firmly ahead of McCarthy with camp entering its back half; barring a reversal, expect an announcement well before Week 1.
- **Commanders O-line**: Andrew Wylie projects to slide in at left tackle for Tunsil, with Josh Conerly possibly moving to the right side — worth monitoring for early-season pass-protection efficiency around Daniels.

## Rookie Watch

- Carnell Tate (WR, TEN) — 4th overall pick building real chemistry with Cam Ward, including a highlight-reel deep TD; early Offensive Rookie of the Year chatter.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Kyler Murray (QB, MIN) — Widening rep advantage over McCarthy strengthens his Week 1 outlook.
- Carnell Tate (WR, TEN) — Rookie big-play flash with Cam Ward raises his early-season target-share ceiling.

**Fallers**
- Jayden Daniels (QB, WAS) — Not injured himself, but loses his blindside protector for most of the season, a real (if secondary) drag on his floor.
