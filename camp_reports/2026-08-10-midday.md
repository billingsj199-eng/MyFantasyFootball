# Training Camp Report — August 10, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet overnight/morning stretch after Sunday's action. The headline is the confirmation that Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil needs triceps surgery and will miss a significant chunk of the season — the biggest fantasy-adjacent ripple is added injury/rhythm risk for Jayden Daniels. Bears WR Luther Burden III is out with a groin injury, Patriots TE Hunter Henry got paid, and Lions backup QB Teddy Bridgewater retired again._

## Top Storylines

- **Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil is having surgery on a torn triceps** and will miss a significant portion of the season — PFF's 3rd-best pass-blocking tackle last year is a real loss; expect more pressure on Jayden Daniels behind Brandon Coleman, a QB who already struggled to stay healthy.
- **Bears WR Luther Burden III is out with a groin injury** suffered in Saturday's practice, expected to miss about a month but targeted to be back for the Week 1 opener vs. Carolina — a real dent in an already-thin Chicago WR room in the meantime.
- **Patriots TE Hunter Henry signed a 2-year extension** (up to $20M, $14.5M gtd) — locks in his role as Drake Maye's top-of-the-seam target through 2028, no distraction risk.
- **Lions QB Teddy Bridgewater retired (again)** mid-camp, ending his bid to be Jared Goff's backup; Detroit quickly signed Josh Dobbs to fill the QB2 role. Negligible direct fantasy value, but worth knowing who's behind Goff.
- **Vikings QB race still unresolved** — Kyler Murray remains the reported "heavy favorite" (roughly 2/3 of first-team reps) but Kevin O'Connell is still targeting Aug. 14 to name a starter; no real change from last night's split-reps update.
- **Rams RB Blake Corum continued to look agile and explosive** through the final open practice of Rams camp, a small but notable note behind Kyren Williams as joint practices with the Cowboys begin this week.
