# Training Camp Report — August 11, 2026 (End of Day)

_Biggest story of the day: the Vikings finally ended their QB competition, naming Kyler Murray the Week 1 starter over J.J. McCarthy. Elsewhere, Josh Jacobs' groin injury got worse news (out "at least this week"), Rico Dowdle is pulling ahead of Jaylen Warren in Pittsburgh, and Puka Nacua left the Rams-Cowboys joint practice with what's being described as minor groin soreness._

## Top Storylines

- **Kyler Murray named Vikings' Week 1 starting QB over J.J. McCarthy.** Kevin O'Connell made the call Tuesday after Murray edged McCarthy in a competition that had gone back and forth all camp. Murray locks in as a low-end QB1/streaming option in Minnesota's offense with Justin Jefferson; McCarthy is the backup and a dynasty stash only.
- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB) will miss "at least this week"** with his groin injury, per Matt LaFleur — an escalation from last week's "not expected to be long-term" framing. MarShawn Lloyd is stepping into first-team preseason reps in his place, making Lloyd a real short-term watch and a name to know as a Jacobs handcuff.
- **Rico Dowdle looks like the Steelers' clear early-down RB1** over Jaylen Warren after four practices of taking the bulk of first-team reps, though Pittsburgh is still expected to run a real committee. Slight lean toward Dowdle in early fantasy drafts, but Warren retains standalone value.
- **Puka Nacua (WR, LAR) exited the Rams-Cowboys joint practice early** Tuesday; McVay initially said he wasn't sure of the injury, but Ian Rapoport later reported it's minor groin soreness with no real concern. Worth a glance before Thursday's session, but not treated as a big deal by the team.
- **Denzel Boston (WR, CLE) is drawing "clear new No. 1 receiver" buzz** in fresh camp coverage, even as GM Andrew Berry still publicly backs Jerry Jeudy as the "bell cow." The gap between the official pecking order and the on-field buzz keeps widening — Boston is a strong best-ball/dynasty stash regardless of Week 1 role.
- **Malik Benson (WR, LV) is still holding a top-3 WR spot** through six Raiders practices, with the coaching staff openly calling the 6th-round pick "a steal." Deep-league/dynasty relevance behind Tre Tucker and Jalen Nailor continues to build.
- **Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI) reportedly looked faster and more explosive than Trey Benson** in a head-to-head look at Cardinals camp — Tyler Allgeier is still atop the RB depth chart, but Love keeps chipping away in a crowded room.
