# Training Camp Report — August 11, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news was dominated by two significant Monday injuries that didn't hit a skill-position player directly but will shake up two offenses: Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil (torn triceps, likely out most of the season) and Lions C Cade Mays (fractured wrist, out 8-10 weeks). On the skill-position side, Lions RB Isiah Pacheco was diagnosed with an MCL sprain but is expected back for Week 1._

## Top Storylines

- **Isiah Pacheco (RB, DET) has an MCL sprain** — missed four straight practices before Dan Campbell confirmed the diagnosis Monday. Expected to be ready for Week 1, but he opens the season as Jahmyr Gibbs' backup, so this is a deep-league/handcuff note more than anything.
- **Commanders LT Laremy Tunsil tore his triceps** in Saturday one-on-ones and will need surgery, likely missing most/all of 2026. Not a skill-position player, but a real downgrade for Jayden Daniels' blind-side protection and the Washington passing game's floor.
- **Lions C Cade Mays fractured a small bone in his wrist** (8-10 week timeline) — Campbell called it "significant." Another offensive-line hit for Detroit, though it doesn't directly touch skill-position fantasy value.
- **Chase Brown (RB, CIN) has an expanded, multi-formation role** in Zac Taylor's offense — lining up in the slot, running routes, and getting jet-sweep touches in addition to standard backfield work. Bengals are using him more like a true every-down weapon, which raises his receiving-work ceiling for 2026.
- **Falcons QB battle is effectively settled for now** — Ian Rapoport reiterated Michael Penix Jr. still isn't fully cleared from his ACL recovery, and Tua Tagovailoa is on track to be the Week 1 starter unless something changes. No new fantasy action needed here, but it's the clearest signal yet.
- **Ricky Pearsall (WR, SF) remains out for the season** (PCL surgery) — no new development, still fully closes the book on his 2026; Deebo Samuel and Jauan Jennings remain the beneficiaries.
