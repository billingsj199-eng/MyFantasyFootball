# Training Camp Report — August 12, 2026 (End of Day)

_Quiet news day overall, but two QB battles got their first concrete signals: Cleveland set its preseason-opener starter (Watson) and Atlanta named its Week 1 hopeful (Tua) for Thursday's/Friday's exhibition openers. Neither decides a regular-season job yet._

## Top Storylines

- **Deshaun Watson will start the Browns' preseason opener Saturday vs. Bears; Shedeur Sanders gets the Week 2 start (Aug. 22 vs. Bills).** First real game action for Watson since 2024. Monken frames it as still a genuine, ongoing competition — don't lock in either QB's Week 1 price yet, but Watson getting first crack is a small signal in his favor.
- **Tua Tagovailoa named Falcons' starter for their preseason opener vs. Broncos.** Stefanski was explicit this is a preseason-only decision, not a Week 1 declaration. Michael Penix Jr. still isn't cleared for full 11-on-11 work as he continues ACL rehab — health, not merit, is driving the pecking order right now.
- **Bengals plan to play their starters (Burrow, Chase, Higgins, Dexter Lawrence) in limited series Thursday vs. Lions**, while Detroit is expected to sit most of its own starters (Jared Goff out). Worth a look if you want an early read on Cincinnati's offense, but don't expect much from Detroit's side.
- **DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. (Steelers) remain out of practice** and are now considered very unlikely to dress for Thursday's opener vs. Green Bay — no change from this morning's outlook, just firmer confirmation.
- **Emeka Egbuka (Bucs WR) left Wednesday's joint practice with the Jets with a minor toe injury.** Not considered serious, and Tampa's starters were expected to see limited-to-no snaps in the preseason opener regardless.
- **Malik Nabers (Giants WR) remains limited to individual drills**, but John Harbaugh said he could mix into team drills as soon as next week — incremental progress in his ACL recovery, still trending toward Week 1 availability.
