# Training Camp Report — August 12, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight/morning window — no bombshells, but Pittsburgh's top two receivers and Green Bay's top two receivers are both dealing with camp bumps and bruises heading into Thursday's preseason openers._

## Top Storylines

- **DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. (Steelers WRs) both missed Tuesday's practice.** Metcalf is out with an undisclosed injury (possibly from a diving catch Saturday); Pittman has now missed back-to-back practices with a right leg injury from Saturday Night Lights. Neither is considered serious, but there's a real chance both sit out Thursday's preseason opener vs. Green Bay — worth a glance before locking in Week 1 outlooks.
- **Matthew Golden and Jayden Reed (Packers WRs) are also both banged up.** Golden (toe) missed multiple practices but returned Tuesday running with the first team — a good sign. Reed (ankle) is day-to-day and could sit Thursday as a precaution but remains on track for Week 1 vs. Minnesota.
- **No material change on the two ongoing injury watches from yesterday:** Josh Jacobs (groin) is confirmed out for Thursday's opener but worked on a side field Tuesday and isn't viewed as a regular-season concern; Puka Nacua's groin soreness is still described as minor with no new downgrade.
