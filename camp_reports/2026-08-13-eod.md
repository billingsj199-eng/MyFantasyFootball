# Training Camp Report — August 13, 2026 (End of Day)

_Six preseason openers tonight (Bengals-Lions, Steelers-Packers, Colts-Patriots, Texans-Chargers, Raiders-Cardinals, Titans-49ers) headline the first real live-game data of camp. Biggest fantasy signal: Brock Purdy was sharp and George Kittle/Jauan Jennings look locked into expanded roles with Ricky Pearsall out for the year._

## Top Storylines

- **Brock Purdy went 23/30 for 295 yards and 3 TDs (140.3 rating) plus 44 rush yards** in San Francisco's 37-24 win over Tennessee — an efficient, extended look that's a good sign for a 49ers passing offense that's had to reshuffle its WR room around Pearsall's injury.
- **George Kittle (8 rec, 88 yards, TD) and Jauan Jennings (2 TD, 3 rec, 37 yards) both had big nights** for SF — early confirmation both are stepping into larger roles with Pearsall out for the season.
- **Christian McCaffrey played deep into the game and carried 22 times for 73 yards** plus a TD and a catch — an unusually heavy preseason workload for a starter, a good health/workload signal after his injury-plagued 2024.
- **Cam Ward (170 yards, 2 TD passing) connected with rookie TE Gunnar Helm for a 34-yard score** in his first extended look of Year 2 — an early positive for Ward's sophomore-season chemistry with the Titans' new pass-catchers.
- **Anthony Richardson went 11/14 for 145 yards with a rushing TD (53 yards on 6 carries)** in his half of action vs. New England before Riley Leonard closed it out; Richardson also lost a fumble and was on the wrong end of a deflected INT. Strong per-play efficiency continues to make the QB1 case even with Daniel Jones sidelined all preseason.
- **Michael Mayer caught a 13-yard TD from Kirk Cousins** on the Raiders' first-team opening drive vs. Arizona — an early rapport-building rep worth tracking for his TE1 outlook in Las Vegas.
- **Rookie WR Malik Benson (Raiders) flashed speed and route-running**, including a 19-yard connection with backup QB Fernando Mendoza — deep-league/dynasty stash to monitor.
- **Bengals starters (Burrow, Chase, Higgins, Chase Brown) all played limited, clean snaps** in a 30-14 win over Detroit — nothing new, but confirms full health heading into Week 2 of preseason.
- **Steelers led Green Bay in the fourth quarter of a still-developing game**; Aaron Rodgers and Jordan Love both rested/played minimally, with Pittsburgh's backup QB battle (Mason Rudolph, Will Howard, rookie Drew Allar) getting extended run — final score wasn't available as of this report.

## Injury Report

No new fantasy-relevant injuries surfaced from tonight's openers. (Ricky Pearsall's season-ending PCL surgery and Luther Burden III's groin injury were already covered in prior reports — no update tonight.)

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **49ers**: Kittle and Jennings both looked like clear beneficiaries of the Pearsall absence in Purdy's extended preseason drives; McCaffrey's 22-carry workload suggests the staff isn't managing his snaps cautiously.
- **Titans**: Ward building an early rapport with rookie TE Gunnar Helm — a name to track in TE-needy leagues if the role sticks into the regular season.
- **Colts**: Richardson (1st half) and Leonard (2nd half) splitting preseason reps per the plan laid out in the midday report; Daniel Jones did not play.
- **Raiders**: Cousins and the first-team offense drove for a Mayer TD before giving way to Mendoza, who found rookie Malik Benson downfield.
- **Steelers**: Rodgers and Love both sat out or played minimally; Rudolph, Howard, and rookie Allar all getting extended preseason QB reps as the backup battle plays out.

## Rookie Watch

- **Gunnar Helm (TE, TEN)**: 34-yard TD catch from Cam Ward — early camp-to-game chemistry worth monitoring in dynasty/TE-premium formats.
- **Malik Benson (WR, LV)**: Flashed speed/physicality, including a 19-yard grab from Mendoza — deep-league stash.
- **Drew Allar (QB, PIT)**: Extended preseason run behind Rudolph/Howard in the Steelers' backup QB competition.
