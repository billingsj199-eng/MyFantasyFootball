# Training Camp Report — August 13, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight cycle news-wise, but a loaded slate of preseason openers tonight (Steelers-Packers, Bengals-Lions, Colts-Patriots, Raiders-Cardinals, Titans-49ers) is generating fresh starter/sit plans worth knowing before kickoff. Biggest item: real backup-QB run for Anthony Richardson in Indy._

## Top Storylines

- **Anthony Richardson named Colts' starter for tonight's opener vs. Patriots and will play the entire first half**, with Riley Leonard taking the second; Daniel Jones (recovering from Achilles) won't play any preseason snaps. Extended look at Richardson's case to reclaim/hold a role behind Jones — worth a deep-league watch if Jones' recovery drags.
- **Steelers' updated depth chart has Roman Wilson at starting slot WR** with DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. both out for tonight's opener vs. Green Bay (Germie Bernard/Ben Skowronek project as the outside options). Wilson's continued first-team reps and punt-return role keep building a deep-league/best-ball case.
- **Denzel Boston (Browns rookie WR) has been promoted to first-team reps**, working alongside Jerry Jeudy and KC Concepcion, and has flashed with a back-shoulder TD grab from Deshaun Watson in 11-on-11 work this week. Real signal his camp buzz is translating into snaps, not just noise.
- **J.J. McCarthy trade rumors (Jets, Panthers, Browns speculated) are circulating** after he officially lost the Vikings' QB1 job to Kyler Murray earlier this week. McCarthy says he wants to stay in Minnesota, but a deal before roster cutdowns would reset his fantasy outlook with a new offense.
- **Jeremiyah Love (Cardinals rookie RB) is trading first-team series with Tyler Allgeier and flashing as a receiver**, but OC Nathaniel Hackett is publicly downplaying the hype, still pointing to James Conner as the incumbent starter. Don't overpay in redraft — the RB room stays muddled.
- **Jared Wiley (Chiefs TE) strained his hamstring Wednesday but was already back at practice Thursday** — no lasting concern for the depth piece competing for scraps behind Travis Kelce.
