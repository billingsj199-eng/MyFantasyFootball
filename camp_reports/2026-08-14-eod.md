# Training Camp Report — August 14, 2026 (End of Day)

_Friday's three preseason openers (Dolphins-Commanders, Broncos-Falcons, Jets-Buccaneers) headline the evening, led by a rough Falcons debut for Tua Tagovailoa and a Commanders QB injury scare._

## Top Storylines

- **Tua Tagovailoa's Falcons debut was ugly**: 3-of-5 for 22 yards (70.4 rating), no TD/INT, and he fumbled a shotgun snap in Atlanta's 17-0 loss to Denver — the QB1 job he inherited after Miami moved on looks shakier than expected out of the gate.
- **Marcus Mariota (QB, WAS) exited in the first quarter** with an apparent right knee injury after being hit following a completion to Dyami Brown; Sam Hartman took over as Washington fell 16-13 to Miami. Watch his status before drawing fantasy-relevant conclusions about Washington's backup QB picture.
- **Malik Willis (QB, MIA) got real run as Miami's new QB1**, going 4-of-5 for 43 yards and rushing twice for 9 yards on the offense's only drive in the 16-13 win — a clean, if brief, first look replacing Tua in Miami.
- **Rookie Cade Klubnik (QB, NYJ) impressed in his NFL debut**, going 5-of-7 for 56 yards and leading the Jets to a 10-0 lead (capped by a Braelon Allen 31-yard TD run) after Geno Smith sat with sore-foot soreness — a real dynasty stash if he keeps climbing the Jets' QB2 depth chart.
- **Braelon Allen (RB, NYJ) broke a 31-yard TD run** in the first quarter — a nice early flash for a back who needs to hold off the rest of the Jets' backfield in camp.
- **Chase Bisontis (rookie OG, ARI) has a confirmed torn MCL** requiring surgery and is "almost certain" headed to IR — not a skill player, but a weakened Cardinals O-line is worth keeping in mind for Jeremiyah Love's workload efficiency.
- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB) could return to practice as soon as late next week**, per Matt LaFleur — an upgrade from Thursday's open-ended "out at least this week" framing on his groin injury.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Falcons**: Tua's rough debut (fumbled snap, 70.4 rating) puts a spotlight on Atlanta's QB room heading into the rest of preseason; Michael Penix Jr. remains out recovering from his ACL and wasn't available as a fallback tonight.
- **Dolphins**: Malik Willis played the Dolphins' lone starters' drive rather than sitting entirely — Miami wants developmental reps for their new QB1 over resting him.
- **Jets**: Klubnik's strong start (leading a 10-0 lead) puts pressure on Bailey Zappe and Brady Cook in the Jets' backup QB competition; Garrett Wilson caught a 21-yard pass from Klubnik on the opening drive.
- **Commanders**: Washington rested the bulk of its starters (including Jayden Daniels) and lost Mariota to injury, leaving Sam Hartman as the QB left standing.
