# Training Camp Report — August 14, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning wrap of Wednesday night's preseason openers — the Chargers-Texans and Steelers-Packers games weren't covered in last night's report, plus a positive Puka Nacua update and continued camp-battle movement for a few rookies._

## Top Storylines

- **Amar Johnson (RB, LAC) ran for 2 TDs and added a 55-yard catch** in the Chargers' 27-7 win over Houston — explosive preseason debut worth a very late dynasty/best-ball flier behind the Chargers' starter.
- **Rookie TE Marlin Klein (HOU) caught a 31-yard pass from Davis Mills** on the Texans' opening drive before giving way after one series — an early rapport note in a Houston TE room without a locked-in starter.
- **Drew Allar (QB, PIT) was outstanding in his preseason debut**: 10/13 for 153 yards and 3 total TDs (2 pass, 1 rush) for a 154.8 rating in Pittsburgh's 28-9 win over Green Bay — the clearest early signal yet of Pittsburgh's post-Rodgers QB succession plan.
- **Germie Bernard (WR, PIT) caught 4 of 5 targets for 51 yards**, working with the first-team offense in place of injured DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. — real YAC ability on display in his NFL debut.
- **Jeremiyah Love's (RB, ARI) preseason debut** showed a clear workload edge: 11 carries for 58 yards and 3 catches for 14 yards on 24 of 30 snaps, compared to just 6 snaps for Tyler Allgeier before Love exited — treat him as the lead back, not a committee piece.
- **Colbie Young (WR, CIN) continued working into first-team reps** opposite Chase/Higgins in Cincinnati's preseason opener, passing Mitchell Tinsley in the slot competition — trending toward locking down the Bengals' WR3 job.
- **Puka Nacua (WR, LAR) practiced fully Thursday**, and Sean McVay said he'll be "ready to roll" for Week 1 — the groin/psoas soreness that pulled him from Tuesday's joint practice with Dallas is no longer a fantasy concern.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Bengals**: Colbie Young keeps stacking first-team reps and answering targets from Burrow, further separating from Mitchell Tinsley in the slot/WR3 battle.
- **Steelers**: With Metcalf and Pittman both dinged up, Bernard got extended run with the 1s; Allar's dominant relief outing behind Rudolph/Howard adds a new layer to the QB depth-chart storyline this camp.
- **Texans**: Rookie TE Marlin Klein and rookie C Keylan Rutledge both got early first-drive run with Davis Mills, an indication the staff trusts both in early-season roles.
- **Chargers**: Backups (Trey Lance at QB, Amar Johnson at RB) dominated snap share in the opener as LA rested most starters — normal preseason-opener usage, nothing alarming for the starters' outlook.
