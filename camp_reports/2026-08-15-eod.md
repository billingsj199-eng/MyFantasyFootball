# Training Camp Report — August 15, 2026 (End of Day)

_All seven 2026 Preseason Week 1 games are in the books (or nearly): Bills 29, Panthers 14; Rams 20, Chiefs 12; Browns 10, Bears 34; Vikings 13, Giants 10; Jaguars 24, Saints 20; Ravens 24, Eagles 7; Cowboys-Seahawks still finishing up late in Seattle. Busy night for backups, rookies, and QB battles._

## Top Storylines

- **Ty Simpson (QB, LAR) turned in the debut of the night**: 21-of-25 for 190 yards and 2 TDs (both to Dean Connors) with zero picks as the Rams beat the Chiefs 20-12 — the clearest riser of the whole slate and a name to know in dynasty.
- **Kyler Murray made his Vikings debut** in the 13-10 win over the Giants, going 2-of-3 for 10 yards on his lone series as Minnesota's new QB1; J.J. McCarthy (4-of-7, 34 yards) played most of the first half and looked competent enough that the QB2 battle isn't fully settled despite ongoing trade rumors.
- **Quinshon Judkins took 80% of Cleveland's starter snaps** and opened the game with three straight carries in the Browns' 34-10 loss to the Bears — clear early-down workhorse signal.
- **Shedeur Sanders threw an interception and was outplayed by Deshaun Watson** in that same Browns loss — Watson remains firmly ahead in Cleveland's QB competition.
- **Bryce Young managed just 3-of-5 for 18 yards** behind a patchwork Panthers line missing both projected starting tackles (Ickey Ekwonu, knee; Taylor Moton, blood clot) in a 29-14 loss to Buffalo — context matters here, don't overreact.
- **Chuba Hubbard (RB, CAR) is week-to-week with a hamstring strain** and sat out tonight's loss to the Bills — coaches are "very confident" for Week 1, but it's worth tracking practice reports.
- **J'Mari Taylor, an undrafted rookie RB, ran for 78 total yards and the go-ahead 4th-quarter TD** as the Jaguars rallied to beat the Saints 24-20 — making a real push for a roster/practice-squad spot.
- **Jaxson Dart took a scary hit and a trip to the medical tent**, then returned to throw a 15-yard TD to Malachi Fields in the Giants' loss — passed the concussion check, finished 3-of-4 for 26 yards and a score.
- **Myles Price (WR, MIN) caught 4 passes for 51 yards, including a one-handed TD** from Carson Wentz — a standout in a crowded Vikings WR room behind Justin Jefferson.
