# Training Camp Report — August 15, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight window between Friday's preseason openers and today's four Saturday games (Browns-Bears, Panthers-Bills, Vikings-Giants, Jaguars-Saints at 1pm ET; Rams-Chiefs at 4pm; Eagles-Ravens at 7pm; Cowboys-Seahawks at 8pm). Mostly catch-up on box-score details from Thursday/Friday's openers plus the still-unresolved Mariota injury._

## Top Storylines

- **Marcus Mariota's knee injury remains unresolved**: Commanders coach Dan Quinn had no update after Friday's game, saying he'd have more information "Sunday" after further evaluation — worth checking back before locking in Washington's QB2 outlook.
- **Graham Mertz (QB, HOU) tore his ACL** in Thursday's opener vs. the Chargers and needs season-ending surgery — leaves Houston with only two healthy QBs on the roster and is a depth-chart item to watch, not a fantasy-relevant loss himself.
- **Jahan Dotson locked in as the Falcons' clear WR2** in Friday's loss to Denver, playing 100% of Tua Tagovailoa's snaps opposite Drake London (who played just two snaps, apparently by design/rotation, not confirmed injury) — Olamide Zaccheaus worked mainly out of the slot instead.
- **Kyle Pitts was Tua's clear first read** in that same Falcons-Broncos game — all 4 of Tua's completions/targets on the opening series went to Pitts (3 catches, 22 yards) with London out early and Bijan Robinson not playing.
- **Will Mallory (TE, IND) led all Colts pass-catchers** with 6 catches on 7 targets for 80 yards in Thursday's 13-13 tie with New England, playing behind Anthony Richardson's clean half (11/14, 145 yards, rushing TD).
- **De'Zhaun Stribling (WR, SF) backed up his camp buzz with real production**: 7 catches on 8 targets for 63 yards, all in the first half of Thursday's win over Tennessee — with Ricky Pearsall out for the year and Christian Kirk banged up, he's carving out real Year 1 FLEX appeal.
