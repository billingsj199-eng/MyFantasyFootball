# Training Camp Report — August 16, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quiet Sunday between preseason weeks — no games tonight, with Preseason Week 2 not kicking off until Friday (Titans-Falcons, Chiefs-Seahawks). Practice-report and transaction news only; biggest item is the Steelers' WR injury situation dragging into a second week._

## Top Storylines

- **Steelers WR1/WR2 Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf both missed Sunday practice again**, and HC Mike McCarthy said they're "hard-pressed" to return this week — Pittman's lower-body issue dates to Aug. 8, Metcalf's undisclosed injury to Aug. 11. Real concern for a newly-paired duo (Pittman just extended 3-yr/$59M after the trade from Indy).
- **Raiders signed veteran WR Noah Brown** after two injury-marred years in Washington — lands in a thin room behind Tre Tucker; deep-league watch only given his injury history.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane (rookie WR, MIA) is behind Rashod Bateman on Miami's first depth chart** — tempers hype on the rookie a bit; he's in the WR3/4 mix for now, not a clear early-target earner.
- **Minor roster churn**: Lions signed RB Trayveon Williams (deep-bench add); Bears waived WR Kyron Hudson. Nothing fantasy-relevant beyond depth.
