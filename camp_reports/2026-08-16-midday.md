## Top Storylines

- **Camden Brown, an undrafted rookie WR, caught 2 TDs (including a diving one-handed grab) as the Cowboys beat the Seahawks 17-7** to close out Preseason Week 1 — a real roster-spot push for a UDFA out of Georgia Southern; deep-league/dynasty stash only for now, but a name to watch through August cuts.
