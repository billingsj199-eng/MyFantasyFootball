# Training Camp Report — August 17, 2026 (End of Day)

_Evening practice reports rolled in with one big injury swing (Breece Hall), a milestone recovery step (Malik Nabers), and a rookie WR's camp likely wiped out (Jordyn Tyson). Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf remain unchanged — still out, no new update tonight._

## Top Storylines

- **Malik Nabers (WR, NYG) worked in full team drills for the first time** since tearing his ACL, taking reps in both 11-on-11 and 7-on-7 — the clearest signal yet that a Week 1 return is realistic.
- **Breece Hall (RB, NYJ) left practice early with a strained right groin** and is being sent for imaging; HC Aaron Glenn called it "not a big deal" but it's worth tracking before finalizing Round 1-2 draft boards.
- **Saints rookie WR Jordyn Tyson's hamstring injury is reportedly a ~2-month issue**, putting his Week 1 availability in real doubt.
- **Free agent RB Najee Harris visited the Giants**, who have thin depth behind an ailing Cam Skattebo — a signing would be notable for NYG backfield shares.
- **Dolphins rookie WR Chris Bell (3rd-round, Louisville) was activated off the NFI list**, clearing him to compete for snaps in Miami's receiver room.
- **Colts WR Alec Pierce is confirmed out for the Falcons joint practices**, officially missing all of training camp as he recovers from ankle surgery — could cost him regular-season games too.
- **No update on Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf** — both remain sidelined as of tonight's report, still trending toward missing Friday's game vs. the Jets.
