# Training Camp Report — August 18, 2026 (End of Day)

_Joint-practice injuries dominated Tuesday: Alvin Kamara left early with a knee issue, Chargers C Tyler Biadasz was carted off, and both Mike Evans and Quentin Johnston exited the 49ers-Chargers session banged up. On the brighter side, Shanahan shut down McCaffrey contract-holdout speculation, Josh Jacobs is back at practice, and Najee Harris has a new home in New York._

## Top Storylines

- **Alvin Kamara left Tuesday's joint practice vs. the Cowboys early with an apparent knee injury**, walking off with a trainer. No official severity yet — a name to watch closely Wednesday morning.
- **Chargers C Tyler Biadasz was carted off Tuesday's joint practice with the 49ers**, unable to put weight on his leg after a 49ers defensive lineman fell on him; rookie 2nd-rounder Jake Slaughter stepped in at center.
- **49ers WR Mike Evans and Chargers WR Quentin Johnston both exited that same joint practice early** — Evans with his ankle/foot wrapped and iced, Johnston limping off. Worth checking Wednesday's report before banking on either.
- **Kyle Shanahan shut down contract-holdout speculation around Christian McCaffrey**, confirming his camp absence is tightness-related, not a standoff — Ian Rapoport backed that up. Removes the scariest read on his absence.
- **Packers RB Josh Jacobs returned to practice Tuesday** after nearly two weeks out with a groin injury — no long-term concern, full workload outlook intact.
- **Najee Harris signed a one-year deal with the Giants**, landing in a crowded backfield after his Chargers stint was wiped out by an eye injury and a torn Achilles. Not fantasy-relevant yet in redraft, but a name to track in a muddled NY backfield.
- **Bills WR Keon Coleman was seen in a walking boot Tuesday** with a sprained foot/toe from Saturday's preseason-opener TD catch; coaching staff isn't sounding alarmed.
