# Training Camp Report — August 18, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news was injury-heavy: a relief follow-up on Breece Hall, a new multi-week knee injury for Bears RB Kyle Monangai, and a real Week 1 scare for rookie Jeremiyah Love in Arizona. Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf remain unchanged, still trending toward missing Friday's game._

## Top Storylines

- **Breece Hall's (RB, NYJ) follow-up MRI confirms his groin strain is minor** — the Jets are calling it "weeks, not months" and still expect him back for Week 1. Good news after last night's early-exit scare.
- **Bears RB Kyle Monangai suffered a hyperextended knee and is expected to miss multiple weeks**, per an MRI Monday. The Bears have roughly a month before their opener, and the outlook is considered positive.
- **Cardinals rookie RB Jeremiyah Love (No. 3 overall pick) has a high ankle sprain and is expected to sit out the rest of the preseason**, with Week 1 now genuinely in some doubt — a real dent in his rookie-year draft stock.
- **Rams WR Puka Nacua left Tuesday's joint practice vs. the Cowboys early with groin soreness**, described as minor by the team; not currently viewed as a long absence.
- **No change on Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf** — HC Mike McCarthy again called both "hard-pressed" to return this week, making them increasingly likely to miss Friday's game vs. the Jets.
