# Training Camp Report — August 19, 2026 (End of Day)

_Wednesday's joint practices produced a genuine season-ending loss (Texans WR Jayden Higgins, torn ACL) plus two fresh injury scares — Ravens C Danny Pinter carted off and Colts TE Tyler Warren exiting with a groin injury, both severity-unknown as of tonight. On the transaction side, Keenan Allen signed with the Colts and the Titans added RB depth. Some good news too: Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf are both now expected back for Week 1 despite missing preseason time._

## Top Storylines

- **Texans WR Jayden Higgins (2025 2nd-rounder) tore his ACL and is out for the season**, per ESPN sources — he went down awkwardly on a red-zone rep during Tuesday's joint practice with the Raiders, with the diagnosis confirmed Wednesday. Houston's WR room now leans harder on Xavier Hutchinson, Tank Dell, and Jaylin Noel.
- **Ravens C Danny Pinter was carted off Wednesday's joint practice vs. the Vikings** after getting rolled up on; team medical staff examined his knee and HC Jesse Minter had no update on severity. Pinter is Baltimore's projected starting center — a real O-line watch point if it's serious.
- **Colts TE Tyler Warren exited Wednesday's joint practice vs. the Falcons with a groin injury**, limping off after a route in 11-on-11s. HC Shane Steichen confirmed the injury but gave no timeline — worth checking Thursday's report before drafting him at his current TE1-adjacent price.
- **Keenan Allen signed a one-year deal (up to $8.32M) with the Colts**, reuniting with HC Shane Steichen from their Chargers days; Indy waived RB Roydell Williams in the corresponding move. With Alec Pierce still on PUP after ankle surgery, Allen has a real path to early-season targets in Daniel Jones' offense.
- **Michael Pittman Jr. and DK Metcalf are both now expected to be ready for Week 1** against the Falcons despite missing preseason practice time — an upgrade from Wednesday morning's "hard-pressed to return" framing on the two Steelers WRs.
- **Titans signed veteran RB D'Ernest Johnson** to a one-year deal (and waived Dominic Richardson) after a camp in which a Titans RB has been banged up in roughly 75% of practices — depth-only addition, deep-league/handcuff relevance at most.
- **Eagles rookie WR Makai Lemon (hamstring) sat out again Wednesday**, nearing two weeks sidelined; Philly is being deliberate given DeVonta Smith's own hamstring issue, putting Lemon's readiness for the Week 1 opener at Washington in some doubt.
