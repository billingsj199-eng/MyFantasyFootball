# Training Camp Report — August 19, 2026 (Midday)

_Follow-ups on Tuesday's joint-practice injuries broke Wednesday morning: Alvin Kamara's knee is an MCL sprain (4-6 weeks, real IR risk), Tyler Biadasz's is far worse than first feared (long-term, ACL damage), while Mike Evans and Quentin Johnston were both cleared as non-issues. Elsewhere, Chiefs WR Tyquan Thornton was carted off with a hamstring strain and the Falcons added pass-rush depth._

## Top Storylines

- **Alvin Kamara (NO RB) diagnosed with an MCL sprain, expected out at least 4-6 weeks** per Adam Schefter — no tear, but he's a real candidate to open the season on IR. Audric Estime is the clearest early-down beneficiary in New Orleans' backfield.
- **Tyler Biadasz's (LAC C) knee injury is worse than initially feared** — NFL Network's Mike Garafolo says it's being treated as a long-term absence with multiple ligaments hurt, and Schefter reports ACL damage. Rookie 2nd-rounder Jake Slaughter is now the presumptive starting center.
- **Mike Evans (SF WR) injury scare is a non-story** — Shanahan says Tuesday's exit was tightness tied to his existing quad issue, not a new injury. No change to his outlook as SF's WR1.
- **Quentin Johnston (LAC WR) is "totally fine"** per Schefter, not expected to miss meaningful time after limping off Tuesday.
- **Chiefs WR Tyquan Thornton was carted off Tuesday's practice with a confirmed hamstring strain** — severity still unclear, worth watching in an already-thin KC WR room behind Rice/Worthy.
- **Rashawn Slater (LAC LT) returned to practice Wednesday, working at left guard**, but the Chargers still aren't planning to play him in Thursday's preseason opener — trending positive but no clean bill of health yet.
- **Falcons signed veteran EDGE Za'Darius Smith** (one year, up to $8M), unretiring to bolster the pass rush after Jalon Walker's season-ending ACL tear and James Pearce Jr.'s suspension — IDP-relevant only, no redraft-skill-position impact.
- **Preseason Week 2 kicks off Thursday** (Raiders@Texans, 49ers@Chargers), with roster cutdowns to 53 players on Aug. 30 — the deadline that will start clarifying a lot of the depth-chart battles tracked all camp.
