# Training Camp Report — August 20, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quiet evening on the injury/transaction front — the main development is confirmation that Ravens C Danny Pinter's Wednesday injury is a season-ending torn patellar tendon. Otherwise the day was mostly about Preseason Week 2 kicking off, with two games tonight (Raiders–Texans, 49ers–Chargers) featuring limited-but-real starter run for Justin Herbert and Fernando Mendoza._

## Top Storylines

- **Danny Pinter's Ravens injury is season-ending** — the carted-off knee injury from Wednesday's joint practice with the Vikings is confirmed as a torn patellar tendon; Pinter is expected to land on IR and not return until 2027. Fantasy takeaway: no direct skill-position impact, but Ethan Pocic now takes over the starting center competition, a small ding to Baltimore's interior run-blocking for Lamar Jackson/Derrick Henry.
- **Preseason Week 2 opened with the Raiders–Texans game** — Houston won 20-13, but HC DeMeco Ryans rested the vast majority of starters (C.J. Stroud, Will Anderson, Danielle Hunter) after Tuesday's Jayden Higgins ACL tear soured the week; Fernando Mendoza played roughly the first half for Las Vegas. Fantasy takeaway: nothing actionable from Houston's side; watch Mendoza's next box score as a late-round QB dart-throw signal.
- **49ers–Chargers preseason game tonight (10pm ET)** — Justin Herbert is set to run LA's first-team offense for one drive in his only preseason look of 2026, with Brock Purdy also expected to get a short series for San Francisco before Trey Lance/backups take over. Fantasy takeaway: too small a sample to move rankings, but it's the last live look at each starter before Week 1.
