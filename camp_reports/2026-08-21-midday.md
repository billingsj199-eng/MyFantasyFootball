# Training Camp Report — August 21, 2026 (Midday)

_No new camp injuries broke overnight — this window was almost entirely driven by Thursday night's two Preseason Week 2 games (Texans 20, Raiders 22 and 49ers 41, Chargers 17). The headline is Fernando Mendoza's second straight shaky preseason outing, while San Francisco's banged-up RB/WR rooms produced several deep-league flashes._

## Top Storylines

- **Fernando Mendoza threw a pick-six on his second pass** in Las Vegas's win over Houston, his second straight rocky preseason outing. Fantasy takeaway: rookie growing pains continue — cools near-term streamer buzz heading into final roster evaluations.
- **Woody Marks got every Houston RB carry** and scored a 20-yard TD while David Montgomery sat out. Fantasy takeaway: a real-game data point favoring Marks in the Texans' backfield timeshare.
- **C.J. Stroud made his preseason debut**, leading a TD drive (4/6, 27 yds) before exiting. Fantasy takeaway: confirms he's healthy and on track for a normal Week 1 workload, no injury concern.
- **Justin Herbert and Brock Purdy both took their only preseason snaps** of 2026 (Herbert 1/2 passing on one series; Purdy 4/6 for 29 yards). Fantasy takeaway: standard healthy vet cameos, nothing that moves either ranking.
- **49ers rookie WR De'Zhaun Stribling strung together several sharp catches** against a Chargers starting corner. Fantasy takeaway: continues climbing San Francisco's thinned-out WR depth chart (Pearsall out, Kittle on PUP) — real deep-league/dynasty stash appeal.
- **Jacob Cowing took a punt back 83 yards for a TD**, sparking SF's 41-17 win. Fantasy takeaway: special-teams flash boosts his 53-man roster odds in a crowded WR room.
- **Khalil Herbert re-signed with San Francisco** weeks after being cut, then scored a TD that same night as Patrick Taylor Jr. went to IR. Fantasy takeaway: deep RB4/5 churn — fringe relevance, but a name to know if SF's backfield thins further.

## Injury Report

No new skill-position injuries since last night's end-of-day digest. (Danny Pinter's season-ending patellar tendon tear remains the week's most significant camp injury — see prior reports.)

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Houston RB room**: Woody Marks handled every carry with Montgomery out Thursday — worth tracking whether that's usage-by-design or just a rest day for Montgomery.
- **San Francisco WR/RB depth**: with Pearsall out and Kittle on PUP, camp bodies like Stribling, Cowing, McCormick, and Khalil Herbert are all getting real preseason run to make the 53-man roster.

- **De'Zhaun Stribling (WR, SF)** — flashing in a wide-open 49ers WR room; worth a deep-league watch.
