# Training Camp Report — August 22, 2026 (End of Day)

_Saturday brought a full 8-game Preseason Week 2 slate. The headline is Michael Penix Jr. finally getting cleared for full 11-on-11 work in Atlanta, plus another lopsided outing in the Browns' Sanders-Watson QB battle. A scary ambulance injury for Eagles depth RB Ja'Quinden Jackson had a positive update by night's end._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Penix Jr. fully cleared for 11-on-11 work** for the first time since his November ACL surgery; he'll practice in team periods Monday. Fantasy takeaway: the Falcons' QB1 battle with Tua Tagovailoa can finally start in earnest — worth tracking closely into September.
- **Shedeur Sanders outplays Deshaun Watson again** in Bills' 31-7 rout of the Browns — Sanders went 9-of-11 for 74 yards with a TD (tipped-pass INT was the lone blemish), while Watson was 5-of-12 for 35 yards, an INT, and a 14.6 rating in relief. Fantasy takeaway: Sanders continues building the stronger camp case for the Week 1 starting job.
- **Ravens' defense throttles Vikings 13-3**; J.J. McCarthy was rough again (5-of-10, 81 yards, no TD) and was pulled after absorbing a couple of hard hits, walking off gingerly. Fantasy takeaway: shaky camp continues for McCarthy, and there's now an injury watch on top of the QB1-battle concerns behind Kyler Murray.
- **Falcons blow out Colts 34-6** behind backup QBs Cooper Rush and Jack Strand, who combined to complete 14-of-15 for 137 yards and a TD in the first half. Fantasy takeaway: mostly a benchwarmers game, but the offense looked crisp — modest positive signal for Atlanta's depth.
- **Rams shut out Saints 34-0.** Fantasy takeaway: another lifeless Saints preseason outing; New Orleans skill-position competition remains muddled.
- **Giants beat Dolphins 20-13.** Fantasy takeaway: no major fantasy-relevant developments; backups dominated the reps on both sides.
- **Bears blast Bengals 27-3** — Chicago played its starters while Cincinnati sat theirs, so treat as a one-sided evaluation game. Fantasy takeaway: don't read much into Bengals backups' modest 256 total yards against Chicago's 1s.
- **Buccaneers beat Chiefs 23-9** — Patrick Mahomes rested; backups handled the load for Kansas City. Fantasy takeaway: nothing actionable for Chiefs fantasy assets from this one.

## Injury Report

- **Ja'Quinden Jackson (RB, PHI)** — taken off the field by ambulance after a violent hit on the opening kickoff vs. the Patriots; the Eagles said he has "strength and movement in all extremities" and is undergoing further evaluation. Fantasy impact: minimal — he's a deep-roster RB with no NFL regular-season carries — but a scary moment worth noting.
- **J.J. McCarthy (QB, MIN)** — pulled from Saturday's loss to Baltimore after taking a couple of hard hits and walking off gingerly; no formal diagnosis yet. Fantasy impact: worth monitoring — an injury on top of already-shaky camp performances would be a significant blow to his outlook.
- Previously reported injuries (Michael Penix Jr.'s ACL — now cleared, see above; Jake Bobo knee, Jayden Higgins ACL, Danny Pinter patellar tendon, Puka Nacua psoas/back, Jeremiyah Love ankle, Ricky Pearsall PCL, Luther Burden groin, Nic Scourton ACL) otherwise unchanged as of this scan.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Browns QB battle**: Sanders continues to outplay Watson in game reps; no formal decision announced yet, but the tape favors Sanders.
- **Vikings QB depth**: McCarthy remains firmly behind Kyler Murray, and Carson Wentz saw extended run behind him Saturday.
