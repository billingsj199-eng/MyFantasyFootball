# Training Camp Report — August 22, 2026 (Midday)

_The headline since last night's digest is Friday night's full Preseason Week 2 slate — Jets, Panthers and Packers all won convincingly, with several depth pieces flashing. No major new injuries have surfaced overnight beyond what's already in prior digests._

## Top Storylines

- **Jets blank Steelers 17-0**, with Geno Smith going a perfect 7-for-7 on the opening TD drive (13-yard score to TE Jelani Woods) before being pulled. Fantasy takeaway: a clean final extended look for Smith; Adonai Mitchell (2-29) continues building camp buzz behind Garrett Wilson.
- **Jets defense picks off Steelers backup QB Will Howard twice**, after New York failed to record a single INT all of last season. Fantasy takeaway: modest positive signal for a Jets defense that could be a streaming option.
- **Panthers roll Jaguars 34-17** behind Bryce Young (5-of-6, 49 yards). Fantasy takeaway: another clean, efficient camp outing continues to build confidence in Carolina's passing game.
- **Jonathon Brooks scores his first career NFL touchdown** in the win, capping a comeback from two separate ACL tears (2023 and 2024). Fantasy takeaway: still buried on Carolina's depth chart, but a name worth stashing in dynasty/deep leagues if he keeps trending up.
- **Jaguars RB Ameer Abdullah, 33, looks spry**: 45 yards on 6 carries (7.5 YPC) plus 3 catches for 37 yards. Fantasy takeaway: could be carving out a real passing-down role in Jacksonville's backfield.
- **Packers thump Broncos 33-13.** Jordan Love (5-of-7, 56 yards) hit MarShawn Lloyd for a TD, while Bo Nix (6-of-9, 76 yards) found rookie-adjacent RB RJ Harvey for a 21-yard score. Fantasy takeaway: Lloyd is getting run with Green Bay's 1s while Josh Jacobs sits out with a groin injury — worth a speculative add in deeper leagues; Harvey's receiving juice is a PPR plus if he locks down passing-down work in Denver.
