# Training Camp Report — August 24, 2026 (End of Day)

_Cutdown to 80 hit today (85→80), with the 53-man deadline six days out (Sunday, Aug. 30). Houston made a fantasy-relevant trade, and three notable injury-comeback stories all popped on the same Monday practice._

## Top Storylines

- **Texans trade for WR Kayshon Boutte**, sending S Jaylen Reed and a 2028 7th-rounder to New England. Fantasy takeaway: fills Houston's need after Jayden Higgins' season-ending ACL tear — Boutte gets a fresh role after being phased out behind Romeo Doubs/A.J. Brown in New England; worth a very late-round dynasty/best-ball flier in an offense that badly needed bodies at WR.
- **Malik Nabers practiced without a red no-contact jersey for the first time** since tearing his ACL, taking 13 reps (some 11-on-11) and catching 6 passes. Fantasy takeaway: Giants coach John Harbaugh called a Week 1 return (Sept. 13 vs. Dallas) "reasonable" — bump Nabers back toward WR1 dynasty and redraft consideration if you'd faded him.
- **DeVonta Smith and rookie Makai Lemon were both full practice participants** for the first time since Aug. 3, each working through hamstring injuries together. Fantasy takeaway: no setback for either — Smith's redraft floor and Lemon's rookie-hype ceiling both stay intact heading into the final preseason week.
- **Kaelon Black has emerged as the new favorite for the 49ers' RB2 job**, passing an injured Jordan James after an efficient camp/preseason showing (8 carries, 33 yards, decisive vision in his most recent look). Fantasy takeaway: worth a stash in Shanahan's backfield as the direct McCaffrey handcuff.
- **Xavier Legette is in a walking boot with no return timeline** and will likely miss Friday's preseason finale after tweaking his ankle in last week's joint practice with Jacksonville. Fantasy takeaway: further muddies Carolina's WR3 picture — Horn, Metchie and Moore are the beneficiaries of any missed time.
- **Jeremiyah Love (ankle) will sit out Arizona's joint practice with Green Bay and Friday's finale**, but LaFleur says he's "progressing well" and the team is still targeting a Week 1 return. Fantasy takeaway: still a hold in dynasty/redraft, just don't expect preseason reps to firm up his role before Sept. 13.
- **Jets rookie TE Kenyon Sadiq (hernia surgery) took another step forward** in practice, with coach Aaron Glenn saying the team is targeting Week 1 for his return. Fantasy takeaway: encouraging sign for a player who's missed extended time since his May surgery.

## Injury Report

- **Xavier Legette (WR, CAR)** — ankle, walking boot, no firm timeline, considered day-to-day; will likely miss the preseason finale. Fantasy impact: opens more camp reps for Horn/Metchie/Moore in the interim.
- **Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI)** — high ankle sprain, held out of Tuesday's joint practice and Friday's finale, but "progressing well" per coach LaFleur; still hopeful for Week 1. Fantasy impact: dynasty/redraft stock holds steady, no fresh setback.
- **Kenyon Sadiq (TE, NYJ)** — recovering from hernia surgery, took a step forward Monday; team targeting Week 1. Fantasy impact: encouraging but still unproven — deep-league TE watch only until he's back on the field in pads.
- **DeVonta Smith (WR, PHI)** and **Makai Lemon (WR, PHI)** — both hamstring, both full practice participants for the first time since Aug. 3. Fantasy impact: no lingering concern for either heading into Week 1.
- **Malik Nabers (WR, NYG)** — ACL recovery, shed the red non-contact jersey for the first time, 13 reps. Fantasy impact: Week 1 return now "reasonable" per Harbaugh.
- No new information since the midday report on Ashton Jeanty's (RB, LV) ankle sprain — still no confirmed timeline.
