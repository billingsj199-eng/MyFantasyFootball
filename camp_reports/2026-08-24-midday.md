# Training Camp Report — August 24, 2026 (Midday)

_Big morning: the Browns finally resolved their QB battle, and San Francisco got both stars back on the field the same day the Raiders' new lead back left practice hurt. Six days until the 53-man cutdown (Sunday, Aug. 30)._

## Top Storylines

- **Deshaun Watson named Browns' Week 1 starter over Shedeur Sanders.** Fantasy takeaway: Watson is back as the presumptive CLE QB1 (his first start since tearing his Achilles in Oct. 2024) despite a rough Saturday (5-of-12, 35 yds, INT); treat him as a low-end streamer at best and keep expectations muted for both Cleveland passing-game options.
- **Shedeur Sanders loses the starting job** despite a strong Aug. 22 outing (9-of-11, 74 yds, TD). Fantasy takeaway: no Week 1 value, but stays one injury/slump away from taking over — worth a dynasty stash, not a redraft pick.
- **Raiders RB Ashton Jeanty left Sunday's practice with a believed sprained ankle**, unable to put weight on the leg; not considered long-term but timeline unknown. Fantasy takeaway: monitor closely — rookie Mike Washington Jr. took the first-team reps behind him, but Jeanty was locked in as Vegas's clear lead back before this.
- **49ers activated George Kittle off the PUP list Sunday**, his first practice since January's Achilles injury. Fantasy takeaway: doesn't guarantee a Week 1 return but is a strong signal he's trending toward playing within the first month — bump his ADP outlook back up.
- **Christian McCaffrey also returned to full practice Sunday** after calf tightness that cost him time earlier in camp. Fantasy takeaway: no setback, RB1 outlook intact; OC Kubiak called having both stars back a "boost" late in camp.
- **Cardinals RB Trey Benson's roster spot is in real jeopardy** — a knee flare-up plus a crowded backfield (Allgeier, Conner, Love) has him fighting just to be RB4 over Bam Knight, with trade buzz building. Fantasy takeaway: cut him from fantasy consideration barring a trade to a better landing spot.
- **Travis Etienne Jr. solidifying as the Saints' clear lead back** with Alvin Kamara still out (MCL sprain, min. one month). Fantasy takeaway: Etienne is the direct early-season beneficiary — a solid streaming/flex option in Weeks 1-4 even in a committee-flavored backfield.

- **Trey Benson (RB, ARI)** — knee flare-up (ongoing management, not a new structural injury). Fantasy impact: further buries him on an already crowded depth chart.

- **Browns**: Watson officially wins the Week 1 job; Sanders remains QB2 and the emergency plan if Watson struggles or gets hurt again.

- **Saints**: Etienne fronting the backfield with Kamara sidelined; Kendre Miller (back) and Devin Neal (hamstring) still working back from their own ailments, thinning the depth behind him.
- **Raiders**: Rookie Mike Washington Jr. getting extra first-team run behind/in place of Jeanty pending his ankle evaluation.
