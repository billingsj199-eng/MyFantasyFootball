# Training Camp Report — August 25, 2026 (End of Day)

_Tuesday practice reports plus fresh injury and depth-chart developments. Cutdown to 53 is Sunday, Aug. 30 at 6pm ET, so every camp-battle update carries extra weight this week._

## Top Storylines

- **Ashton Jeanty (LV) DNP Tuesday with his ankle sprain**, and HC Klint Kubiak was noncommittal beyond "he's on the mend." Fantasy takeaway: real Week 1 risk — rookie Mike Washington Jr. has taken over starting reps in the meantime.
- **Ty Chandler (NO) suffered a knee injury the Saints fear could be season-ending**, per HC Kellen Moore. Fantasy takeaway: thins an already-shorthanded Saints RB room (Kamara out), further boosting Zamir White/Travis Etienne Jr.'s workload outlook.
- **Panthers HC Dave Canales confirmed Chuba Hubbard and Jonathon Brooks will operate as a Week 1 committee** once Hubbard is healthy. Fantasy takeaway: caps Hubbard's bell-cow ceiling and makes Brooks a real Week 1 factor, not just handcuff insurance.
- **Zamir White made his Saints practice debut** two days after the trade from San Francisco, and spoke to media for the first time. Fantasy takeaway: confirms he's in the Week 1 mix behind Etienne with Kamara sidelined.
- **Sam LaPorta (DET) held out of practice this week with a hip injury**; Dan Campbell was non-committal on Week 1 availability. Fantasy takeaway: real doubt for a top-5 fantasy TE — watch closely into cutdown week.
- **Caleb Douglas (MIA) listed as a starting WR** on the Dolphins' first official depth chart, alongside Jalen Tolbert and Malik Washington. Fantasy takeaway: the rookie 3rd-rounder is a real Week 1 snap-share bet, not just a camp name.
- **Breece Hall (NYJ) worked on the side field cutting/changing direction**, and Aaron Glenn confirmed the plan remains for a Week 1 return from his groin strain. Fantasy takeaway: trending in the right direction for the opener vs. Tennessee.
- **Josh Jacobs (GB) back at full practice** after a near two-week groin absence, though pending NFL discipline over an offseason arrest still looms over his workload outlook.

## Injury Report

- **Ashton Jeanty (RB, LV)** — ankle sprain, DNP Tuesday. Timeline unclear; Kubiak wouldn't rule out a longer absence. Fantasy impact: Mike Washington Jr. is the direct beneficiary and a priority stash.
- **Ty Chandler (RB, NO)** — knee injury vs. Rams, Saints fear season-ending. Fantasy impact: thins Saints depth further, non-factor for now.
- **Sam LaPorta (TE, DET)** — hip flare-up, held out all week. Fantasy impact: Week 1 status genuinely uncertain for a top-tier fantasy TE.
- **Breece Hall (RB, NYJ)** — groin strain (since Aug 18), now doing change-of-direction work. Fantasy impact: on track for Week 1 vs. Titans.
- **Chris Olave (WR, NO)** — left practice early after a hard landing on a deep ball; Kellen Moore said he just had the wind knocked out of him, no further testing needed. Fantasy impact: scare only, no lingering concern.
- **Luther Burden III (WR, CHI)** — groin injury recovery "going well" per Rome Odunze, trending toward the Week 1 opener vs. Carolina. Fantasy impact: positive update after missing most of camp/preseason.
- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB)** — back at practice after groin absence; pending league discipline is now the bigger variable for his Week 1 workload.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Panthers RB**: Canales confirms a Hubbard/Brooks Week 1 committee — Brooks has been taking most first-team reps in Hubbard's absence.
- **Dolphins WR**: Caleb Douglas starting alongside Tolbert/Washington on Tuesday's initial depth chart; Malik Willis remains the established starting QB.
- **Saints RB**: Zamir White debuts in the rotation with Etienne as Kamara (MCL) and now Chandler (knee) are both out.
- **Vikings**: Initial 2026 depth chart lists Aaron Jones Sr. and Jordan Mason as co-starters at RB.

## Rookie Watch

- **Mike Washington Jr. (RB, LV)** — Two strong preseason showings (63 yds/6 car, 56 yds/9 car) and now the direct beneficiary of Jeanty's ankle injury, taking starting reps. Kubiak wants more from him in pass protection/receiving before fully trusting him on passing downs.
- **Caleb Douglas (WR, MIA)** — Confirmed Week 1 starter on Tuesday's depth chart. Building chemistry with Malik Willis since camp opened.
- **Cyrus Allen (WR, KC)** — 5th-round rookie drawing heavy camp buzz working with Patrick Mahomes since July; ceiling of a weekly WR3-4 with spike-week upside if Rice/Worthy miss time.
