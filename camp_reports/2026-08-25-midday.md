# Training Camp Report — August 25, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight/morning stretch with no preseason games since Sunday — most movement came via Monday's transaction wire rather than the practice field. 53-man cutdown deadline is Sunday, Aug. 30 at 6pm ET._

## Top Storylines

- **Saints trade for RB Zamir White**, sending LB Deion Jones to the 49ers. Fantasy takeaway: real depth addition behind Alvin Kamara (MCL sprain, out 4-6 weeks) — White is now in the mix for early-down work alongside Travis Etienne Jr. in New Orleans' backfield.
- **Cardinals waived RB Trey Benson** with a knee injury designation, officially ending his run behind Conner/Allgeier/Love. Fantasy takeaway: off the fantasy radar in Arizona; only relevant again if he clears waivers and lands a fresh opportunity elsewhere.
- **Jordan James (rib fracture) is back at practice** for the 49ers, but Kaelon Black still holds the edge for the RB2/McCaffrey-handcuff role. Fantasy takeaway: competition isn't fully settled — Black is the priority stash, but don't fully close the book on James.
- **Chuba Hubbard (hamstring) reportedly hit 20 mph in rehab** and remains on track for Week 1, per beat reporting. Fantasy takeaway: encouraging progress: expect an early-season committee with Jonathon Brooks as Hubbard ramps back up to full cutting speed.

## Injury Report

- **Trey Benson (RB, ARI)** — knee; waived by the Cardinals Monday, reverts to IR if unclaimed. Fantasy impact: no longer fantasy-relevant in Arizona.
- **Jordan James (RB, SF)** — rib fracture; back at practice, still working back into the RB2 competition. Fantasy impact: Kaelon Black remains the preferred stash, James is a name to recheck before Week 1.
- **Chuba Hubbard (RB, CAR)** — hamstring, previously week-to-week; now hitting 20 mph in rehab and trending toward a Week 1 return. Fantasy impact: no change to his ROS outlook, still on track.
- No new updates on Ashton Jeanty (ankle, LV), Jeremiyah Love (ankle, ARI), Xavier Legette (ankle, CAR) or Malik Nabers (ACL, NYG) since last night's report.
