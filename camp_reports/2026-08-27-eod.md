# Training Camp Report — August 27, 2026 (End of Day)

_Preseason Week 3 finales played out across the league tonight (Steelers-Bills, Patriots-Browns, 49ers-Raiders, Rams-Chargers) with virtually every Week 1 starter held out — cutdown to 53 is Sunday, Aug. 30 at 6pm ET. Biggest actual fantasy news came off the field: Josh Jacobs was formally charged, and Wan'Dale Robinson left a joint practice getting checked for a concussion._

## Top Storylines

- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB) was formally charged with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property** stemming from his May arrest in a Brown County domestic incident. Fantasy takeaway: this escalates rather than resolves the suspension question — the NFL's own investigation is still pending, and a ruling before Week 1 is a real possibility. Treat him as legitimate bust risk until there's clarity.
- **Mike Washington Jr. (RB, LV) started again with Ashton Jeanty out and ran for 49 yards on 8 carries** in the preseason finale against the 49ers. Fantasy takeaway: three strong showings in a row now — he's locked in as a real complementary/passing-down piece even once Jeanty is back, and a must-add if Jeanty's ankle lingers into Week 1.
- **Wan'Dale Robinson (WR, TEN) is being evaluated for a concussion** after a hit from Bears safety Xavier Woods during Thursday's joint practice, which also sparked a brief on-field altercation between the teams. Fantasy takeaway: he walked off on his own power, but keep an eye on the protocol with Week 1 approaching.
- **Preseason finales were mostly backup showcases** — Kenny Pickett and Jaylen Warren (No. 2 RB, not a threat to Steelers' starting job) had big nights for Pittsburgh in a 27-15 win over Buffalo (Josh Allen sat), and Dillon Gabriel outplayed both Watson and Sanders' preseason TD totals combined in Cleveland's 27-10 win over a Patriots team that also rested its starters. None of it moves the needle for fantasy-relevant players since the guys who matter didn't play.
- **Roster cuts are three days out** (Sunday 6pm ET deadline) — expect a wave of camp-battle losers and depth-piece cuts through the weekend; watch for anyone in a timeshare getting released outright.

## Injury Report

- **Wan'Dale Robinson (WR, TEN)** — evaluated for a concussion after a big hit in Thursday's joint practice with the Bears; walked off under his own power. Fantasy impact: no confirmed diagnosis yet, but any time in protocol this close to Week 1 is worth tracking.
- **Ashton Jeanty (RB, LV)** — sat out again for the preseason finale vs. the 49ers, no new timeline given. Fantasy impact: unresolved, still trending week-to-week (see Wednesday's report for full context).
