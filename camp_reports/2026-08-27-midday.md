# Training Camp Report — August 27, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning update. Cutdown to 53 is Sunday, Aug. 30 at 6pm ET — three days out. A genuinely quiet overnight news-wise (no new season-ending injuries), with the biggest developments being incremental status updates on stories already in progress: Jeanty's ankle, Nabers' ACL return, and the backup opportunity opening up behind Jeanty._

## Top Storylines

- **Mike Washington Jr. (RB, LV) got real first-team reps Wednesday while Ashton Jeanty sat out**, catching a touchdown out of the backfield in red-zone work and impressing as a receiver. Fantasy takeaway: he's already flashed 8+ YPC across two preseason games — worth a speculative add in deeper leagues as a real change-of-pace/passing-down threat even once Jeanty is back.
- **Ashton Jeanty (RB, LV) status remains murkier than Wednesday's optimism suggested** — coach Klint Kubiak was vague ("he's on the mend"), and Ian Rapoport described it as a low ankle sprain with no clear return timeline, adding there's a real chance he sits out Week 1. Fantasy takeaway: don't fully write off the earlier injury concern — treat as week-to-week, not resolved.
- **Malik Nabers (WR, NYG) has now practiced in a white (contact) jersey multiple days in a row**, taking part in live 7-on-7 and 11-on-11 team drills for the first time this offseason. Coach John Harbaugh said he's "moving the best we've seen him look" but wouldn't commit to a Week 1 return, and Nabers himself won't guarantee it either. Fantasy takeaway: clearest positive signal yet on his ACL recovery, though still not a lock for Week 1.
- **Cyrus Allen (WR, KC), one of the standout rookies of camp, is back at practice after a shin bruise** but was eased back in rather than given a full workload. Fantasy takeaway: stock stays hot — he'd drawn Stefon Diggs comparisons and first-team reps before the injury — but monitor practice reports for a return to full participation.
- **Josh Jacobs (RB, GB) suspension decision still unresolved.** Schefter reiterated a possible ban (6-8 games under the personal conduct policy) remains on the table, with the Packers publicly saying they're prepared for it. Fantasy takeaway: real, unresolved bust risk for anyone drafting him without checking news right up to Week 1.

## Injury Report

- **Ashton Jeanty (RB, LV)** — ankle sprain from Sunday's practice, believed low (not high) ankle sprain per Rapoport; Kubiak declined to give a timeline. Fantasy impact: trending to week-to-week, real chance he's out for Week 1 — a step back from Wednesday's more optimistic framing.
- **Malik Nabers (WR, NYG)** — recovering from torn ACL/meniscus; now practicing in a white jersey and taking live team reps. Fantasy impact: real positive momentum, but neither team nor player will guarantee Week 1 availability yet.
- **Cyrus Allen (WR, KC)** — shin bruise, back at practice but not full participation. Fantasy impact: worth monitoring, but no long-term concern reported.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Raiders backfield**: With Jeanty out, Mike Washington Jr. took the RB1 reps in Wednesday's practice, catching a red-zone touchdown and continuing to show well as a receiver — a real signal the rookie could carve out a complementary role in the Kubiak two-back system even once Jeanty is healthy.

## Rookie Watch

- **Cyrus Allen (WR, KC)** — back at practice after a shin injury; still viewed as one of the top rookie standouts of camp.
