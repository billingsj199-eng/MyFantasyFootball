# Training Camp Report — August 28, 2026 (End of Day)

_Final Friday of the preseason — most starters getting a light or no workload with Sunday's 6pm ET cutdown to 53 two days away. The Falcons' QB battle took center stage again as Penix sat a second straight week while Tua got a "final audition" in Miami._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Penix Jr. will not play in Friday's finale vs. Miami** — Falcons HC Kevin Stefanski says he's still not comfortable putting Penix's surgically repaired knee at risk after just three padded 11-on-11 practices since his return. Fantasy takeaway: no Week 1 starter has been named; Penix says he's confident he'll be ready for the opener, but his practice-only track record keeps this competition unsettled heading into cutdowns.
- **Tua Tagovailoa started Friday's finale at his former team, Miami** — widely framed as a "final audition" after reportedly squandering an early lead in the competition with an uneven camp. Fantasy takeaway: how Tua performs tonight is the last real evidence before the Falcons make a call — still don't lock in either QB's Week 1 outlook.
- **Bills rookie WR Skyler Bell (4th-round) starred in Thursday's preseason finale vs. Pittsburgh** — caught 7-of-10 targets for 49 yards and added a 28-yard touchdown on an end-around. Fantasy takeaway: deep dynasty/best-ball stash in a crowded Buffalo WR room; worth a late-round look in devy/rookie formats.
- **Roster cutdown to 53 is now two days out (Sunday, Aug. 30, 6pm ET)**, with the waiver claim deadline moved up to Monday 10am PT because of the 49ers' early flight to Melbourne for their Sept. 10 opener. Fantasy takeaway: expect a wave of releases and priority-claim scrambles across the weekend — check waiver wire Monday morning, not afternoon.
- Quiet night otherwise league-wide — most other storylines from this morning (James Conner's Week 1 murkiness, Kenneth Walker III's injury absence, Germie Bernard's Steelers WR3 role, Tucker Kraft's clean bill of health) had no material updates since the midday report.

## Injury Report

- **Michael Penix Jr. (QB, ATL)** — recovering from a torn ACL (November surgery); cleared for 11-on-11 last weekend but held out of Friday's finale as a precaution. No firm Week 1 status yet, but he says he expects to be ready.
- No other new fantasy-relevant injuries confirmed since the midday report; James Conner (ARI) and Kenneth Walker III (SEA) situations remain as previously reported, with no official update today.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Falcons QB race**: Still unresolved. Penix has reportedly closed the gap and then some after Tua's shaky camp, but no starter has been named and won't be until after tonight's game and further practice.

## Rookie Watch

- **Skyler Bell (WR, BUF)** — 4th-round rookie, strong final preseason showing (7/10, 49 yds, 1 TD) — buried on the depth chart for now but a name worth knowing in deeper leagues.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Skyler Bell (WR, BUF) — best preseason performance yet, closing camp with momentum.

**Fallers**
- Tua Tagovailoa (QB, ATL) — needs tonight's start to matter; QB1 job still not locked up despite Penix's limited practice reps.
