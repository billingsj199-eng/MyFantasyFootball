# Training Camp Report — August 29, 2026 (End of Day)

_Cutdown day. All 32 teams are trimming to 53 ahead of Sunday's 6pm ET deadline, so Saturday's news cycle was almost entirely transactions rather than practice-field storylines. Most cuts were camp bodies with zero fantasy value — this report only lists the ones that actually move a fantasy roster._

## Top Storylines

- **Dolphins traded backup QB Quinn Ewers to Jacksonville.** Fantasy takeaway: buried behind Trevor Lawrence; drop as a deep-league/dynasty QB stash.
- **Patriots acquired RB Corey Kiner from Arizona** for a 2028 7th-rounder. Fantasy takeaway: slots in as New England's RB3 behind Rhamondre Stevenson and TreVeyon Henderson — speculative add only, not roster-worthy yet.
- **Bills released WR/returner Mecole Hardman Jr.** Fantasy takeaway: off the board in Buffalo; Ray Davis absorbs the punt-return work, no direct fantasy shift.
- **Giants HC John Harbaugh said Odell Beckham Jr. "has done enough to make an NFL roster."** Fantasy takeaway: positive signal he survives Sunday's cuts as a deep-league WR5/6 flier; still not startable.
- **Steelers RB Kaleb Johnson's roster spot remains unresolved** — beat reporting says he's "increasingly likely" to be waived, but no move confirmed as of Saturday night. Fantasy takeaway: recheck Sunday before the deadline; if released, watch for a waiver claim that could reset his outlook.
- **No new skill-position injuries broke in this window** — Saturday was dominated by roster moves, not fresh injury news. Tua Tagovailoa (ATL) and Kenneth Walker III (KC) stories from midday remain unchanged.
- **Cutdown deadline is Sunday 6pm ET; waiver claims close Monday** — expect the real fantasy-relevant wave of cuts and claims to land Sunday afternoon/evening. Don't finalize bench/waiver rankings until Monday.

## Injury Report

- No new fantasy-relevant injuries since the midday report.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Patriots RB room**: adding Corey Kiner (via trade) behind Stevenson/Henderson signals New England wants a third healthy body heading into cuts — worth a watch if either starter has a camp injury.
- **Steelers RB room**: Kaleb Johnson's future undecided; a release would leave Jaylen Warren as Pittsburgh's clear lead back.

## Rookie Watch

- Nothing new and rookie-specific in this window beyond the ongoing Kaleb Johnson bubble situation (see above).

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Odell Beckham Jr. (WR, NYG) — HC comments suggest he's made the 53.

**Fallers**
- Kaleb Johnson (RB, PIT) — trending toward being waived per beat reporters, unconfirmed.
- Mecole Hardman Jr. (WR, BUF) — released outright.
- Quinn Ewers (QB, MIA→JAX) — traded to a deeper QB depth chart.
