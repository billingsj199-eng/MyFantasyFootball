# Training Camp Report — August 29, 2026 (Midday)

_Roster cutdown to 53 is now roughly 26 hours away (Sunday 6pm ET), so Friday night's finales were the last live look at bubble players. The Falcons' QB race got its final on-field evidence and still isn't resolved._

## Top Storylines

- **Falcons beat the Dolphins 17-12 in the preseason finale, with Tua Tagovailoa going 7-of-8 for 95 yards (116.1 rating) but losing fumbles on back-to-back series** — one on a botched snap, the other when Brian Robinson Jr. fumbled after a Tua handoff/screen sequence. Fantasy takeaway: efficient through the air but the same ball-security issue that's dogged him all camp; Stefanski still hasn't named a Week 1 starter, so don't lock in either Falcon QB yet.
- **Kenneth Walker III's team tag needs a correction: he signed with the Chiefs in free agency back in March (Super Bowl LX MVP with Seattle), not the Seahawks** — our last two reports mislabeled him. He's missed the last two Chiefs practices with ankle swelling that Andy Reid attributes to a shoe issue, not a structural injury. Fantasy takeaway: low concern per Reid, but worth a clean recheck before Week 1 given the missed reps; treat this as a KC RB story going forward, not a Seahawks one.
- **Browns waived WR Cedric Tillman ahead of Thursday's finale** — a 2023 third-rounder who caught 71 passes for 833 yards and 5 TDs over three seasons. Fantasy takeaway: thins Cleveland's WR room further behind Jerry Jeudy; watch the wire, he could resurface elsewhere as a helpful depth piece.
- **Charlie Kolar (Chargers TE) drawing real praise from Justin Herbert**, who said he's excited to get him the ball; Kolar signed a 3-year, $24.3M deal and is listed as a co-starter with David Njoku and rookie Oronde Gadsden on LA's first depth chart. Fantasy takeaway: real breakout-candidate buzz, but a crowded three-TE room caps his target share — deep-league TE2 with spike-week upside, not an every-week starter yet.
- **49ers rookie RB Kaelon Black started Wednesday's finale vs. Las Vegas and ran 4 times for 28 yards (7.0 YPC)** in his last audition before cuts. Fantasy takeaway: closes the preseason with the clearest claim to the McCaffrey-handcuff/RB2 job over an injured Jordan James.
- **Luke McCaffrey (Commanders WR) is genuinely on the roster bubble** — Washington's WR room is deeper this year with Stefon Diggs added and Antonio Williams/Jaylin Lane also in the mix. Fantasy takeaway: even if he makes the 53, expect a fringe-relevance role; not worth a late-round dynasty stash right now.
- **Cutdown deadline is Sunday at 6pm ET, waiver claims close Monday at 1pm ET (10am PT, moved up for the 49ers' Australia trip)** — expect a heavy wave of releases through the weekend. Fantasy takeaway: don't finalize backup/handcuff rankings until Monday afternoon at the earliest.

## Injury Report

- **Kenneth Walker III (RB, KC)** — missed his second straight practice with ankle swelling Reid attributes to a shoe issue, not a structural problem. No long-term concern reported; recheck before Week 1. (Note: he plays for Kansas City, not Seattle — correcting our prior team tag.)
- No other new fantasy-relevant injuries since last night's report.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Falcons QB battle**: Still open after Tua's finale — Stefanski says he'll name a starter "whenever they tell me I have to," with no clear timeline.
- **Chargers TE room**: Kolar, Njoku, and rookie Gadsden all played meaningful snaps on the first unofficial depth chart — a three-way timeshare rather than a clear TE1.
- **49ers RB2**: Kaelon Black holds the edge over Jordan James heading into cuts.
- **Commanders WR depth**: Luke McCaffrey squeezed by Diggs, Antonio Williams, and Jaylin Lane.

## Rookie Watch

- **Kaelon Black (RB, SF)** — 4 carries for 28 yards (7.0 YPC) in his final preseason tune-up; enters cutdown week as the favorite for San Francisco's RB2 job.
- **Oronde Gadsden (TE, LAC)** — part of the three-TE rotation behind Herbert; worth monitoring as camp battles resolve into Week 1 usage.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Kaelon Black (RB, SF) — best and last preseason showing before cuts.
- Charlie Kolar (TE, LAC) — real QB co-sign and breakout buzz, tempered by a crowded room.

**Fallers**
- Tua Tagovailoa (QB, ATL) — clean passing efficiency undercut by two more lost fumbles; QB1 job still unresolved.
- Cedric Tillman (WR, CLE) — waived ahead of the finale, off Cleveland's roster.
- Luke McCaffrey (WR, WAS) — genuinely on the roster bubble with Washington's deeper WR room.
