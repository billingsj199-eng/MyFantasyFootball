# Training Camp Report — August 30, 2026 (End of Day)

_The 53-man roster cutdown deadline passed at 6pm ET today. This report covers the final wave of cuts and trades around the deadline. Waiver claims process Monday at 1pm ET — check the next report for who lands where._

## Top Storylines

- **Cowboys waive Jaydon Blue, Phil Mafah, and Israel Abanikanda — leaving only Javonte Williams and Malik Davis at RB.** Fantasy takeaway: Malik Davis is now Dallas' clear backup and a real stash; Williams' workload is safer than it looked, but Dallas will likely add a body off waivers/PS this week.
- **Commanders trade Brian Robinson Jr. to the 49ers for a 2026 sixth-round pick.** Fantasy takeaway: Washington now runs a committee with Austin Ekeler atop the depth chart over Jeremy McNichols, Chris Rodriguez, and rookie Jacory Croskey-Merritt; in San Francisco, Robinson gives Christian McCaffrey a real early-down/short-yardage complement and tanks Isaac Guerendo's value as a CMC handcuff.
- **Titans waive former starting QB Will Levis.** Fantasy takeaway: no fantasy relevance in Tennessee (Mitchell Trubisky is Cam Ward's backup now), but watch for Levis to land a backup/competition job elsewhere.
- **Broncos waive rookie 5th-round TE Justin Joly** despite an 88-yard TD in the preseason finale. Fantasy takeaway: a real surprise given the draft capital invested — expect Denver to try to re-sign him to the practice squad; devy/rookie dynasty stock takes a hit for now.
- **Kaleb Johnson was traded to Green Bay, not cut** — resolving the "unresolved" status flagged in today's midday report. Fantasy takeaway: buried on Green Bay's depth chart behind the incumbents for now, but worth a speculative add in deep dynasty leagues as a change-of-scenery flier.

## Injury Report

_No new fantasy-relevant injuries beyond what's already tracked — Nabers (NYG, ACL setback), Stowers (PHI, hamstring), Higgins (HOU, torn ACL), Jeanty (LV, ankle sprain), Burden (CHI, groin), and Monangai (CHI, knee) remain unchanged from prior coverage. Today's news was almost entirely roster-cut driven._

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Cowboys backfield**: Down to just Javonte Williams (starter) and Malik Davis (backup) after waiving Blue, Mafah, and Abanikanda in the same afternoon.
- **Commanders backfield**: Austin Ekeler listed atop the depth chart post-Robinson trade, ahead of McNichols, Rodriguez, and Croskey-Merritt — a committee with real touch uncertainty.
- **49ers backfield**: Robinson slots in as a complementary early-down/goal-line piece behind McCaffrey, pushing Isaac Guerendo down the pecking order.
- **Titans QB room**: Cam Ward (starter) and Mitchell Trubisky (backup) is now the full picture after Levis' release.

## Rookie Watch

- **Justin Joly (TE, DEN)** — surprisingly waived after an uneven camp, salvaged only by a garbage-time 88-yard TD; a name to watch for practice-squad or waiver-wire re-emergence.
- **Jacory Croskey-Merritt (RB, WAS)** — 7th-round rookie now firmly in the mix in a crowded backfield after the Robinson trade, though currently behind Ekeler/McNichols/Rodriguez on paper.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Malik Davis (RB, DAL) — surprise ascension to clear backup role after three RBs were waived ahead of him.
- Brian Robinson Jr. (RB, SF) — new role as CMC's early-down/goal-line complement.
- Austin Ekeler (RB, WAS) — moves to the top of a muddled but now Robinson-free depth chart.

**Fallers**
- Jaydon Blue (RB, DAL) / Phil Mafah (RB, DAL) / Israel Abanikanda (RB, DAL) — all waived, out of the Dallas mix for Week 1.
- Isaac Guerendo (RB, SF) — devalued as a CMC handcuff now that Robinson is in the building.
- Justin Joly (TE, DEN) — waived despite draft capital invested.
- Will Levis (QB, TEN) — officially out in Tennessee.
