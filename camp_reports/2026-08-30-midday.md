# Training Camp Report — August 30, 2026 (Midday)

_The 53-man roster cutdown deadline is 6pm ET TODAY, so this window (Saturday night through Sunday midday) is dominated by trades and releases as teams get to 53. Waiver claims don't process until Monday 1pm ET, so nothing on the wire yet — check the end-of-day report for the last wave of cuts before the deadline._

## Top Storylines

- **Dolphins traded WR Tutu Atwell back to the Rams for RB Jarquez Hunter.** Fantasy takeaway: Miami's WR room thins further — watch Douglas and others for a target bump; Hunter is a deep-bench Dolphins flier only.
- **Giants released WR Isaiah Hodgins** in Saturday's cutdown moves. Fantasy takeaway: no direct impact, but confirms Nabers/Slayton/Mooney/Beckham/Fields as the Giants' WR corps.
- **Odell Beckham Jr. is confirmed on the Giants' initial 53-man roster**, closing the loop on Friday's Harbaugh comments. Fantasy takeaway: now a legitimate deep-league WR5/6 flier, not just speculative.
- **Malik Nabers left a recent practice early and won't commit to a Week 1 return.** Fantasy takeaway: red-flag setback in his ACL recovery — treat him as a game-time question for the opener rather than a lock.
- **Raiders released WR Noah Brown** just over a week after signing him. Fantasy takeaway: watch for a landing spot elsewhere as a depth add.
- **Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten is getting 1st-team reps** now that Travis Etienne has been traded to New Orleans. Fantasy takeaway: Coen is praising his contact balance and 4.28 speed — a real breakout sleeper in a Chris Rodriguez Jr. committee.
- **Vikings' backfield remains a genuine committee** — Aaron Jones is "RB1" on paper but Jordan Mason out-snapped him last season and is expected to keep the larger share. Fantasy takeaway: real risk for both in redraft leagues; don't bank on a clear lead back.
- **Eagles rookie TE Eli Stowers is struggling in camp and now dealing with a hamstring injury.** Fantasy takeaway: poor camp as both a receiver and blocker plus a fresh injury clouds his rookie outlook — could open the season on IR.
- **Rookie WR KC Concepcion is reported atop the Browns' WR pecking order.** Fantasy takeaway: his current ADP (~pick 125) may undervalue his role — a dynasty rookie-draft riser to watch.
- **Steelers RB Kaleb Johnson's roster status is still unresolved** heading into the 6pm deadline — no confirmed move either way despite conflicting reports. Fantasy takeaway: don't finalize bench/waiver rankings on him until the deadline passes; recheck in the end-of-day report.
