# Training Camp Report — August 31, 2026 (End of Day)

_Genuinely slow window. The 1pm ET waiver claims deadline produced 29 claims leaguewide, but almost all were non-skill positions (OL/DL/LB/DB/specialists) — a normal, unremarkable cutdown-Monday. No new skill-position injuries surfaced since the midday report._

## Top Storylines

- **Cowboys claim RB Emari Demercado off waivers from the Chiefs.** Fantasy takeaway: slots in as Dallas's clear No. 3 RB behind Javonte Williams and Malik Davis — immediate stash value if either back gets hurt.
- **Jaydon Blue clears waivers, signs with the Eagles' practice squad.** Fantasy takeaway: no path to relevance on Philly's practice squad — drop from stash lists unless he's later elevated.
- **Dolphins claim rookie TE Justin Joly off waivers from the Broncos.** Fantasy takeaway: lands in a thin Miami TE room (Dulcich, Kacmarek, Sims) — worth a depth-stash look in dynasty/TE-premium formats, though no immediate path to relevance.
- **Will Levis clears waivers, hits free agency.** Fantasy takeaway: no relevance now, but worth watching if a QB-needy team signs him as a backup.
- **Waiver deadline overall was a non-event for fantasy.** Fantasy takeaway: the volume of roster churn (29 claims) didn't touch any startable players — nothing else moves the needle from this afternoon's wire.

## Injury Report

_No new fantasy-relevant injuries since this morning's report. Conner (ARI, foot, IR), McNichols (WAS, quad, IR), Nabers (NYG, ACL), Stowers (PHI, hamstring), Higgins (HOU, ACL), Jeanty (LV, ankle), Burden/Monangai (CHI) all remain unchanged._

- **Cowboys backfield fully sorted:** Javonte Williams starts, Malik Davis is the clear backup/handcuff, and new waiver claim Emari Demercado is the No. 3 — Blue, Mafah, and Abanikanda are all off the roster.
- **Dolphins TE room:** Justin Joly joins Greg Dulcich, rookie Will Kacmarek, and Ben Sims — a crowded, low-value group for now.
