# Training Camp Report — August 31, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet news window the day after the 53-man cutdown deadline. Waiver claims from Sunday's cuts are due at 1pm ET today — the biggest fallout (who claims whom, practice squad formations) will land in tonight's report. Today's items are mostly IR/exempt-list paperwork finalized as part of the same cutdown wave that didn't make it into last night's report._

## Top Storylines

- **Josh Jacobs formally placed on the Commissioner's Exempt List.** Fantasy takeaway: he can't practice or play while exempt and his Week 1 status is in real jeopardy — MarShawn Lloyd is now Green Bay's clear starting RB, with Kaleb Johnson (acquired yesterday) as the primary handcuff/change-of-pace piece.
- **James Conner (ARI) officially on IR with a designation to return — must miss at least 4 games.** Fantasy takeaway: earliest return is Week 5 vs. Detroit; with Trey Benson already cut, Jeremiyah Love and Tyler Allgeier now form Arizona's Week 1 committee.
- **Jeremy McNichols (WAS) also placed on IR (quad) with a designation to return — out at least 4 games.** Fantasy takeaway: thins an already crowded Commanders RB room further; Austin Ekeler, Jacory Croskey-Merritt, and Chris Rodriguez are the Week 1 options now.
- **Waiver claims deadline hits 1pm ET today.** Fantasy takeaway: roughly 1,100 players who were cut over the weekend clear or get claimed this afternoon — check tonight's report for who lands where and which practice squads take shape.
