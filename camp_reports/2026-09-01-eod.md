# Training Camp Report — September 1, 2026 (End of Day)

_Quiet day compared to the midday cutdown/trade flurry — mostly Tuesday practice-participation notes as teams start Week 1 prep, plus one skill-position transaction debut. No major new injuries, holdouts, or depth-chart shakeups._

## Top Storylines

- **Bucs' top two WRs (Egbuka, McMillan) both missed Tuesday's practice.** Fantasy takeaway: Egbuka (toe) and McMillan (knee) are both "day-to-day, week-to-week" per Todd Bowles — not alarming individually, but having both banged up at once muddies Tampa Bay's Week 1 WR2/3 outlook.
- **Bengals' Chase and Higgins both limited Tuesday.** Fantasy takeaway: Ja'Marr Chase (knee, hyperextension) and Tee Higgins (heel contusion) were limited, not full — both still on track for Week 1 vs. Tampa Bay per Zac Taylor, but worth a Wednesday/Thursday practice-report check.
- **Mike Evans and Jacob Cowing both sat out 49ers practice.** Fantasy takeaway: Evans (adductor/groin) hopes to return "early next week" per John Lynch; SF opens Sept 10 in Australia, a tight runway worth monitoring given Evans' age and injury history.
- **Alec Pierce back practicing, GM hopeful for Week 1.** Fantasy takeaway: Colts WR (ankle surgery) is trending in the right direction for a Sept 13 opener vs. Baltimore — moves from unknown to probable-ish for deep-league drafters.
- **Kaleb Johnson makes Packers practice debut.** Fantasy takeaway: The newly-acquired RB (traded from Pittsburgh after Josh Jacobs' Exempt List placement) is now on the field behind presumed starter MarShawn Lloyd — a very late-round/waiver stash in deeper leagues.
- **Zay Flowers held out of Monday practice (lower body).** Fantasy takeaway: Ravens are managing him for Week 1 health, not treating it as a real issue — still fully expected to play the Sept 13 opener vs. Indianapolis.
