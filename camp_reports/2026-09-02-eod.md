# Training Camp Report — September 2, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quiet Wednesday of practice reports and status updates rather than fresh injuries — several players trended in a more positive direction than this morning's digest suggested, with one exception (Kyle Monangai) trending the wrong way._

## Top Storylines

- **George Kittle (SF) flying to Australia with the team, "still got a shot" at Week 1.** Fantasy takeaway: More encouraging than last night's GM-voiced "concern" — no setbacks in his Achilles rehab, trending back toward a Week 1 TE1 look vs. the Rams in Melbourne.
- **Emeka Egbuka's (TB) toe sprain diagnosed as "stable."** Fantasy takeaway: Removes the worst-case fear from missing Tuesday's practice — safe to keep him as Tampa Bay's clear WR1 for the opener at Cincinnati.
- **Zay Flowers (BAL) expected back at full practice next week, on track for Week 1.** Fantasy takeaway: The recent quad-related absences look like load management, not a real injury threat — stay confident starting him Week 1 vs. the Colts.
- **Kyle Monangai (CHI) still not practicing, Week 1 status now in "serious doubt."** Fantasy takeaway: Outlook has worsened from the earlier "positive" read on his hyperextended knee — fade him for the opener and watch Roschon Johnson behind D'Andre Swift.
- **Ashton Jeanty (LV) remained out of Wednesday practice.** Fantasy takeaway: Tempers this morning's GM/HC optimism — still trending toward Week 1 vs. Miami, but each missed day nudges up Mike Washington Jr.'s handcuff stock.
