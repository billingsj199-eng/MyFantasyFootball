# Training Camp Report — September 2, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news is quiet — teams are mostly in Week 1 prep mode now that camps have wound down. One IR move for a startable RB and a handful of encouraging injury notes are the headliners._

## Top Storylines

- **Isiah Pacheco placed on IR (MCL sprain + back injury).** Fantasy takeaway: Lions' RB1 will miss at least 4 games, sidelining him for the Sept 13 opener vs. New Orleans and possibly into Week 7 depending on how the Week 6 bye lines up with his return window — bump the backup RB (likely a committee behind him) up draft boards/waiver priority in Lions leagues.
- **Ashton Jeanty (LV) working with trainers, GM voices optimism.** Fantasy takeaway: GM John Spytek said he expects Jeanty back "maybe quicker than some people would expect," and HC Klint Kubiak said the team is "counting on him" for the Sept 13 opener vs. Miami — trending toward Week 1 availability after weeks of vague "on the mend" updates.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) avoided serious injury on ankle tweak.** Fantasy takeaway: DNP Tuesday, but a league source says he should be "good to go" for New England's Week 1 opener at Seattle (Sept 9) — a scare, not a setback.
- **Brian Thomas Jr. (JAX) full-go at Tuesday practice.** Fantasy takeaway: The shoulder scare from last week's joint practice with Tampa Bay is officially a non-issue for Week 1 — full clearance for Jacksonville's presumptive WR1.
- **Dameon Pierce signs to Eagles practice squad.** Fantasy takeaway: Depth-only stash for the deepest dynasty/devy leagues; no path to real touches behind Philadelphia's current backfield.
