# Training Camp Report — September 3, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quieter half-day after a busy midday cycle. The headline is Cincinnati: Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins both sat out Wednesday's practice, though the Bengals still expect both for Week 1. Elsewhere, Christian Kirk landed on IR, Michael Wilson got paid long-term in Arizona, and Malik Nabers, Darren Waller, and J.K. Dobbins all took real steps toward Week 1 roles._

## Top Storylines

- **Ja'Marr Chase (CIN) and Tee Higgins (CIN) both DNP Wednesday** (knee/heel), a step down from Tuesday's "limited." Fantasy takeaway: Not yet a real threat to Week 1 status — Zac Taylor still expects both back — but worth a final practice-report check before the Sept. 13 opener.
- **Christian Kirk (SF) placed on IR with a calf strain, out a minimum of four games.** Fantasy takeaway: Off Week 1-4 boards entirely; opens up incremental target share for San Francisco's other receivers early in the season.
- **Michael Wilson (ARI) signed a 3-year, $75M contract extension.** Fantasy takeaway: Confirms his standing as a long-term piece of Arizona's passing game — steady WR3/4 with target-share security, not a huge Week 1 signal either way.
- **Malik Nabers (NYG) says he's feeling "real good" but still won't commit to playing the Sept. 13 opener vs. Dallas.** Fantasy takeaway: Trending in the right direction off the ACL tear — treat as likely-to-play but not a guaranteed workload yet.
- **Darren Waller (CAR) got first-team-offense reps Wednesday, and HC Dave Canales was encouraged by his route-running.** Fantasy takeaway: Real signal he's trending toward a legitimate Week 1 role as Carolina's starting TE.
- **J.K. Dobbins (DEN) — HC Sean Payton says he's "moving well" and on track for the Sept. 14 opener at Kansas City.** Fantasy takeaway: The soft-tissue scare that limited his camp reps appears resolved; still shares the backfield with RJ Harvey, Tyler Badie, and rookie Jonah Coleman.
- **Nick Bosa (SF) got his first full practice since last year's ACL tear.** Fantasy takeaway: DST/IDP-level note only — no offensive fantasy impact, but a positive sign for San Francisco's Week 1 pass rush in Australia.
