# Training Camp Report — September 3, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news is mostly incremental follow-ups on stories already in motion — the notable new needle-movers are Kyle Monangai's knee getting a firm "multiple weeks" MRI diagnosis, Mike Evans (SF) returning to practice, TreVeyon Henderson (NE) getting cleared for Week 1, and Jeremiyah Love (ARI) getting a "50-50" Week 1 read from NFL Network._

## Top Storylines

- **Kyle Monangai (CHI) MRI confirms hyperextended knee — officially "multiple weeks," Roschon Johnson stepping up.** Fantasy takeaway: Take him off Week 1 boards; Roschon Johnson is now the clear early-down complement to D'Andre Swift and a speculative-add in deeper leagues.
- **Mike Evans (SF) back at practice Wednesday after missing most of camp with a quad/groin/abductor issue.** Fantasy takeaway: Encouraging sign he trends toward a Week 1 role in San Francisco's receiver room — worth a bump off the injury-risk discount.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) "good to go" for the Sept. 9 opener at Seattle, source confirms.** Fantasy takeaway: Relief for Henderson holders, though he opens in a timeshare with Rhamondre Stevenson — treat as a low-end RB2/flex rather than a clear lead back.
- **Jeremiyah Love (ARI) pegged "50-50" for Week 1 by NFL Network's Garafolo on his high-ankle sprain.** Fantasy takeaway: HC LaFleur is encouraged by the progress but non-committal — hold him as a game-time-decision-tier rookie rather than a lock in Week 1 lineups.
- **Emeka Egbuka (TB) walked back from "stable" to HC Todd Bowles calling him "day-to-day, week-to-week" on the toe.** Fantasy takeaway: A mild step back in confidence from yesterday's more upbeat read — still no practice limp reported, but don't consider him fully cleared yet.
- **Rashee Rice (KC) got his first 11-on-11 team-drill reps of camp, taking contact and popping right back up.** Fantasy takeaway: Real progress off the offseason knee procedure — trending toward a full Week 1 route share alongside Worthy.
- **Isiah Pacheco's (DET) IR move now specified as a back injury, not the previously reported knee issue.** Fantasy takeaway: Doesn't change the fantasy math — Jahmyr Gibbs is still the clear bell-cow for at least the first four weeks.
