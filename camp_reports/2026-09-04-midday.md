# Training Camp Report — September 4, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet overnight/morning news cycle — no trades, no season-ending injuries, no holdout resolutions. The main thread is a pair of Thursday practice scares in Chicago (both seem fine) alongside continued positive momentum for George Kittle and Nick Bosa ahead of the 49ers' trip to Australia._

## Top Storylines

- **George Kittle (SF) and Nick Bosa (SF) are officially traveling to Australia for Sunday's opener vs. the Rams.** Fantasy takeaway: Kittle is "trending in the right direction to play" per Rapoport after two clean practice days — real upgrade from Wednesday's more guarded language; still no final call until the team lands.
- **Nick Bosa practiced fully for the first time since his ACL tear.** Fantasy takeaway: IDP/DST-level signal only, but a genuinely positive step in his recovery.
- **D'Andre Swift (CHI) left Thursday's practice in visible discomfort after a goal-line carry.** Fantasy takeaway: Schefter says it was just a cramp — no real concern, but worth a Friday practice-report check before locking him in.
- **Rome Odunze (CHI) also exited Thursday's practice early, hobbling on a leg issue.** Fantasy takeaway: Described as minor ("should be all good" per Chicago Tribune); low-level scare only.
- **Alec Pierce (IND) was a full practice participant Thursday for the first time all summer.** Fantasy takeaway: Real step forward off ankle surgery — Ballard says the hope is he's ready for the Sept. 13 opener at Baltimore.
- **Ashton Jeanty (LV) still has not returned to team practice** (working only on the side since his Aug. 23 ankle sprain), even as the Raiders keep voicing optimism for Week 1 vs. Miami. Fantasy takeaway: Treat as a real yellow flag — team confidence hasn't yet been backed up by practice reps.
