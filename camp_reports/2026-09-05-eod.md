# Training Camp Report — September 5, 2026 (End of Day)

_A quiet Saturday. The two marquee unresolved stories (Nacua conduct review, Jacobs legal situation) remain exactly where they were at midday, but Nacua now has a second, purely physical thread to track, and the Patriots filed their first injury report of the season with a notable ankle absence. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 13._

## Top Storylines

- **Puka Nacua (LAR) picks up a second watch-item** — beyond the still-open NFL conduct-policy review, McVay says the groin/psoas injury Nacua suffered in a joint practice is "making good progress," with Nacua reportedly hitting high-speed reps at a good clip. Fantasy takeaway: two separate paths to a Week 1 absence now (discipline + injury) instead of one — still probable to play, but confirm both before locking him in for the Melbourne opener vs. SF.
- **Josh Jacobs (GB) — no new movement.** Still on the Commissioner's Exempt List with his court appearance Sept. 10; nothing further from the team or league today. Fantasy takeaway: unchanged — Marshawn Lloyd remains the Week 1 lead back in Green Bay until this resolves.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) sits out again as Patriots file season's first injury report** — still hasn't practiced since his Aug. 24 ankle injury; Saturday's session came and went without the return HC Mike Vrabel had floated. Fantasy takeaway: real questionable-for-Wednesday risk now with only two practice windows left before the Seattle opener — Rhamondre Stevenson is the direct beneficiary if Henderson sits.
- **Falcons still haven't named a Week 1 starting QB** — GM Ian Cunningham said "not yet" this week on Tua Tagovailoa vs. Michael Penix Jr., leaving Atlanta as the last team in the league without a named starter days before kickoff. Fantasy takeaway: both remain unstartable/streaming-only until the job is settled — don't bank on either in Week 1 lineups yet.
- **Chase/Higgins (CIN) and Jeanty (LV) — no change** since this morning's digest; both storylines continue on the same trajectories described in the last several reports.
