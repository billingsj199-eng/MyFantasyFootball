# Training Camp Report — September 6, 2026 (End of Day)

_Sunday's biggest fantasy shakeup came out of Santa Clara, where the 49ers' receiver corps took a serious hit — Ricky Pearsall is done for the year and Christian Kirk opens the season on IR, pushing Deebo Samuel into a starting role. Elsewhere, rookie backs Jadarian Price and Jonah Coleman both picked up real buzz, and Cleveland's Quinshon Judkins got a workhorse endorsement from his own coach. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 10-13._

## Top Storylines

- **Ricky Pearsall (SF) is out for the season** — the 49ers WR will have season-ending PCL surgery on his right knee, an injury that flared up again in camp after first occurring last season. Fantasy takeaway: drop him everywhere; this is a notable receiving-corps hit ahead of the opener in Australia vs. the Rams.
- **Christian Kirk (SF) opens the year on injured reserve** — the calf injury that cost him all of preseason is more serious than first believed, and he'll miss at least the first four games. Fantasy takeaway: combined with Pearsall's injury, San Francisco's receiver room is suddenly thin behind Deebo Samuel, Mike Evans, and rookie De'Zhaun Stribling — Samuel's snap share and target volume both jump.
- **Jadarian Price (SEA) is drawing "prime Dalvin Cook" praise** — teammate Julian Love told reporters the rookie's burst reminds him of peak Cook, and with Kenneth Walker III now in Kansas City, Price is Seattle's presumptive early-down back. Fantasy takeaway: he'll see real volume behind an offense replacing 252 touches, though Seattle's stated plan to rotate multiple backs caps some upside.
- **Jonah Coleman (DEN) trending toward a bigger early role** — per ESPN's Dan Graziano, the belief in Denver is that Coleman overtakes RJ Harvey on the depth chart and would be next in line if J.K. Dobbins misses time. Fantasy takeaway: a legitimate stash given Dobbins' extensive injury history — Coleman's third-down/pass-pro chops give him standalone appeal too.
- **Quinshon Judkins (CLE) gets a "sky is the limit" nod from his own position coach** — RB coach Duce Staley says Judkins, back from last December's dislocated ankle/fractured fibula, "can play all three downs" in Todd Monken's offense. Fantasy takeaway: strengthens his case as Cleveland's lead back over Jerome Ford entering Week 1.
- **Dontayvion Wicks (PHI) emerging as Jalen Hurts' No. 2 target** — the receiver Philly acquired in a trade with Green Bay has built real camp chemistry with Hurts, catching his first pass of Saturday's live scrimmage. Fantasy takeaway: worth a speculative add in Eagles-driven leagues as a potential WR2 behind A.J. Brown/DeVonta Smith if his role holds into the season.
- **Tory Horton (SEA) limited Sunday with a hamstring issue** — he was among four Seahawks limited participants ahead of Wednesday's opener, adding a real question mark to his Week 1 workload. Fantasy takeaway: monitor Tuesday's report before trusting him in shallower formats.

- Seahawks: Jadarian Price is the presumptive lead back replacing Kenneth Walker III, though Seattle has signaled a multi-back rotation rather than a true bell cow.
- Broncos: Jonah Coleman is gaining ground on RJ Harvey and would be first in line for lead-back work if J.K. Dobbins misses time.
- Browns: Duce Staley's public "all three downs" comments point to Judkins as the clear lead over Jerome Ford once fully up to speed.
