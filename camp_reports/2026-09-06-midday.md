# Training Camp Report — September 6, 2026 (Midday)

_Sunday morning brought real movement on two backfield committees and a clearer(-ish) signal on the Falcons' QB job, while Nacua's conduct review cleared the highest bar (no Week 1 discipline) even as it stays technically open. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 13._

## Top Storylines

- **Puka Nacua (LAR) avoids Week 1 discipline** — the NFL has not suspended or placed Nacua on paid leave over the personal-conduct review, and he was back at full practice Sunday looking sharp physically. Fantasy takeaway: clear to start him for the Sept. 10 opener vs. SF in Australia, though the league could still act later in the season if its investigation concludes discipline is warranted.
- **Falcons set a real deadline on the QB1 call** — Atlanta has scheduled the starter to speak to media Wednesday, effectively locking in a decision by then, and ESPN's Jeremy Fowler says an announcement could come as early as Monday. Fantasy takeaway: a resolution is finally close — check Monday before setting lineups, but Tua remains the more startable name if Penix (recovering from ACL surgery) isn't tabbed.
- **Cam Skattebo pulls away as Giants RB1** — after opening camp as a co-starter with Tyrone Tracy Jr., Skattebo now sits unchallenged atop New York's unofficial depth chart following a couple of costly Tracy mistakes; Tracy shares the RB2 mix with Najee Harris and Devin Singletary. Fantasy takeaway: Skattebo is trending toward a true bell-cow role — a buy in redraft leagues if his price hasn't caught up.
- **Panthers map out a Brooks/Hubbard committee for Week 1** — coach Dave Canales says the plan is series-based rotation between Chuba Hubbard and rookie Jonathon Brooks (if Hubbard's hamstring is full-go), with Brooks getting extra third-down passing-game work. Fantasy takeaway: muddies both backs' floors — Hubbard remains the lead dog, but Brooks has real standalone value as a pass-catcher.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) still hasn't practiced, but a Boston Globe report says he should be "good to go"** for Wednesday's opener at Seattle despite the extended absence. Fantasy takeaway: still trending game-time-decision territory — Rhamondre Stevenson remains a real handcuff play until Henderson actually returns to practice.
- **Cyrus Allen's (KC, rookie WR) camp buzz keeps translating to ADP** — he was reportedly the most-targeted player in Chiefs minicamp reps and has climbed past Nikko Remigio and Jalen Royals into the WR4 slot in Kansas City's rotation. Fantasy takeaway: a legitimate late-round dynasty/best-ball dart throw given the Mahomes chemistry reports.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- Giants: Cam Skattebo is now the unambiguous RB1, with Tyrone Tracy Jr., Najee Harris, and Devin Singletary rotating behind him.
- Panthers: Jonathon Brooks and Chuba Hubbard are planned as a series-by-series committee for Week 1, assuming Hubbard clears his hamstring — Brooks gets extra passing-down work.
- Falcons: starter decision could be announced as soon as Monday's media availability; Cooper Rush and UDFA Jack Strand remain buried on the depth chart barring multiple injuries.

## Rookie Watch

- Cyrus Allen (WR, KC) — climbing the Chiefs' WR pecking order after standing out as the most-targeted player in camp reps; PPR ADP has surged from off-the-board in July into the low 220s.
- Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR) — earned the Week 1 committee role with Hubbard after a strong, healthy close to camp roughly 18 months removed from his second ACL surgery.

## Risers / Fallers

**Risers**
- Cam Skattebo (RB, NYG) — clear RB1 now, no longer sharing the top line with Tracy.
- Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR) — locked into a real Week 1 role in Carolina's committee.
- Cyrus Allen (WR, KC) — continued ADP climb on camp buzz and Mahomes reps.

**Fallers**
- Tyrone Tracy Jr. (RB, NYG) — slides to a timeshare with Najee Harris and Devin Singletary after losing the RB1 job outright.
