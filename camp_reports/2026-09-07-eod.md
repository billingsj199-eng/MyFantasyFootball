# Training Camp Report — September 7, 2026 (End of Day)

_Labor Day practice reports rolled in from every corner of the league as teams enter their final Week 1 prep. Biggest theme: receiver health swung both ways — Cincinnati got Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins back, while Rome Odunze's and Malik Nabers' situations both got murkier. The 49ers began their trip to Melbourne without Christian McCaffrey or Nick Bosa practicing. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 10-13._

## Top Storylines

- **Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins (CIN) both return to practice** — Chase (knee) and Higgins (heel) were back at Monday's session, with Zac Taylor "encouraged" by both. Fantasy takeaway: both trending toward a normal Week 1 workload vs. Tampa Bay after a scare-filled camp.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) outlook slides the wrong direction** — still hasn't practiced since Aug. 24 with an ankle injury; HC Mike Vrabel was noncommittal Monday ("we'll see how he feels"). Fantasy takeaway: trending toward missing Wednesday's opener at Seattle — have Rhamondre Stevenson/Corey Kiner as the standby plan.
- **Christian McCaffrey and Nick Bosa sit out 49ers' first Australia practice** — neither took part in team drills as San Francisco began Week 1 prep in Melbourne; Bosa insists he'll play. Fantasy takeaway: likely just workload management with the opener still days out, but worth confirming on Wednesday's injury report.
- **Malik Nabers (NYG) injury update turns cryptic** — the Giants WR's latest comments on his ACL recovery raised more questions than answers, with no clearance confirmed yet. Fantasy takeaway: treat as a game-time-decision risk heading into the Dallas opener rather than a lock.
- **Puka Nacua (LAR) gets firm Week 1 green light** — Sean McVay confirmed he's expected available for Thursday's opener vs. the 49ers in Melbourne, with the psoas issue no longer a real concern. Fantasy takeaway: start with confidence.
- **Rome Odunze (CHI) injury described as "concerning"** — the Bears WR, who exited practice hobbling last week, got an update Monday that left his Week 1 status murkier; official word won't come until Wednesday's injury report. Fantasy takeaway: monitor closely before locking him into lineups.
- **Titans LB Cedric Gray in concussion protocol after UTV accident** — not a fantasy-relevant position, but worth knowing for Tennessee's defensive outlook; his season-opener availability is in question.
