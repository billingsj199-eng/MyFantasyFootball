# Training Camp Report — September 7, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet Labor Day morning news-wise, but one big domino fell: the Falcons finally named Tua Tagovailoa their Week 1 starter over Michael Penix Jr., who will be inactive Wednesday. Elsewhere it was mostly injury-report bookkeeping two days out from the opener — Zach Charbonnet officially opens the year on Reserve/PUP, George Kittle is trending toward playing in Australia, and Ashton Jeanty remains a game-time watch. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 10-13._

## Top Storylines

- **Tua Tagovailoa (ATL) named Week 1 starter** — the Falcons ended their QB competition, going with Tagovailoa over Michael Penix Jr. for Wednesday's opener vs. Pittsburgh. Fantasy takeaway: locks him in as a streaming option with Drake London, Bijan Robinson, and Darnell Mooney around him.
- **Michael Penix Jr. (ATL) ruled inactive for Week 1** — coach Kevin Stefanski confirmed Penix, still working back from his ACL, won't be active Wednesday; Cooper Rush is the backup. Fantasy takeaway: off the board entirely for Week 1, with no clear timeline to reclaim snaps.
- **Zach Charbonnet (SEA) officially opens the season on Reserve/PUP** — still recovering from his January ACL tear, he'll miss at least the first four games. Fantasy takeaway: removes any lingering doubt about Seattle's backfield — Jadarian Price is the clear early-down starter to open the year.
- **George Kittle (SF) trending toward a Week 1 return** — expected to come off the PUP list before Wednesday's trip to Australia, with a target return date of Sept. 10 vs. the Rams. Fantasy takeaway: firmer positive signal than last week's cautious optimism; worth trusting in lineups if he's confirmed active.
- **Ashton Jeanty (LV) still not practicing, but optimism holds** — the Raiders rookie was seen doing side work with trainers Monday (his first sighting on the field since the ankle injury), and HC Klint Kubiak said the team is "counting on him" for the opener vs. Miami. Fantasy takeaway: still a game-time-decision risk; check Tuesday and Wednesday injury designations before locking him in.
- **Panthers confirm Hubbard/Brooks Week 1 committee, with Hubbard fully healthy** — Chuba Hubbard is past his hamstring issue and "ahead of schedule," while Jonathon Brooks is resting through minor soreness but expected available. Fantasy takeaway: no change to the split-backfield outlook from earlier in camp — treat both as touch-capped RB2/3 options in Week 1.
