# Training Camp Report — September 8, 2026 (End of Day)

_Tuesday night brought the first official Week 1 injury/depth-chart paperwork of the season. TreVeyon Henderson is officially out for Wednesday's opener, Malik Nabers got a more encouraging signal on his Week 1 chances, and Rome Odunze's situation got a clearer (if still unresolved) explanation. Seattle's first injury report of the year exposed real safety depth issues, and Las Vegas's first depth chart confirmed Ashton Jeanty at RB1 and rookie Fernando Mendoza as Kirk Cousins' backup. Regular season opens Wednesday, Sept. 9 (Patriots at Seahawks), with the rest of Week 1 following Sept. 10-13._

## Top Storylines

- **TreVeyon Henderson (NE) officially ruled out for Wednesday's opener** — DNP at all three Patriots practices this week on his ankle injury. Fantasy takeaway: start Rhamondre Stevenson with confidence as the clear early-down/goal-line lead in Week 1.
- **Malik Nabers (NYG) gets a more positive signal** — ESPN's Dan Graziano reported "it sounds like [Nabers is] going to be good to go" for the Giants' opener, a step up from his own noncommittal comments earlier this week. Fantasy takeaway: still no official word, but trending toward a confident Week 1 start vs. Dallas.
- **Rome Odunze's (CHI) situation gets more specific, still unresolved** — HC Ben Johnson clarified the leg issue is "something different," not a reaggravation of last year's foot injury, and called him "more day-to-day" with hope he's ready for Sunday vs. Carolina. Fantasy takeaway: still can't be trusted as a confident start until Chicago's first injury report drops.
- **Seahawks' first Week 1 injury report exposes safety depth** — Ty Okada (hamstring) is out, and Nick Emmanwori (ankle, returning from offseason surgery) and Tory Horton (hamstring) are both questionable for Wednesday. Fantasy takeaway: no direct offensive fantasy impact, but worth knowing if you roster any Seahawks IDP; Horton's questionable tag is the one to track for best-ball/deep-league flex spots.
- **Raiders' first Week 1 depth chart locks in the backfield and QB pecking order** — Ashton Jeanty is listed as the starting RB1 despite still not being a full practice participant, and rookie Fernando Mendoza is confirmed as Kirk Cousins' direct backup (QB2) over Scott O'Connell. Fantasy takeaway: Jeanty's depth-chart placement is a good sign for Week 1 availability vs. Miami (Sept. 13); Mendoza is a dynasty stash if Cousins misses time.
- **Pat Bryant (DEN) confirmed at WR4, not the breakout some expected** — Denver's first Week 1 depth chart lists Courtland Sutton, Jaylen Waddle, and Marvin Mims Jr. all ahead of Bryant, despite earlier camp buzz he could pass Sutton. Fantasy takeaway: bench-stash only in Week 1 redraft leagues; the target competition is more crowded than the hype suggested.
