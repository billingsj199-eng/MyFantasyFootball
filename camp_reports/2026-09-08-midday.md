# Training Camp Report — September 8, 2026 (Midday)

_Final Tuesday practices before Week 1 kicks off Wednesday (Patriots @ Seahawks). The 49ers had their best participation numbers yet in Melbourne, Malik Nabers took a step in the right direction after Monday's cryptic comments, and TreVeyon Henderson's Week 1 outlook kept sliding the wrong way. Also: three rookie WRs (Malachi Fields, Carnell Tate) and a passing-down RB (Kenneth Gainwell) are carving out real Week 1 roles._

## Top Storylines

- **49ers get (almost) everyone back at Tuesday's Melbourne practice** — Christian McCaffrey, Nick Bosa, and George Kittle all practiced after sitting out Monday's session; only a hamstring-limited rookie DT was absent. Fantasy takeaway: no real injury concern for CMC or Kittle — full speed ahead for Friday's opener vs. the Rams.
- **TreVeyon Henderson's Week 1 outlook keeps sliding** — DNP again Tuesday, and beat writers are now calling his availability "extremely unlikely" for Wednesday's opener, though he did travel with the team to Seattle. Fantasy takeaway: start Rhamondre Stevenson with confidence; Corey Kiner is the change-of-pace flier.
- **Malik Nabers takes a step forward after Monday's uncertainty** — worked in most of Tuesday's team reps, and HC John Harbaugh confirmed he was out there "start to finish," walking back some of Monday's more cautious tone. Fantasy takeaway: bump him back toward a confident Week 1 start vs. Dallas, though nothing is officially locked until Wednesday's injury report.
- **Malachi Fields (NYG rookie WR) penciled in as Week 1 WR2** — the Notre Dame product the Giants traded up for in Round 3 has jumped Darnell Mooney, Calvin Austin III, and Odell Beckham Jr. on the depth chart. Fantasy takeaway: streamable in deeper/dynasty leagues behind Nabers.
- **Carnell Tate (TEN rookie WR) closes camp with the inside track on WR1** — beat out Wan'Dale Robinson and Calvin Ridley for the top role in Tennessee's offense after a strong finish to camp. Fantasy takeaway: rookie WR3/4 dart throw with real target-share upside catching passes from Cam Ward.
- **Kenneth Gainwell (TB) carving out a real passing-down role** — Baker Mayfield says he's been "completely overwhelmed" with Gainwell's talent; he's settling in as the 1B to Bucky Irving's 1A. Fantasy takeaway: PPR flex/handcuff value even without the early-down work.
- **Rome Odunze (CHI) status still unresolved** — no fresh update since Monday's "concerning" report; Chicago won't issue its first Week 1 injury report until Wednesday. Fantasy takeaway: still can't be trusted as a confident start — check Wednesday's report before locking lineups.
