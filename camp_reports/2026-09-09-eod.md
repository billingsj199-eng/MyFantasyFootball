# Training Camp Report — September 9, 2026 (End of Day)

_The season kicked off tonight with Patriots-Seahawks in Seattle, and it delivered the day's biggest fantasy news: Sam Darnold left in the first quarter with a hip injury. Elsewhere, Brock Bowers had a minor knee procedure that will cost him time, Khalil Shakir is trending toward playing Week 1, and Baker Mayfield got paid._

## Top Storylines

- **Sam Darnold (hip) ruled out for the rest of the Week 1 opener vs. the Patriots** — Sacked by Dre'Mont Jones on Seattle's first series, Darnold was evaluated in the blue medical tent and taken to the locker room for X-rays; the Seahawks ruled him out for the remainder of the game with New England leading. Fantasy takeaway: Drew Lock (6/8, 51 yards in relief so far) is Seattle's QB the rest of tonight, and Darnold's severity/timeline is unknown heading into Sunday — a real risk to fantasy managers starting him or Seattle pass-catchers next week.
- **Brock Bowers underwent a knee procedure Tuesday, expected to miss "a game or two"** — Per Schefter, Bowers had a meniscus trim after getting banged up late in camp; the Raiders hope to have him back for Week 3 vs. New Orleans. Fantasy takeaway: bench Bowers for at least Week 1, and add Michael Mayer as the streaming/handcuff option in Vegas' offense.
- **Baker Mayfield signs 3-year, $165M extension with the Buccaneers** — The deal, announced days before Tampa Bay's opener vs. Cincinnati, is the largest contract in franchise history and runs through 2029. Fantasy takeaway: no snap or role change, but it locks in Mayfield as Tampa's unquestioned starter and stabilizes his supporting cast (Evans, Egbuka, etc.) as long-term fantasy assets.
- **Khalil Shakir trending toward playing Week 1** — Bills' first injury report of the week had him back at practice progressing from a high ankle sprain, with the team optimistic he'll suit up Sunday vs. Houston. Fantasy takeaway: still just a WR4/flex with DJ Moore now in the mix, but worth rostering as depth if he clears the final practice report.
