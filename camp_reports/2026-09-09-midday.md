# Training Camp Report — September 9, 2026 (Midday)

_Opening day. The Seahawks host the Patriots tonight (8:35 PM ET, Netflix) to kick off the 2026 season, with the 49ers-Rams Melbourne opener following Thursday night. News is thin ahead of tonight's kickoff — most rosters are locked and yesterday's Week 1 storylines (Henderson out, Nabers/Odunze trending toward playing) are unchanged. The two real developments: Patrick Mahomes is officially cleared to start Monday night after his ACL/LCL tear, and the 49ers took a second significant injury blow in Melbourne._

## Top Storylines

- **Patrick Mahomes officially confirmed as Chiefs' Week 1 starter** — Andy Reid confirmed Mahomes will start Monday night vs. Denver, roughly nine months removed from a torn ACL and LCL, and will play without limitations after working exclusively with the first-team offense all camp. Fantasy takeaway: no lingering setback reported — start him with confidence as a top-tier QB1 in his return.
- **49ers DT Alfred Collins out for the season (torn patellar tendon)** — Suffered the injury at Tuesday's practice in Melbourne while the team prepares for Thursday's opener vs. the Rams; it's the 49ers' second season-ending D-line loss (along with DE Sam Okuayinonu) before Week 1 even kicks off. Fantasy takeaway: no offensive fantasy impact, but a real hit to San Francisco's front seven — worth knowing if you roster the 49ers DST.
- **George Kittle full-go despite lingering groin issue** — Kittle said he feels "wonderful" and traveled with the team to Melbourne for Thursday's opener, though GM John Lynch has voiced some concern about the groin injury that limited his practice reps late in camp. Fantasy takeaway: trust him as a top-5 TE Thursday night barring a pregame change.
- **Zach Charbonnet's runway comes into focus** — Now officially confirmed out for at least Seattle's first four games on Reserve/PUP, Charbonnet can begin practicing as soon as Week 3 and is eligible to return Week 5 vs. San Francisco. Fantasy takeaway: gives Jadarian Price (tonight's Week 1 starter) a clear four-game runway as the lead back before any competition returns.
