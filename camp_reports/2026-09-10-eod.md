# Training Camp Report — September 10, 2026 (End of Day)

_Thursday brought firmer numbers on two of the day's biggest injury stories (A.J. Brown, Sam Darnold), a legal-system update that puts Josh Jacobs' Week 1 availability in doubt, a fresh injury scare for newly-named Falcons starter Tua Tagovailoa, and an in-progress TNF opener in Melbourne (Rams-49ers) that cost the 49ers a promising rookie WR to a re-aggravated ankle injury._

## Top Storylines

- **A.J. Brown's MRI is in: high-ankle sprain, out at least 4 weeks** — Firms up Thursday morning's "2-6 week" guess into a real number. Fantasy takeaway: bench him outright; Patriots' passing game is a committee (Kayshon Boutte, Kyle Williams, Stefon Diggs) until at least Week 6.
- **Sam Darnold gets good news: muscle strain, not joint damage** — HC Mike Macdonald says Seattle "dodged a bullet" after further imaging on the hip injury from Wednesday's opener. Fantasy takeaway: still expected to sit Week 2 at Arizona, but Rapoport says the absence "could be just a few weeks" rather than a long-term IR stay — Drew Lock is a streamer, not a season-long QB1.
- **Josh Jacobs pleads no contest to misdemeanor battery/property-damage charges** — Stemming from a May domestic incident; the NFL is expected to remove him from the commissioner's exempt list and impose a suspension. Fantasy takeaway: his Week 1 (and beyond) availability is now genuinely in question — hold but don't rely on him until a games-missed number comes down.
- **Tua Tagovailoa grabs his back at Thursday practice, oblique injury, limited; Cooper Rush ran with the 1s** — Comes just days after Atlanta named him the Week 1 starter over Michael Penix Jr. Fantasy takeaway: real question mark for Sunday at Pittsburgh — don't lock in Falcons pass-catchers until Friday's report.
- **De'Zhaun Stribling (rookie WR, SF) carted off in tonight's Melbourne opener vs. the Rams** — Left with a non-contact left-ankle injury, tried to return ~20 minutes later, went down again on his first route back and was ruled out. Fantasy takeaway: a rookie who'd been drawing "best 49ers WR in camp" buzz now carries real injury risk into the season.
- **Puka Nacua active and playing through a psoas injury in the same Melbourne game** — McVay cleared him pregame; no setback reported as the game played out. Fantasy takeaway: treat as a full-workload WR1, but keep an eye on the next injury report just in case.
- **Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR) limited Wednesday with groin soreness, but HC Dave Canales is "very confident" he plays Sunday** — Would be his first game action since Week 14 of the 2024 season. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward a role in his long-awaited debut, but expect a touch cap early.
- **Malik Nabers logs a second straight full practice; HC John Harbaugh: "I think he's ready to go"** — Still no official confirmation for Sunday night vs. Dallas. Fantasy takeaway: trending firmly toward a return from his ACL tear — have a Plan B ready until he's active on the final report.
- **Dontayvion Wicks (WR, GB) doubtful for Saturday vs. Minnesota with a concussion** — Did not practice all week. Fantasy takeaway: expect him out — bumps Romeo Doubs and Matthew Golden up the Packers' Week 1 target hierarchy.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Falcons**: Cooper Rush took every first-team rep in team drills Thursday after Tua left with his oblique injury — worth tracking into Friday in case Atlanta needs a Plan B at QB1 for Week 1.
- **Packers**: With Wicks doubtful (concussion) for Saturday vs. Minnesota, expect an uptick in early-down/red-zone work for Romeo Doubs and rookie Matthew Golden.
- **Patriots**: A.J. Brown's 4+ week absence pushes New England's passing-game volume toward Stefon Diggs, Kayshon Boutte, and Kyle Williams.

## Rookie Watch

- **Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR)** — Not a 2026 rookie, but making his long-delayed NFL debut Sunday after the 2024 ACL tear; groin soreness limited him Wednesday but coaches are confident he suits up.
