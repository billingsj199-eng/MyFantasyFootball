# Training Camp Report — September 10, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning follow-up on Wednesday's Week 1 opener (Seahawks 13, Patriots 10) plus fresh injury reports as the rest of Week 1 sets its practice slates. Biggest headline: A.J. Brown suffered a high-ankle sprain and is headed for an MRI. Sam Darnold avoided a fracture but has no clear return timetable. Jaxon Smith-Njigba torched New England even with a backup QB. Several Week 1 questionable tags (Chase, Jeanty, Love, Stowers) are trending toward clearer answers._

## Top Storylines

- **A.J. Brown (ankle) has a confirmed high-ankle sprain, MRI scheduled Thursday** — X-rays came back negative for a fracture, but reports peg this as a likely 2-6 week injury. Fantasy takeaway: New England doesn't play again until 9/20 vs. Pittsburgh, so there's real time to heal — watch for an IR designation; downgrade the Pats' passing game short-term.
- **Sam Darnold: fracture ruled out, but no timetable set** — Further imaging after last night's hip injury confirmed no break, but Seattle says more tests are needed. Fantasy takeaway: Drew Lock should be treated as the presumptive starter until Darnold is cleared; Seattle pass-catchers carry added uncertainty into Week 2.
- **Jaxon Smith-Njigba stayed dominant even with Drew Lock throwing** — 8 catches on 11 targets for 122 yards and a 45-yard touchdown in the Week 1 win over New England. Fantasy takeaway: reigning OPOTY form held up through a QB change mid-game — an elite WR1 regardless of who's under center in Seattle.
- **Ja'Marr Chase trending toward a full Week 1 workload** — Limited in practice on a hyperextended knee, but Bengals HC Zac Taylor said he expects both Chase and Tee Higgins available for the Sept. 13 opener. Fantasy takeaway: safe to start Chase as a top overall WR1.
- **Ashton Jeanty gets a confident nod from his head coach** — Raiders' Klint Kubiak gave a one-word "yes" when asked if he's optimistic Jeanty (ankle) plays Week 1 vs. Miami; the sprain is now described as low-ankle, not high. Fantasy takeaway: trending in the right direction, but he's still not practicing — check Friday's final injury report before locking him in as an every-down RB1.
- **Jeremiyah Love upgraded from "50-50" to "tracking toward playing"** — The Cardinals rookie's high-ankle sprain outlook improved ahead of Sunday's game vs. the Chargers. Fantasy takeaway: trending up, but treat him as a high-end RB2 rather than a bell-cow workload in his Week 1 debut.
- **Eli Stowers (hamstring) back at limited practice, but buried on the depth chart** — The Eagles rookie TE is behind Dallas Goedert, Johnny Mundt, and E.J. Jenkins even when healthy. Fantasy takeaway: not a Week 1 factor even if he's active.
- **Denzel Boston (rookie WR, CLE) locking down the "X" role and red-zone targets in camp** — Coach Todd Monken has compared his catch radius to a Mike Evans type. Fantasy takeaway: real role, but Cleveland's shaky QB situation caps the immediate upside — deep-league stash only.
