# Training Camp Report — September 11, 2026 (End of Day)

_Friday's final Week 1 injury reports brought real clarity: Tua Tagovailoa is officially OUT (Cooper Rush starts), Brock Bowers is a surprise scratch after knee surgery, and Jonathon Brooks cleared completely. Elsewhere, Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins and Malik Nabers all trended toward playing, while Jalen McMillan looks like a Sunday scratch for Tampa Bay._

## Top Storylines

- **Tua Tagovailoa officially OUT vs. Steelers; Cooper Rush starts for Atlanta** — Upgrades from Friday midday's "TBD" to a confirmed absence. Fantasy takeaway: fade the Falcons' passing-game pieces in Week 1 and treat Rush as streaming-only.
- **Brock Bowers ruled out after a knee meniscus trim, expected to miss 1-2 games** — A real surprise; the procedure wasn't reported until this week. Fantasy takeaway: bench him for the Dolphins matchup and monitor the Raiders' TE snaps as a short-term proxy.
- **Jonathon Brooks completely clears the injury report, "no limitations" vs. Bears** — Full turnaround from Wednesday/Thursday's groin-limited practices. Fantasy takeaway: he reclaims full early-down/passing-down work, which caps Chuba Hubbard's Week 1 ceiling.
- **Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins both "on track" for Sunday vs. Buccaneers** — Zac Taylor confirmed no real hedge on either the knee (Chase) or heel (Higgins). Fantasy takeaway: lock both in as normal-workload starters.
- **Malik Nabers trending toward his season debut vs. Cowboys** — Full practice participant Wednesday coming back from a torn ACL; coach says "he's ready to go" but final call still pending. Fantasy takeaway: start him with confidence, but check Sunday's inactive list first.
- **Alvin Kamara (MCL sprain) questionable after a full Friday practice** — Progressing faster than the original 4-6 week outlook. Fantasy takeaway: have a Saints backfield contingency queued as a likely game-time call.
- **Jalen McMillan (knee) doubtful for Buccaneers vs. Bengals** — Opens a real snap window in Tampa's WR room. Fantasy takeaway: rookie Ted Hurst III and Tez Johnson both get a bump if he sits.
- **Jeremiyah Love cleared for Week 1 as Cardinals RB2 behind Tyler Allgeier** — Ankle scare resolved after being "50/50" earlier in the week. Fantasy takeaway: expect real receiving work in a likely trailing, pass-heavy script vs. the Chargers — comfortable flex play.
- **Rome Odunze (calf) officially questionable but expects to play at Carolina** — "I'm feeling ready to go," per Odunze; Ben Johnson still cautious. Fantasy takeaway: no change from midday's lean-to-start call, confirm active status pregame.
