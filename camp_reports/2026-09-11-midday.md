# Training Camp Report — September 11, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet Friday morning-into-midday news cycle: no new season-altering injuries, but a genuine surprise out of Thursday night's box score (a rookie RB cutting into Christian McCaffrey's touches), incremental clarity on Josh Jacobs' discipline, good news on De'Zhaun Stribling's Achilles, and Breece Hall shedding his groin-injury question marks. Tua Tagovailoa's Week 1 status is still unresolved as of midday._

## Top Storylines

- **Rookie Kaelon Black out-carries Christian McCaffrey 14-10 in the 49ers' Thursday win over the Rams** — The 3rd-round Indiana product ran for 65 yards (4.6 YPC) while CMC managed 68 total yards from scrimmage on a lighter workload. Fantasy takeaway: don't panic-sell McCaffrey, but this bears watching into Week 2 — Black is now a real speculative add.
- **Josh Jacobs stays on the commissioner's exempt list; no suspension timeline announced** — Despite Thursday's no-contest plea, the NFL says nothing has changed — he still can't practice or play. Fantasy takeaway: continue to hold-and-wait; there's no games-missed number to plan around yet.
- **De'Zhaun Stribling's Achilles confirmed intact; ankle MRI still pending** — Kyle Shanahan ruled out the worst fear from Thursday's cart-off. Fantasy takeaway: real, if lesser, relief for a rookie who'd drawn "best 49ers WR in camp" buzz — still no timeline until the MRI reads out.
- **Breece Hall (groin) full practice again Friday, calls the missed time a "good vacation"** — No lingering hedge from the Jets. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a normal-workload RB1 at Tennessee Sunday.
- **Tua Tagovailoa still without an official Friday designation** — Rapoport says his oblique-strain status remains "TBD" for Sunday at Pittsburgh as of midday. Fantasy takeaway: don't lock in Falcons pass-catchers (or Tua himself) until Atlanta's final report drops.
- **Rome Odunze trending toward playing, calf "not serious" per Chicago's own read** — An upgrade in tone from earlier in the week. Fantasy takeaway: lean back toward starting him vs. Carolina, final check still recommended.
