# Training Camp Report — September 12, 2026 (End of Day)

_Saturday night into Sunday-morning news ahead of the full Week 1 slate. Biggest swing: Dallas' backup RB spot changed hands overnight after a season-ending injury. Several Friday "questionable" tags got real texture — a calf diagnosis for Odunze, fresh optimism on Kamara, and a new game-time-decision framing for Nabers._

## Top Storylines

- **Cowboys RB Malik Davis out for the season after hip surgery, night before the opener** — Dallas placed him on IR and promoted Israel Abanikanda from the practice squad. Fantasy takeaway: Javonte Williams remains the lead back; Abanikanda is now the direct Dallas backup to speculatively stash.
- **Raiders TE Michael Mayer signs 3-year, $45M extension, locked in as Week 1 starter for injured Brock Bowers** — Fantasy takeaway: streamable TE1 volume in Vegas' opener vs. Miami while Bowers is out.
- **Rome Odunze's Week 1 issue specified as a calf injury; limited all three practices, still not formally cleared** — He says he plans to play, but Chicago hadn't signed off as of Saturday night. Fantasy takeaway: keep Luther Burden III and Colston Loveland as your contingency plan if Odunze sits.
- **Bears' Loveland and Burden both see their stock climb** — With Chicago's WR corps banged up, Loveland profiles as a premier weekly TE option and Burden as a top-10 WR play if Odunze is out. Fantasy takeaway: both are strong plays regardless, better if Odunze sits.
- **Alvin Kamara reportedly "two weeks ahead of schedule" on his MCL, per insider Nick Underhill** — Counters Friday's practice-squad-RB-elevation pessimism. Fantasy takeaway: still treat as a game-time risk; Travis Etienne Jr. remains the floor play vs. Detroit.
- **Malik Nabers now framed as a straight "game-time decision" (ESPN's Fowler); Giants sign WR Dalen Cambre to the 53-man roster** — Fantasy takeaway: the roster move reads as insurance against a Nabers absence — have Wan'Dale Robinson ready as the hedge.
- **Ashton Jeanty gets a confident "expecting to have all our guys ready" from HC Kubiak, no formal injury tag** — Fantasy takeaway: start him with confidence in the Miami opener.
- **Jeremiyah Love (ARI) listed questionable on the final injury report; call may not come until ~90 minutes before kickoff** — Fantasy takeaway: trending to play, but keep Trey Benson/Emari Demercado in your back pocket for lineup locks.
