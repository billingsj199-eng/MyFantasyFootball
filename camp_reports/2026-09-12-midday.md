# Training Camp Report — September 12, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet Saturday morning by design — teams locked their Friday injury designations before this window opened, so most Week 1 news is incremental. The one real wrinkle: New Orleans elevated a practice-squad RB, adding fresh doubt to Alvin Kamara's already-questionable status. Everything else from Friday's report (Tua/Bowers OUT, Chase/Higgins/Nabers/Love/Brooks trending to play, McMillan doubtful, Odunze questionable) is unchanged — see yesterday's end-of-day digest for details._

## Top Storylines

- **Saints elevate practice-squad RB C.J. Donaldson ahead of Kamara's questionable game-time call** — New Orleans wouldn't need the extra body if they were fully confident in Kamara's MCL. Fantasy takeaway: treat Kamara as a real game-time risk vs. the Lions; Travis Etienne Jr. is the clean lead-role floor play regardless of Kamara's status.

Fantasy takeaway: no change to his questionable tag, but the hedge is a notch more cautious than Friday's "trending to play" framing — have a Giants WR contingency plan ready.
- **Eagles rookie TE Eli Stowers (hamstring) questionable, says "the plan" is to play** — Limited practice participant who missed the preseason finale. Fantasy takeaway: streaming-only relevance behind Dallas Goedert, but worth a late check if you're desperate at TE.
