# Training Camp Report — September 13, 2026 (End of Day)

_Week 1 Sunday is in the books (Giants-Cowboys SNF still in progress as of this report). Biggest developments since midday: George Kittle left the 49ers' win with a hamstring injury (expected out ~4 weeks), Kyler Murray was knocked out of his Vikings debut with a concussion, Malik Nabers is active and playing in his return from ACL surgery, and Ashton Jeanty and Jeremiyah Love both delivered strong rookie/injury-scare-be-damned performances._

## Top Storylines

- **George Kittle exits 49ers' 17-13 win over Seattle with a hamstring injury** — Had 4 catches for 25 yards and a TD before leaving; reports point to at least a 4-week absence (targeting Week 6 at Tampa Bay). Fantasy takeaway: bench Kittle for the next month and grab backup TE Jake Tonges, who caught the game-winning score in relief.
- **Kyler Murray knocked out of Vikings debut with a concussion** — Hit late in the 1st quarter vs. Green Bay; Carson Wentz rallied Minnesota from a 22-10 deficit to a 39-22 win. Fantasy takeaway: Murray is in the concussion protocol into Week 2 — know Wentz as the direct streamer/backup if he's held out.
- **Malik Nabers active for his first game back from a torn ACL** — Playing tonight vs. Dallas; New York's Cam Skattebo opened the game with a 10-play, 83-yard TD drive. Game still in progress as of this report — final stat lines TBD, check tomorrow's report for the recap.
- **Ashton Jeanty shakes off his ankle injury with a monster Raiders debut** — 23 carries for 102 yards, all 6 targets caught for 45 yards, 2 total TDs in a 27-13 win over Miami. Fantasy takeaway: erases the injury concern entirely — a clear workhorse RB1 role heading into Week 2.
- **Jeremiyah Love scores on his very first career touch in Cardinals' 26-14 upset of the Chargers** — 5-yard rushing TD on the opening drive despite starting behind Tyler Allgeier; 3rd RB since 2015 to score a rushing TD on his career-opening drive. Fantasy takeaway: priority waiver add — monitor the Allgeier/Love split closely into Week 2.
- **Caleb Williams erupts for 2 pass TDs + 2 rush TDs in Bears' 59-37 rout of Carolina** — First 65-plus rushing yard, multi-rush-TD game of his career; Rome Odunze played but saw only 3 targets (2-52) in the blowout script. Fantasy takeaway: Williams is a streaming QB1 to watch; Odunze's volume was game-script capped, not injury-related.
- **Derrick Henry runs for 144 yards and 3 TDs in Ravens' 41-23 win over Indianapolis** — Passed Marcus Allen for 3rd all-time in career rushing TDs. Fantasy takeaway: still a clear-cut lead-back RB1 entering his age-32 season.
- **Ja'Marr Chase held to just 2 catches for 12 yards on 4 targets in Bengals' 33-27 win** — His lowest yardage game since Nov. 2023, despite no injury designation; Cincinnati leaned on Chase Brown (16 carries, 56 yards, 1 TD) and defense/turnovers. Fantasy takeaway: not an injury concern, a game-flow blip — don't panic-sell Chase off one game.
- **Josh Allen and Dalton Kincaid power Bills past Houston, 36-31** — Allen: 334 pass yards + 4 total TDs (2 rushing); Kincaid: 5 catches for 130 yards, most by a Bills player in a Week 1 game since 1999. Fantasy takeaway: Kincaid is an every-week TE1 target on current usage.
- **Bijan Robinson piles up 173 total yards and a TD but fumbles twice in a 20-13 loss to Pittsburgh** — 21 carries/83 yards + 8 catches/90 yards/1 TD, accounting for the vast majority of Atlanta's offense. Fantasy takeaway: elite workhorse volume remains, but the fumbles are worth a watch-list flag, not a bench decision.
