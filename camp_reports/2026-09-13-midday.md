# Training Camp Report — September 13, 2026 (Midday)

_Week 1's early games are about to kick off. Biggest news since last night: Alvin Kamara is now confirmed OUT for the opener (not just a maybe), A.J. Brown suffered a high-ankle sprain in Wednesday's opener and is week-to-week, and 49ers rookie WR De'Zhaun Stribling is out at least a month. Odunze and Chase both trending toward playing; Nabers remains a true game-time call for tonight's SNF opener._

## Top Storylines

- **Alvin Kamara officially NOT expected to play vs. Lions** — Despite a full Friday practice, Rapoport reports the Saints are holding him out; he's now targeting a Week 2 debut at Baltimore. Fantasy takeaway: bench Kamara everywhere, start Travis Etienne Jr.-caliber alternatives; new Saints backfield timeshare (Kendre Miller/Devin Neal) will need in-game monitoring.
- **A.J. Brown (now with the Patriots) suffers high ankle sprain in Wednesday's season-opening loss to Seattle** — X-rays negative for fracture, but he's in a boot and expected to miss time. Fantasy takeaway: drop Brown from Week 1-2 lineups; Stefon Diggs and Hunter Henry see a bump in target competition relief for New England.
- **49ers rookie WR De'Zhaun Stribling out at least a month with a deltoid ankle sprain** — Hurt in the first half of SF's Week 1 win over the Rams in Australia; surgery possible but not currently planned. Fantasy takeaway: a real rookie stash evaporates for a month; expect more Ricky Pearsall/Jauan Jennings volume in San Francisco's offense in the interim.
- **Sam Darnold (Seahawks) exits opener with unusual hip muscle strain** — No fracture, no immediate IR plans, but he's expected to miss time, possibly into their Week 3 game vs. Washington given the extra rest. Fantasy takeaway: bench Darnold and any Seattle pass-catchers dependent on him until a backup/return plan is clear.
- **Rome Odunze trending to play vs. Panthers** — Rapoport reports Odunze is expected to go despite the calf/questionable tag; he says "I'm feeling ready to go... I plan on playing," but Chicago has final sign-off. Fantasy takeaway: still monitor pregame inactives — if he's out, Luther Burden III and Colston Loveland remain the direct beneficiaries.
- **Malik Nabers still a true game-time decision for tonight's SNF opener vs. Cowboys** — Nabers says the call is his to make ("I'll let God tell me") and has stressed that a full week of practice doesn't guarantee he plays. Fantasy takeaway: have Wan'Dale Robinson queued as the emergency Giants WR play until lineups lock tonight.
- **Ja'Marr Chase carries no injury designation into Sunday vs. Buccaneers** — Full practice participant Wed/Thu, rested Friday for maintenance rather than his knee. Fantasy takeaway: start Chase with confidence in his usual role.
- **Cowboys rookie safety Caleb Downs projected for heavy slot-corner usage in NFL debut** — Downs saw the bulk of his camp reps in the slot, with Malik Hooker/Jalen Thompson holding down the safety spots in base looks. Fantasy takeaway: IDP-only relevance, no standard-format fantasy impact.
- **Carnell Tate (Titans rookie WR) off the injury report, good to go vs. Jets** — Favorable Week 1 matchup for his debut, but a quiet, pass-catch-optional preseason tempers expectations. Fantasy takeaway: streamable flex in deeper leagues, not a confident season-opening starter.
