# Training Camp Report — September 14, 2026 (End of Day)

_Monday brought injury follow-ups from Week 1's full slate plus Mahomes' season debut on MNF, still in progress as of this report (Chiefs led Broncos 21-7 in the 3rd quarter). Zay Flowers got a clean MRI, Ja'Kobi Lane's wrist is worse than first thought, and rookies Caleb Douglas and Kenyon Sadiq both delivered notable NFL debuts._

## Top Storylines

- **Mahomes' return is off to a strong start on MNF** — 15-yard rushing TD plus a passing score, Chiefs up 21-7 on the Broncos through three quarters as of this report. Fantasy takeaway: no signs of the ACL/LCL tear slowing him down; Rashee Rice (TD catch-and-run) looks like his clear top target again.
- **Zay Flowers' MRI comes back clean** — Rapoport reports the hamstring injury isn't considered serious and he remains day-to-day. Fantasy takeaway: keep him in Week 2 lineups pending Wednesday's practice report.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane's wrist fracture may be worse than initially believed** — Ravens HC Jesse Minter says the rookie WR will get second and third opinions this week. Fantasy takeaway: expected to miss at least Week 2 vs. New Orleans; not a lineup factor short-term.
- **Ladd McConkey still has no official diagnosis** — Reported as a rib/chest injury from Sunday's hit; Chargers have given no timeline. Fantasy takeaway: treat as a real Week 2 risk until a clearer update arrives.
- **Dylan Sampson's knee severity still unknown** — Browns' top pass-catching back and kick returner was on crutches in a brace after Week 1. Fantasy takeaway: monitor closely before trusting him in Week 2 lineups.
- **Jalen Coker breaks out then shows up in a walking boot** — 8 catches, 138 yards, 2 TDs on 9 targets in Sunday's loss to the Bears, but seen in a boot Monday. Fantasy takeaway: he says it's minor and expects to play Week 2 vs. Atlanta — priority waiver add regardless.
- **Brock Bowers upgraded to day-to-day** — Kubiak says he's "working his tail off" to return from his knee procedure. Fantasy takeaway: real chance to play Week 2 vs. the Chargers; worth stashing if the roster spot is available.
- **Kyler Murray's Week 2 status uncertain** — Still in concussion protocol; felt "pretty good" Monday but no clearance yet. Fantasy takeaway: have Carson Wentz rostered as the direct streamer if Murray can't go at Chicago.
- **Rookie debuts: Caleb Douglas and Kenyon Sadiq both flash** — Douglas set a Dolphins rookie-debut record with 94 yards (5/7 targets); Sadiq scored on a jet sweep on the Jets' opening drive despite a hernia-shortened camp. Fantasy takeaway: both worth Week 2 waiver looks in their respective offenses.
