# Training Camp Report — September 14, 2026 (Midday)

_The last piece of Week 1's Sunday slate is now final, and Monday morning brought the fallout: fresh injuries to Zay Flowers and Ladd McConkey, a signature Trey McBride outing, and Isaiah Likely's explosive Giants debut alongside Malik Nabers and Jaxson Dart. Tonight's Chiefs-Broncos MNF caps Week 1, with Patrick Mahomes officially clear to make his return from ACL/LCL surgery._

## Top Storylines

- **Giants outlast Cowboys 28-20 on SNF** — Jaxson Dart was sharp (23/29, 230 yards, 3 TD passing plus 54 rush yards), Cam Skattebo ran for 81 yards and a TD on 18 carries, and Isaiah Likely exploded in his Giants debut. Fantasy takeaway: New York's offense looks completely retooled under John Harbaugh — Dart, Skattebo, Likely and Nabers are all in play weekly.
- **Isaiah Likely erupts in Giants debut: 8 catches on 8 targets for 78 yards and 2 TDs** — His first game since following Harbaugh from Baltimore on a 3-year deal. Fantasy takeaway: instant must-start TE1 with clear target trust from Dart in the red zone.
- **Malik Nabers' ACL comeback is clean** — 6 catches for 69 yards on 9 targets in his first game since tearing his ACL last September; no setback reported. Fantasy takeaway: back to normal WR1 role and volume for the Giants.
- **Zay Flowers exits Ravens' 41-23 win with a hamstring injury** — Left in the 2nd quarter after torching Indy for 5 catches, 150 yards (career high) and a TD on 6 targets. Considered day-to-day with an MRI pending; per Rapoport he likely would've returned in a playoff game. Fantasy takeaway: monitor his practice status closely, but this reads more like a minor tweak than a multi-week absence.
- **Ladd McConkey exits Chargers' loss with a chest injury, no return** — Left in the 3rd quarter of the 26-14 loss to Arizona after going 5-82-1 on 7 targets. HC Jim Harbaugh had no postgame update. Fantasy takeaway: real watch-list flag heading into practice reports this week — treat as a game-time risk until further word.
- **Trey McBride: 9 catches on 12 targets for 95 yards and a TD** in Arizona's 26-14 upset of the Chargers, hauling in Jacoby Brissett's only TD pass. Fantasy takeaway: locked-in elite TE1 regardless of who's at QB for the Cardinals.
- **Chig Okonkwo exits his Commanders debut with a hamstring injury** — Just 2 catches for 16 yards on 3 targets before checking out against Philadelphia. Fantasy takeaway: John Bates is the next man up at TE in Washington if Okonkwo misses time.
- **Parker Washington leads Jaguars' Week 1 win** — 5 catches, 83 yards and a 30-yard TD from Trevor Lawrence, pacing Jacksonville in every receiving category. Fantasy takeaway: emerging as a real weekly WR3/flex option if the target share holds.
- **Patrick Mahomes cleared with no injury designation for tonight's MNF vs. Denver** — Full practice week completed, 9 months removed from a torn ACL/LCL. Fantasy takeaway: locked-in QB1 for his season debut; Chris Jones is also expected to play through a calf issue.
