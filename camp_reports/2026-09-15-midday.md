# Training Camp Report — September 15, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning update following Monday's full slate. Headline: Mahomes' MNF debut closed as a 31-10 Chiefs rout, with Kenneth Walker III (now a Chief, not a Bronco — correcting last night's report) running away with the game. Elsewhere, McConkey's injury got a diagnosis, Sampson's is still pending, and the 49ers' backfield picture keeps getting more interesting._

## Top Storylines

- **Mahomes' MNF debut ends in a blowout** — Final: 15/27, 184 yards, 2 pass TD, 1 INT, plus a 23-yard rushing TD as the Chiefs beat the Broncos 31-10. Fantasy takeaway: full workload, no visible rust or limitations off the ACL/LCL tear.
- **Correction: Kenneth Walker III's monster game was for Kansas City, not Denver** — He signed with the Chiefs in free agency this offseason (not a trade). Final line: 23 carries for 173 yards and a TD, plus 3 catches for 18 yards and a receiving TD. Fantasy takeaway: workhorse usage in a run-heavy offense built to protect Mahomes' knee — a real weekly RB1 if this volume holds.
- **Ladd McConkey's injury identified as a rib injury** — Schefter/Rapoport report he's day-to-day per HC Jim Harbaugh; Chargers want to see how he feels in practice before setting his Week 2 status. Fantasy takeaway: real risk for Sunday at Las Vegas — track practice participation Wed–Fri.
- **Dylan Sampson "improving daily," but no firm diagnosis yet** — Browns HC Kevin Stefanski says a fuller update on the knee comes Wednesday. Fantasy takeaway: don't bench-and-forget, but don't trust him in lineups until Wednesday's news.
- **Kyler Murray still in concussion protocol** — Took part in Monday meetings and film review, "feeling pretty good," but no clearance yet for Week 2 at Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: keep Carson Wentz rostered as the direct streamer if Murray can't go.
- **De'Zhaun Stribling (rookie WR, SF) out at least a month** — Deltoid ligament sprain in his ankle from Week 1; surgery not currently planned but still being evaluated. Fantasy takeaway: not rosterable short-term; opens more 49ers WR snaps behind the top two.
- **Tua Tagovailoa trending questionable for Week 2 vs. Carolina** — Oblique strain kept him out Week 1; Rapoport notes oblique injuries are tricky for QBs given throwing mechanics. Fantasy takeaway: don't trust Dolphins pass-catchers until Wednesday's practice report.
- **Kaelon Black looks like the clear 49ers RB2** — Out-carried Christian McCaffrey 14-10 in Week 1 (28 snaps/13 routes to CMC's 35/22), with Jordan James a healthy scratch. Fantasy takeaway: priority Week 2 waiver add, both as a standalone flex piece and CMC-injury insurance.
- **Devaughn Vele quietly the Saints' clear WR2** — 7 catches, 69 yards, 1 TD on 9 targets in Week 1 behind Chris Olave; role grows further with rookie Jordyn Tyson on IR. Fantasy takeaway: streamable PPR flex in Tyler Shough's high-volume passing offense.
- **Myles Garrett (Rams) headed to IR for knee surgery** — Out a minimum of 4 games, earliest return Week 6 vs. Arizona; the knee had limited him through camp. Fantasy takeaway: IDP-only relevance, but a real blow to the Rams' pass rush.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **49ers backfield**: Kaelon Black (28 snaps, 13 routes, 14 carries/65 yds) essentially split work with McCaffrey (35 snaps, 22 routes, 10 carries/68 yds) in Week 1, with Jordan James inactive. Some of that split reportedly tied to McCaffrey cramping — worth watching whether it's a real committee or a one-week blip.
- **Chiefs backfield**: Walker's 23 carries dwarfed any other KC back; Rashee Rice remains Mahomes' clear top target after his MNF score.
- **Eagles O-line**: Dickerson (knee) to IR; expect Drew Kendall or another interior lineman at LG — a downgrade worth monitoring for Saquon Barkley's efficiency.
- **Saints WR corps**: With Jordyn Tyson (hamstring) on IR into October, Vele is locked in as the clear WR2 behind Olave in a pass-heavy Shough offense.
