# Training Camp Report — September 16, 2026 (End of Day)

_Wednesday practice-report day wrapped with one real bombshell (A.J. Brown's IR timeline got worse, not better) and a batch of confirmations on names already on your radar from midday: Murray took an encouraging step forward, Flowers/McConkey stayed off the practice field as expected, and Bowers got a firmer, more optimistic Week 2 outlook._

## Top Storylines

- **A.J. Brown officially on IR — and the timeline just got worse.** Patriots WR has a high-ankle sprain that will avoid surgery, but Ian Rapoport now says it's a 6-week absence (not the ~4 weeks first reported), with a possible Week 8 return vs. Miami. Fantasy takeaway: drop him from active lineups in shorter-IR formats; he misses Steelers, Jaguars, Bills, Raiders, Jets and Bears.
- **Nico Collins' Week 2 outlook turns shaky.** The Texans WR is having tests done on a hamstring tweaked at Wednesday practice, just days after his 67-yard TD grab vs. Buffalo. DeMeco Ryans called him "week-to-week." Fantasy takeaway: real DNP risk building for Sunday vs. Cincinnati — have a contingency (Woody Marks/Nico backup, streaming WR) ready.
- **Kyler Murray takes a real step forward.** Vikings QB returned to a limited practice Wednesday in concussion protocol; Kevin O'Connell wants him medically cleared by Friday to start Sunday at Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: more confident than midday's "trending" report — treat him as a probable Week 2 starter, but Carson Wentz is still the direct handcuff if Friday's session goes sideways.
- **Brock Bowers' Week 2 outlook brightens.** Raiders TE won't practice Wednesday but Klint Kubiak hopes he can go Thursday/Friday; ESPN's Dan Graziano says the knee (post-meniscus-trim) is "not overly serious." Fantasy takeaway: monitor Thu/Fri practice reports — a Week 2 return vs. the Chargers is realistically in play.
- **Joe Burrow's back scare downplayed.** Bengals QB was limited Wednesday with back tightness from Sunday's win over Tampa Bay but firmly said he'll play Sunday vs. Houston ("that's ridiculous" when asked about missing the game). Fantasy takeaway: no lineup changes needed, but worth a glance at Friday's final report.
- **Flowers and McConkey both sit out Wednesday, as expected.** Zay Flowers (hamstring) and Ladd McConkey (rib) were both non-participants; neither situation has changed materially from what was known at midday — both remain day-to-day heading into Thursday/Friday sessions.
