# Training Camp Report — September 16, 2026 (Midday)

_Wednesday injury-report day. The four names left pending from last night — McConkey, Murray, Tua, Sampson — moved: Sampson's now officially on IR, Murray got a genuinely hopeful update, Tua's situation is still murky but with a new wrinkle (Penix possibly back Week 2 or 3), and Zay Flowers' outlook got worse even though the injury itself isn't serious._

## Top Storylines

- **Dylan Sampson officially lands on IR** — Browns placed him on IR Tuesday for the sprained knee suffered in Week 1, out a minimum of four games; Jaleel McLaughlin signed off the practice squad to fill the roster spot. Fantasy takeaway: Quinshon Judkins is now the unquestioned lead back in Cleveland for at least a month — a real add/start in all formats.
- **Zay Flowers' Week 2 outlook downgraded despite a "not serious" injury** — Adam Schefter said playing Sunday vs. New Orleans looks "unrealistic" for the hamstring even though Ian Rapoport reported it isn't long-term. Fantasy takeaway: plan for a hold-out; monitor Thursday/Friday practice before trusting him in lineups.
- **Kyler Murray trending toward playing Week 2** — Mike Garafolo reported the Vikings haven't ruled him out; Murray arrived at the facility "feeling pretty good" Monday, sat in meetings, and watched film without symptoms worsening. Fantasy takeaway: more optimistic than last night's update — still needs to clear protocol, but Carson Wentz is no longer the leans-favorite.
- **Tua's Week 2 stays murky, but Penix's return timeline surfaces** — Falcons still have "no updates" on Tua as of Monday; Ian Rapoport says Michael Penix Jr. (ACL) may be ready as soon as Week 2 or 3, and Cooper Rush remains the trending starter vs. Carolina. Fantasy takeaway: don't bank on any Falcons QB for points this week — treat the passing game as a coin flip on volume.
- **Ladd McConkey still unresolved** — Harbaugh reiterated Wednesday-Friday practice participation will decide his Sunday (at Las Vegas) availability; no clearer signal yet. Fantasy takeaway: check back Thursday/Friday before locking him into lineups.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane (BAL rookie WR) set for wrist surgery** — Expected to miss about a month. Fantasy takeaway: no fantasy relevance himself, but confirms the Ravens' WR room (already missing Flowers) is thin this week.
