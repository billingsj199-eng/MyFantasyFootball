# Training Camp Report — September 17, 2026 (End of Day)

_Thursday's biggest developments: TNF kicked off with Josh Allen torching the Lions early at the new Highmark Stadium, Nico Collins' hamstring got a firm (if mild) diagnosis, and Friday is shaping up as the decision day for Kyler Murray, Zay Flowers, and the Falcons' QB situation. Kaelon Black — now the 49ers' clear lead back after McCaffrey's IR move — popped up on the injury report himself with a groin issue._

## Top Storylines

- **Josh Allen lighting up the new Highmark Stadium on TNF.** Bills QB had 3 total TDs (1 rush, 2 pass) and the Bills led Detroit 21-7 by the second quarter; game was still in progress at file time. Fantasy takeaway: another lock-in QB1 week for Allen — check final box score Friday morning for the full stat line and which pass-catchers cashed in (Kincaid and Knox both drew looks).
- **Nico Collins' hamstring gets a diagnosis: Grade 1 strain.** Texans WR was DNP Thursday; team doesn't consider it serious, but he could still miss 1-3 games. Fantasy takeaway: moves from "murky" to a real questionable tag for Sunday vs. Cincinnati — have a contingency ready through Friday's report.
- **Falcons' Week 2 QB battle still unresolved — Rush favored.** Tua Tagovailoa (oblique) hasn't practiced all week and is unlikely to play; Michael Penix Jr. got a full practice but isn't considered ready to start; Cooper Rush is the likely starter vs. Carolina, final call Friday. Fantasy takeaway: fade Falcons passing-game pieces regardless of who starts.
- **Kaelon Black added to the injury report — groin, DNP/limited Thursday.** Notable given he's now the 49ers' presumptive lead back with McCaffrey on IR; Shanahan called it minor ("being smart" with him). Fantasy takeaway: not alarming yet, but a name to watch closely on Friday's report given his suddenly critical role.
- **TreVeyon Henderson declares himself ready for his Week 2 debut.** Patriots rookie RB was a full practice participant again Thursday and told reporters he's "ready to go" vs. Pittsburgh. Fantasy takeaway: about as confirmed as a Week 2 debut gets before the official Friday report — flex/handcuff stock stays up.
- **Zay Flowers' tone softens to "day-to-day."** Ravens WR was a DNP again Thursday, but HC Jesse Minter backed off the more pessimistic "unrealistic for Week 2" framing from Wednesday. Fantasy takeaway: a limited Friday practice would meaningfully raise his chances of playing — worth checking one more time before locking in a Sunday decision.
- **Kyler Murray: Friday is the decision day.** Vikings QB remained limited Thursday in concussion protocol; HC Kevin O'Connell explicitly named Friday as when he'll know if Murray clears for Sunday at Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: no change in status, but confirms Wentz remains the direct hedge if Friday goes sideways.
- **Ed Oliver ruled out pregame with a hip injury.** Bills DT wasn't on the injury report all week before going down in warmups ahead of TNF. Fantasy takeaway: IDP/DST-only relevance — marginally softer Bills front vs. Detroit.
