# Training Camp Report — September 17, 2026 (Midday)

_The 49ers backfield took the biggest hit of the young week: Christian McCaffrey landed on IR (calf/Achilles, out at least 4 games) hours after his own primary backup situation at the position got murkier elsewhere — Vikings RB Jordan Mason also went to IR (fractured thumb, needed surgery). Elsewhere, Wednesday's injury storylines mostly held serve into Thursday: Bowers trending up, Henderson trending up, Nico Collins/Murray still pending._

## Top Storylines

- **Christian McCaffrey placed on IR — out at least 4 games.** 49ers RB is dealing with a calf/Achilles issue that never fully resolved; San Francisco made it official Thursday. Fantasy takeaway: rookie 3rd-rounder Kaelon Black, who already out-touched McCaffrey in Week 1, is now the 49ers' clear lead back — priority wire add/start in all formats.
- **Jordan Mason also headed to IR.** Vikings RB fractured his thumb (surgery already performed), expected to miss at least 4 weeks. Fantasy takeaway: Aaron Jones Sr. is now Minnesota's unquestioned early-down back; Vikings elevated DeeJay Dallas and signed Jerome Ford to the practice squad as thin depth behind him.
- **Brock Bowers trending toward a Week 2 return.** Raiders TE (knee, post-meniscus-trim) didn't practice Thursday, but HC Pete Carroll said he expects Bowers to play Week 2 vs. the Chargers. Fantasy takeaway: more confidence than Wednesday's "not overly serious" line — plan to start him barring a setback on Friday's report.
- **TreVeyon Henderson trending up for Week 2.** Patriots rookie RB (ankle) returned to full practice participation Wednesday after missing the opener; on track to make his season debut vs. the Steelers. Fantasy takeaway: Rhamondre Stevenson keeps the starting job, but Henderson should carve out passing-down/space touches immediately — a flex stash worth activating.
- **Nico Collins' Week 2 outlook still murky.** Texans WR underwent hamstring testing after Wednesday's tweak; ESPN says the early read isn't a serious long-term issue, but Sunday vs. Cincinnati is still in real jeopardy. Fantasy takeaway: no resolution yet — have a Plan B ready until Friday's report.
- **Kyler Murray still not fully cleared.** Vikings QB remained limited Wednesday in concussion protocol; HC Kevin O'Connell still wants him at phase 5 by Friday to start Sunday at Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: no change from last night — Carson Wentz remains the direct handcuff if Friday goes sideways.
