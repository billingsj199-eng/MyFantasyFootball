# Training Camp Report — September 18, 2026 (End of Day)

_Friday's practice/status reports for Week 2: Tua Tagovailoa (doubtful) joins Michael Penix Jr. (out) on Atlanta's sideline, Nico Collins and Chig Okonkwo are ruled out, Kyler Murray did NOT clear concussion protocol, Puka Nacua popped up with a new hip issue, and Ladd McConkey took a real step toward playing. Rookies Antonio Williams and Jadarian Price both trending up._

## Top Storylines

- **Kyler Murray did not clear concussion protocol — officially OUT for Sunday at Chicago.** Despite midday reports of positive momentum, the Vikings did not clear him; Carson Wentz starts, with a good chance Murray returns Week 3 at Tampa Bay. Fantasy takeaway: Wentz is a two-week streamer bridge at best — don't chase the Vikings passing game until Murray's back.
- **Michael Penix Jr. ruled out again (knee); Tua Tagovailoa now doubtful (oblique) too.** Cooper Rush starts for a second straight week vs. Carolina with both Atlanta QBs sidelined. Fantasy takeaway: keep expectations modest for every Falcons skill player until one of the two returns.
- **Nico Collins ruled out for Week 2 vs. Cincinnati (Grade 1 hamstring strain).** Didn't practice Thursday or Friday; team doesn't believe it's serious but Week 3 is uncertain too. Fantasy takeaway: a real Houston passing-game hit — expect a bump in target share for the Texans' No. 2/3 WRs and increased checkdown work to the backs.
- **Puka Nacua added to the injury report with a hip issue, DNP Friday.** Missing practice put his Monday night availability vs. the Giants in real question, though the Monday game gives him extra recovery time before a final call. Fantasy takeaway: a name to track closely through the weekend — start him with a contingency plan in place.
- **Zay Flowers trending toward sitting out Sunday vs. New Orleans (hamstring).** Returned to practice Friday but did very little in the viewing period; considered doubtful. Fantasy takeaway: expect DeAndre Hopkins and Rashod Bateman to see extra volume in the Ravens' passing game if Flowers sits.
- **Ladd McConkey took a real step forward, back at Friday practice after two straight DNPs.** Now considered questionable (up from worse) for Sunday vs. the Raiders. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward a green light — check pregame inactives before locking lineups.
- **Brock Bowers returned to limited practice Friday but is doubtful for Monday night vs. the Chargers.** Raiders will make a pregame call; coach Pete Carroll is optimistic Bowers plays Week 3 vs. New Orleans regardless. Fantasy takeaway: still bench him with Michael Mayer as the streaming alternative.
- **Chig Okonkwo ruled out vs. Dallas (hamstring), didn't log a single practice this week.** Ben Sinnott and John Bates step into expanded TE snaps for Washington. Fantasy takeaway: neither is a startable option, but Sinnott is the priority if Okonkwo's absence stretches into multiple weeks.
- **Rookie WR Antonio Williams (WAS) named NFL's Week 1 Rookie of the Week.** Went 4-for-4 on targets for 64 yards and a TD on just 10 routes — an NFL-best 2.36 EPA/target. Fantasy takeaway: still a limited route-runner (29% route rate) but a name to watch for a snap-share bump, especially with Okonkwo out this week.
- **Seahawks committing to rookie RB Jadarian Price as the early-down lead back.** After a 5.2 YPC Week 1 (10 carries, 52 yards), coaches are signaling they want to feed him more in Week 2 vs. Arizona. Fantasy takeaway: an RB2/FLEX riser as Seattle leans into the rookie over George Holani.
