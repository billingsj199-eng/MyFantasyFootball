# Training Camp Report — September 18, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning developments since Thursday's TNF: Josh Allen's final line is in (5 total TDs, 41-31 win), DJ Moore left that same game with a shoulder injury, Sam Darnold is officially out for Week 2 (Drew Lock starts), and both Cooper Kupp and Trey McBride cleared their injury scares with full Thursday practices. Brock Bowers' outlook softened slightly — still hasn't practiced this week._

## Top Storylines

- **Josh Allen's TNF stat line is final: 5 total TDs in a 41-31 win.** 20-of-31 for 248 yards and 3 pass TDs, plus 69 rush yards and 2 rush TDs, as the Bills christened new Highmark Stadium. Fantasy takeaway: another top-3 fantasy QB week — check Kincaid's and Knox's target shares before locking your Week 3 TE plans.
- **DJ Moore banged up in that same Bills win.** Left with a shoulder injury believed to be an AC joint sprain; not considered serious, but his Week 3 status vs. the Chargers is in doubt. Fantasy takeaway: a name to monitor next week, no Week 2 impact since Buffalo is on a normal schedule.
- **Sam Darnold officially ruled out for Week 2 vs. Arizona — Drew Lock starts.** Glute injury, expected out roughly 3-4 weeks with a chance to return Week 3. Jalen Milroe is the backup. Fantasy takeaway: real QB downgrade in Seattle, but Jaxon Smith-Njigba's elite target share should hold up regardless of who's throwing.
- **Cooper Kupp and Trey McBride both cleared their injury scares.** Kupp (back) and McBride (back/rest) were each upgraded to full practice Thursday after appearing on Wednesday's report. Fantasy takeaway: both are full-workload starts for Sunday's Seahawks-Cardinals game — no lingering concern.
- **Kyler Murray trending positively in concussion protocol.** Did more in Thursday's practice than Wednesday's, and the Vikings reportedly expect him to clear by Friday. Fantasy takeaway: momentum toward starting Sunday at Chicago, but Carson Wentz stays on standby until it's official.
- **Brock Bowers' Week 2 outlook softens.** Still hasn't practiced since his Week 1 knee procedure; DNP again Friday, with the team now saying he's only "hoping" to play Monday night vs. the Chargers (down from earlier "expects to play" language). Fantasy takeaway: keep Michael Mayer rostered as the priority streaming/handcuff option until Bowers actually practices.
- **Omar Cooper Jr. ruled out for Week 2 with an ankle sprain.** Jets rookie WR is considered week-to-week, possibly up to 4 weeks. Fantasy takeaway: Isaiah Williams is the direct beneficiary for extra Jets WR snaps Sunday vs. Green Bay.
- **Jacory Croskey-Merritt is Washington's clear early backfield leader.** 16 carries for 63 yards and a TD in the Week 1 win over Philadelphia, well ahead of Rachaad White (7 carries) and rookie Kaytron Allen (4 carries). Fantasy takeaway: worth a speculative add in a crowded but Croskey-Merritt-led RB room.
