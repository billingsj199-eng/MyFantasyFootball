# Training Camp Report — September 19, 2026 (End of Day)

_Saturday's final injury reports brought real movement on three names that were still up in the air at midday: Michael Pittman Jr. and Zay Flowers both got downgraded from questionable to OUT, while Brock Bowers trended the other way with a strong Saturday practice. Puka Nacua remains the closest thing to a game-time decision heading into Monday night._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Pittman Jr. (PIT) downgraded to OUT vs. New England**, closing the book on the questionable tag from three straight DNPs. Fantasy takeaway: he's off the board for Week 2 — pivot to the depth chart change below.
- **Rookie Germie Bernard (PIT) is in line for a real Week 2 role with Pittman out**, working as the WR3/slot option after just 3 snaps in Week 1. Fantasy takeaway: a priority streaming/waiver add in a plus matchup vs. a Patriots defense that struggled in the opener.
- **Zay Flowers (BAL) downgraded to OUT vs. New Orleans**, worse than the "trending doubtful" read from midday. Fantasy takeaway: Rashod Bateman becomes the clear top target in Baltimore's passing game — start with confidence.
- **Puka Nacua (LAR) missed practice for a second straight day and stays questionable for Monday night vs. the Giants.** McVay clarified the injury is groin soreness (not the core-muscle issue from August) despite the "hip" tag on the official report. Fantasy takeaway: still trending toward a game-time decision — have Davante Adams/Tutu Atwell plans ready as a hedge.
- **Brock Bowers (LV) turned in a strong Saturday practice** — Pete Carroll called him "great" and said he did "really well," a clear step up from Thursday's DNP. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward playing Monday night vs. the Chargers despite an official doubtful/questionable tag — worth starting with a pregame check.
- **Joe Burrow (CIN) is confirmed full-go** after a full Friday practice; Zac Taylor said it would be "a major surprise" if he doesn't play Sunday vs. Houston. Fantasy takeaway: no real change from midday — start him as a normal-workload QB1.
- **Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ) has officially been placed on IR** with his ankle sprain, confirming a minimum four-game absence. Fantasy takeaway: Isaiah Williams keeps the extra Jets WR snaps through at least mid-October.
