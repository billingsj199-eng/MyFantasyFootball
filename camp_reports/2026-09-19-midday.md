# Training Camp Report — September 19, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet Saturday, as expected the day before Week 2 kicks off. The main movement: three new questionable tags (Michael Pittman Jr., Chris Olave, RJ Harvey) that weren't yet on the injury report as of Friday's end-of-day scan, Joe Burrow's back designation firming up after a full Friday practice, and Week 1 usage data tilting the Steelers' and Broncos' backfield timeshares._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Pittman Jr. (PIT) is questionable for Sunday vs. New England after three straight DNPs with a foot injury.** He was limited Wednesday, then sat out Thursday and Friday entirely. Fantasy takeaway: real chance he's inactive — have a Steelers-WR contingency plan ready.
- **Chris Olave (NO) is officially questionable vs. Baltimore with a hamstring issue that popped up Thursday.** Reports call it precautionary and he's trending toward playing. Fantasy takeaway: keep him in your lineup but check inactives 90 minutes before kickoff.
- **Joe Burrow (CIN) practiced in full Friday and is expected to start vs. Houston** despite an official questionable tag for back tightness. Zac Taylor called it a formality ("He practiced in full today. He feels good."). Fantasy takeaway: start him as a full-strength QB1.
- **RJ Harvey (DEN) is questionable vs. Jacksonville, limited in practice for a second straight day with a hamstring issue.** If he sits, J.K. Dobbins would see a real bump in early-down work. Fantasy takeaway: Dobbins is a priority handcuff/flex stream this week if Harvey's ruled out.
- **Steelers backfield tilting toward Jaylen Warren for Week 2.** Warren out-touched Rico Dowdle 16-10 (including 6 targets) in Week 1 despite fewer snaps, and is now the slight fantasy favorite in a plus matchup vs. a Patriots run defense that struggled in the opener. Fantasy takeaway: Warren's the better PPR play; Dowdle slides to more of a matchup/flex option.
- **Puka Nacua (LAR) situation still unresolved ahead of Monday night vs. the Giants** — backup Jordan Whittington also missed Friday practice (quad), and the Rams say more clarity is expected Saturday. Fantasy takeaway: no change yet from Friday's DNP news, but this is the closest thing to a "must-monitor" name through the weekend.
