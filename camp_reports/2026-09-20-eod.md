# Training Camp Report — September 20, 2026 (End of Day)

_A brutal injury day overshadowed the on-field action: Caleb Williams and Jayden Reed both left games in concerning fashion, and Denver's backfield lost both RJ Harvey and J.K. Dobbins in the same afternoon. Meanwhile Monday night's slate got clearer — Brock Bowers now looks like a lock to sit and Puka Nacua is trending the wrong way._

## Top Storylines

- **J.K. Dobbins exited Denver's win over Jacksonville with a hamstring injury** in the third quarter after 10 carries for 36 yards — with R.J. Harvey already inactive, the Broncos finished the game on rookie Jonah Coleman and Tyler Badie. Fantasy takeaway: Coleman, who scored the go-ahead TD, is now the top waiver-wire RB add in the league until Dobbins' timeline clears.
- **Caleb Williams left the Bears' 9-3 loss to Minnesota with a non-contact hamstring injury** and didn't put weight on the leg; he's ruled out and undergoes imaging Monday. Fantasy takeaway: downgrade every Chicago passing-game piece until there's clarity — Tyson Bagent would start if Williams misses time.
- **Jayden Reed was carted off on a backboard with a neck injury** on the Packers' second offensive play at MetLife Stadium and stayed overnight in the hospital, though he posted his own reassuring update afterward; Green Bay still won 20-17 in OT. Fantasy takeaway: shift shares to Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks and Matthew Golden until Reed's timeline is known — a scary scene that needs a clean update before you trust it fades quickly.
- **Brock Bowers is now expected to miss Monday night's game vs. the Chargers**, per Ian Rapoport, with a Week 3 return targeted. Fantasy takeaway: hard downgrade for MNF — Michael Mayer is the streaming/DFS pivot.
- **Puka Nacua is "trending in the wrong direction" for Monday night** per Adam Schefter, after groin/hip soreness kept him out of practice all week. Fantasy takeaway: lean toward him sitting vs. the Giants — have Adams/Atwell queued as the hedge.
- **RJ Harvey and Zay Flowers were both confirmed inactive as expected**, and Michael Pittman Jr. sat out too — no surprises there, but Rashod Bateman (7/88/1 on 9 targets) fully cashed in on the Flowers absence in Baltimore's loss to New Orleans. Fantasy takeaway: Bateman is a weekly must-start as long as Flowers is out.
- **Chris Olave played through Thursday's hamstring scare with zero setback**, going for 8/86/1 in the Saints' win over Baltimore. Fantasy takeaway: no lingering concern — treat him as a full-workload starter again.
- **Joe Burrow was fully healthy and effective**, throwing for 207 yards and 2 TDs in a 20-6 win over a Texans team missing Nico Collins; Ja'Marr Chase (7/75/2 TD) and Tee Higgins (95 yards) both thrived. Fantasy takeaway: Cincinnati's passing game is back to full strength and start-everyone mode.
- **Rico Dowdle exited Pittsburgh's loss to New England with a toe injury**, while Jaylen Warren racked up 77 scrimmage yards. Fantasy takeaway: Warren is now the unambiguous Steelers lead back — Dowdle's Monday practice status is the swing factor for Week 3.
- **Marvin Mims Jr. (foot) was a surprise inactive for Denver**, and Jaylen Waddle made the Broncos pay with a 138-yard day in the comeback win over Jacksonville. Elsewhere, Darius Cooper (PHI) caught the first reception and TD of his career on the game-winning score with 9 seconds left in a 24-20 win at Tennessee, and the Colts upset the Chiefs 20-17 on Sunday Night Football.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Broncos backfield is in crisis mode**: with Harvey inactive and Dobbins hurt, 4th-round rookie Jonah Coleman scored the go-ahead touchdown and is the clear priority option if Dobbins' hamstring lingers, with Tyler Badie as the change-of-pace piece behind him.
- **Packers WR room reshuffles after Reed's injury**: Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks and Matthew Golden all see immediate target-share bumps.
- **Ravens passing game funnels through Rashod Bateman** with Flowers sidelined — he led the team with 9 targets.
- **Steelers backfield settles**: Jaylen Warren is the unambiguous lead RB with Dowdle's toe injury clouding his Week 3 outlook.
- **Broncos WR2 spot opens up** behind Courtland Sutton/Jaylen Waddle with Marvin Mims Jr. sidelined; Waddle himself looks fully entrenched as Denver's WR1 after his 138-yard day.
- **Texans passing game redistributes** with Nico Collins out — Dalton Schultz, Xavier Hutchinson, Kayshon Boutte and Jaylin Noel are absorbing extra looks, though none posted standout counting stats in the loss to Cincinnati.
