# Training Camp Report — September 21, 2026 (End of Day)

_Monday Night Football (Rams-Giants) delivered the day's biggest swing: Jaxson Dart left with a knee injury that won't have a real severity read until Tuesday's MRI. Elsewhere, MRIs closed the loop on two of the day's earlier injury scares — Dallas Goedert (MCL sprain) and Caleb Williams (confirmed Grade 1 hamstring) — and the Chargers made David Njoku's absence official with an IR move and a real timeline._

## Top Storylines

- **Jaxson Dart left MNF vs. the Rams with a knee injury**, hit mid-throw by Josaiah Stewart and Byron Young; he walked off (no cart) but didn't immediately go for X-rays. Fantasy takeaway: best case is an MCL sprain (4-6 weeks), worst case is a multi-ligament tear — do not start him or any Giants pass-catcher for Week 3 until Tuesday's MRI is back; Jameis Winston is the direct backup.
- **Malik Nabers left the same game with a shoulder injury but returned** after Rams CB Trent McDuffie yanked his arm down defending a pass; officially questionable. Fantasy takeaway: escaped the scarier outcome — treat as a normal (if banged-up) starter pending any postgame update.
- **Puka Nacua was officially inactive for MNF** with hip/groin soreness that Sean McVay says is unrelated to his earlier psoas issue. Fantasy takeaway: Davante Adams is the clear beneficiary again — he handled nearly half of L.A.'s receiving work in Week 1 with Nacua playing.
- **Dallas Goedert's MRI came back as a sprained MCL**, expected to cost him "a few weeks" (at least 4 games if the Eagles use IR); Philadelphia is re-signing Zach Ertz to fill the gap. Fantasy takeaway: bench/drop Goedert for the next several weeks — Ertz and Grant Calcaterra are the streaming options in his place.
- **David Njoku is officially on IR with a fibula injury**, out a minimum of four weeks with Week 8 (vs. the Rams, Nov. 1) as the earliest realistic return; Charlie Kolar also landed on IR, gutting the Chargers' TE room. Fantasy takeaway: drop Njoku in shallower leagues — this is a real multi-week absence, not a week-to-week watch.
- **Caleb Williams' MRI confirmed a Grade 1 hamstring strain**, and Ben Johnson called him "week to week" while adding he'll "likely miss some time," though Williams hasn't been ruled out for next Monday vs. Philadelphia yet. Fantasy takeaway: better than the worst-case fear, but treat him as day-to-day — keep Tyson Bagent rostered as insurance in deeper/2-QB leagues.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Giants' QB room is now Jameis Winston (with Dart hurt) behind a shakier offensive line** after Dart's exit — worth checking early-week practice reports before trusting any Giants skill player projection.
- **Chargers' tight end depth chart is effectively empty** with both Njoku and Kolar on IR; watch the waiver wire for any TE streamer the team elevates this week.
- **Eagles are restocking at TE with Zach Ertz** as Goedert's replacement in the near term.
