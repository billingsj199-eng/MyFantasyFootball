# Training Camp Report — September 21, 2026 (Midday)

_Monday brought a wave of medical updates on Sunday's Week 2 casualties — most of it encouraging (Dobbins, Reed, Darnold, Murray) — but the injury list also grew, headlined by Jayden Daniels dislocating the same elbow again. Monday Night Football (Rams-Giants, Raiders-Chargers) is still ahead tonight, so Bowers/Nacua/Njoku status updates for that slate are the thing to watch this afternoon._

## Top Storylines

- **Jayden Daniels dislocated his left elbow for the second straight season**, this time on the final play of the first half in Washington's loss to Dallas; X-rays showed no fracture but he's out indefinitely. Fantasy takeaway: Marcus Mariota takes over at QB — fade every Washington passing-game piece until Daniels is back.
- **Caleb Williams gets an MRI Monday, with initial hope for only a Grade 1 hamstring strain.** Fantasy takeaway: even the best-case outcome could cost him next Monday's game vs. Philadelphia — stash Tyson Bagent in deeper leagues.
- **J.K. Dobbins' hamstring is looking better than first feared** — Sean Payton called it "a little," and there's real optimism for a Week 3 return against Denver's next opponent, the Rams. Fantasy takeaway: don't fully bench him, but confirm Wednesday's practice status before trusting him over Jonah Coleman.
- **Jayden Reed has movement in all his extremities** and posted his own upbeat update from the hospital. Fantasy takeaway: encouraging, but no timetable yet — Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks and Matthew Golden remain the priority Packers WR pickups.
- **Tua Tagovailoa was diagnosed with fractured ribs and is officially out for Week 3** vs. the Raiders. Fantasy takeaway: Jacoby Brissett starts — fade Miami's passing-game pieces for at least one more week.
- **Sam Darnold is "recovering faster than anyone anticipated,"** per Ian Rapoport, though a Week 3 return remains a stretch with Week 4 the more realistic target. Fantasy takeaway: still start Drew Lock/lean on Jaxon Smith-Njigba's target share regardless of who's throwing him the ball.
- **Kyler Murray is expected to clear concussion protocol early this week** and is on track to start Week 3 for Minnesota. Fantasy takeaway: Murray reclaims the job the moment he's cleared; Wentz was fine in relief but is strictly a streamer once Murray's back.
- **A rough afternoon for Philadelphia's other skill players:** Saquon Barkley (stinger) and Dallas Goedert (knee) both left Sunday's win over Tennessee, though Barkley returned to finish the game. Fantasy takeaway: Barkley looks fine; Goedert (replaced by Johnny Mundt) needs a clean practice week before trusting him again.
- **David Njoku was carted off unable to bear weight** in the second quarter of the Chargers' loss to Las Vegas, and Alec Pierce was carted off with a heel/ankle injury (X-rays negative) during the Colts' SNF upset of Kansas City. Fantasy takeaway: both are real questions for Week 3 — check Wednesday's injury report before locking either into lineups.
- **Jonathon Brooks re-aggravated the groin injury** that cost him camp time during Carolina's blowout win over Atlanta. Fantasy takeaway: day-to-day per Dave Canales — a name to track but not yet a lineup scratch.
