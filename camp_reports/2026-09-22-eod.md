# Training Camp Report — September 22, 2026 (End of Day)

_The day's biggest swing: Jaxson Dart's knee is worse than Monday night's optimism suggested, with season-ending surgery now in play and Jameis Winston stepping into a full-time starter role. The Bears' QB room also took a hit when Tyson Bagent landed in concussion protocol. On a brighter note, Michael Penix Jr. was officially named the Falcons' Week 3 starter for Thursday's game vs. Green Bay._

## Top Storylines

- **Jaxson Dart's Tuesday MRI showed the knee injury is worse than initially believed**, and the Giants are now facing the possibility of season-ending surgery. Fantasy takeaway: Dart is off the board for redraft and a real dynasty concern — Jameis Winston is now viewed as the full-time starter.
- **Jameis Winston is in line to be the Giants' full-time starter** with Dart's status in doubt for the rest of the season. Fantasy takeaway: streamable/QB2 value in deeper leagues with Nabers and the Giants' pass-catchers behind him.
- **Tyson Bagent was placed in the NFL's concussion protocol**, muddying the Bears' Week 3 plans behind an already-sidelined Caleb Williams (hamstring). Fantasy takeaway: if both are out, Case Keenum would start vs. Philadelphia — treat Chicago's passing game as unpredictable until a starter is confirmed.
- **Michael Penix Jr. was named the Falcons' Week 3 starter** for Thursday night vs. Green Bay, his first start since tearing his ACL last November; Tua Tagovailoa (full practice Tuesday) will back him up. Fantasy takeaway: Penix is startable with Drake London and Kyle Pitts after Atlanta's 0-2 start finally gets its top QB back.
- **Alec Pierce is expected to miss multiple weeks** with the heel injury he aggravated Sunday, and the Colts moved fast to sign veteran Darius Slayton. Fantasy takeaway: drop Pierce in shallow leagues; Slayton is a deep-league flier once he learns the offense, with Josh Downs also in line for more targets.
- **Jayden Reed was a non-participant again Tuesday** and is expected to miss Thursday's short-week game vs. Atlanta with his neck/back injury. Fantasy takeaway: keep riding Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks and Matthew Golden for Green Bay's Thursday game.
- **Malik Nabers had a shoulder scare at Tuesday's practice** but was called "good" by HC Brian Daboll, who expects him back at practice Thursday. Fantasy takeaway: trust Nabers for a full workload in Week 3 barring a setback.
