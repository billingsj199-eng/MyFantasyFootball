# Training Camp Report — September 22, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news is mostly follow-through on Monday night's injury scares. Jaxson Dart got the "going to be OK" signal from his GM after clean X-rays, Jayden Daniels' elbow now heads to outside specialists with a possible surgery call looming, Nico Collins' hamstring is dragging into a second missed week, and Caleb Williams' hamstring is bad enough that Tyson Bagent is stepping into starters' reps Wednesday._

## Top Storylines

- **Jaxson Dart's Tuesday MRI didn't come with a public diagnosis yet, but the signs are good**: X-rays were negative, and Giants GM Joe Schoen told ESPN Dart is "going to be OK." Fantasy takeaway: still don't trust Giants pass-catchers for a firm Week 3 QB plan until the MRI read is confirmed, but the doomsday multi-ligament outcome looks increasingly unlikely.
- **Malik Nabers said he popped his own shoulder back into place on the sideline** and played through it, but he was a non-factor after returning (1 catch on 4 targets) and still needs X-rays to rule out further damage. Fantasy takeaway: keep him rostered as a start, but the shoulder is a real watch item into Week 3.
- **Jayden Daniels will see outside elbow specialists Tuesday and Wednesday**, with Dan Quinn saying the exam of the elbow's stability will determine next steps; X-rays showed no fracture, but surgery is still on the table and could be season-ending if recommended. Fantasy takeaway: hold Daniels but plan around Marcus Mariota for at least this week — a surgery announcement would be a much bigger dynasty/redraft swing.
- **Caleb Williams won't practice this week, and Tyson Bagent will work with Chicago's starters when practice resumes Wednesday** — "almost certainly" setting up Bagent's first start since 2023 vs. the Eagles on MNF. Fantasy takeaway: Bagent is now a streamable QB2 in deeper leagues if he's the Week 3 starter; downgrade Bears pass-catchers' floors accordingly.
- **Nico Collins is trending toward missing a second straight game**, with Texans coach DeMeco Ryans offering no real update ("still seeing how he progresses") and a source telling Aaron Wilson he's not currently expected to play vs. the Colts. Fantasy takeaway: Dalton Schultz (12-140-0 in Week 2 with Collins out) remains a strong start as long as Collins is sidelined.
- **Zay Flowers has a "good shot" to play Week 3 vs. Dallas**, per Ravens HC Jesse Minter, after a limited practice week following his Week 2 hamstring re-aggravation. Fantasy takeaway: cautiously trust Flowers again pending Wednesday's practice report, but keep Rashod Bateman in the flex mix as insurance.
