# Training Camp Report — September 23, 2026 (End of Day)

_Wednesday practice reports brought a wave of fresh IR moves and backfield chaos: Jonathon Brooks and Alec Pierce both landed on IR, the Chargers lost both tight ends for a month, and Denver's backfield is now three-deep in walking wounded. Two starting QBs (Caleb Williams, Jayden Daniels) remain in limbo._

## Top Storylines

- **Panthers RB Jonathon Brooks is done for at least 6 weeks.** Placed on IR and set for core muscle surgery after a Sunday groin injury; Carolina signed Isaiah Simmons in a corresponding move (not a RB). Fantasy takeaway: Rico Dowdle-type bellcow scenarios open up in Carolina's backfield — check the waiver wire for whoever inherits early-down work.
- **Colts WR Alec Pierce goes to IR (heel), out at least 4 weeks.** Aggravated the same heel three days after playing through it vs. KC; hoping to avoid a second surgery. Fantasy takeaway: more snaps for Josh Downs and the Colts' other outside receivers in a high-powered offense.
- **Chargers lose both tight ends for the month.** David Njoku (fibula, carted off) and Charlie Kolar (forearm) are both on IR ahead of Sunday vs. Buffalo, leaving Oronde Gadsden II as the only healthy TE on the 53-man roster. Fantasy takeaway: Gadsden II is an immediate streaming/waiver add in all TE-needy leagues.
- **Denver's backfield is banged up three-deep.** Rookie Jonah Coleman (ankle) did not practice Wednesday, while J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey (both hamstring) were limited. Coach Sean Payton wasn't alarmed, but all three carry real Sunday-night risk vs. the Rams. Fantasy takeaway: monitor Thursday/Friday reports closely — whichever back is healthiest by Sunday becomes a must-start, and this remains a muddled committee otherwise.
- **Caleb Williams' hamstring makes him a game-time watch for MNF.** Bears coach Ben Johnson said Williams likely won't practice this week and is "week to week," but has not ruled him out of next Monday's game vs. Philadelphia. Fantasy takeaway: roster Tyson Bagent as insurance in Bears-QB and streaming leagues; downgrade the entire Chicago passing offense until Williams is confirmed.
- **Jayden Daniels' elbow surgery decision still pending.** Commanders QB is consulting specialists this week on the same joint he dislocated last year; doctors say the non-throwing-side location makes surgery a closer call than it looks. Fantasy takeaway: no clarity yet — hold Daniels but brace for missed time either way.
- **Mike Evans trending toward playing through a hip injury.** Kyle Shanahan said the 49ers WR will "have to be managed" in practice but is expected to suit up Week 3; the injury reportedly isn't serious. Fantasy takeaway: keep him in lineups, just expect a workload cap and monitor Friday's status.
- **Aaron Jones Sr. sits out Wednesday, uncertain for Sunday.** The Vikings RB is working through a knee issue suffered in Week 2's win over Chicago; with Jordan Mason still on IR, Minnesota signed three veteran backs (DeeJay Dallas, Jerome Ford, Jaret Patterson) as depth insurance. Fantasy takeaway: Jones is a game-time call vs. Tampa Bay — have Dallas/Ford/Patterson on your radar as speculative adds if Jones sits.
- **Jadarian Price's Week 2 injury is a shoulder, not a chest issue, and it's not considered serious.** X-rays came back negative, but head coach Mike Macdonald wouldn't guarantee Price plays Sunday. Emanuel Wilson (21 carries, 92 yards in Week 2) is the direct beneficiary either way. Fantasy takeaway: Wilson is a strong Week 3 add regardless of Price's Friday status; Price himself remains streamable if active.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Chargers TE room**: Oronde Gadsden II is now the unquestioned lead tight end after Njoku and Kolar both went to IR the same day; LA may elevate a practice-squad TE (Patrick Herbert or Evan Svoboda) for blocking depth, but Gadsden gets the receiving work.
- **Broncos backfield**: With Coleman, Dobbins and Harvey all banged up simultaneously, Denver's committee approach vs. the Rams on Sunday Night Football is genuinely unresolved heading into the weekend.
- **Vikings backfield**: Kevin O'Connell held Jones out of Wednesday's practice entirely; rookie Demond Claiborne is the only camp-tenured RB left, prompting the trio of veteran street free-agent signings.
