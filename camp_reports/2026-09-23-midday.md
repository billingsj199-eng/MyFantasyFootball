# Training Camp Report — September 23, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet overnight/morning stretch. The main movement: Jayden Reed's neck injury sounds more serious than Tuesday night's framing, Brock Bowers is finally practicing and trending toward his 2026 debut Sunday, and Marvin Harrison Jr.'s disappearing act in Arizona is now drawing "change of scenery" talk._

## Top Storylines

- **Jayden Reed's neck injury is worse than "DNP, might miss Thursday."** He's back in Green Bay for further testing and consultations, and coach Matt LaFleur says it's "way too early" to know if he plays again this season. Fantasy takeaway: treat Reed as a longer-term issue, not a one-week miss — keep riding Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks and Matthew Golden.
- **Brock Bowers is actually practicing this week**, not just "targeted" — Raiders coach Klint Kubiak says he's gearing up to play Sunday vs. the Saints in his 2026 debut. Fantasy takeaway: a real green light after weeks of false starts; plan to start him if he's active, target/route volume permitting.
- **Marvin Harrison Jr. has all but vanished from Arizona's offense** — one catch for 33 yards and four total targets through two games, with both Cardinals running backs out-catching him. Fantasy takeaway: bench him in all formats; SI's Albert Breer is already floating a change-of-scenery scenario if the target share doesn't change soon.
- **Jaxson Dart's surgery decision is still pending** as of Wednesday — no confirmed diagnosis or timetable yet, though it reportedly does not involve the ACL. Fantasy takeaway: no change to the outlook from last night — Jameis Winston remains the presumptive Giants starter regardless of Dart's eventual procedure.
