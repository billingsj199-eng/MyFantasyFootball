# Training Camp Report — September 24, 2026 (End of Day)

_Friday's practice reports will set final Week 3 tags, but Thursday's news already moved the needle: Puka Nacua's outlook got worse, San Francisco picked up a second banged-up pass catcher in George Kittle, and Michael Penix Jr.'s long-awaited return kicked off Thursday night in Green Bay._

## Top Storylines

- **Puka Nacua's Week 3 outlook worsened Thursday.** Sean McVay said flatly "he's not making great progress" with the hip/groin issue and likened the soreness to a "pre-hernia" strain, adding he has "a ton of confidence" in the group if Nacua sits again. Fantasy takeaway: lean toward him missing a second straight game — Davante Adams is the clear top target in LA either way.
- **George Kittle (hamstring) did not practice Thursday**, adding a second banged-up 49ers pass-catcher alongside Mike Evans ahead of Sunday's game vs. the Rams. Fantasy takeaway: real risk he sits — monitor Friday's report before trusting him in Week 3 lineups.
- **Mike Evans trending toward playing Sunday vs. Arizona.** Rapoport reported Thursday morning the hip injury gives him "a good chance" to practice and play this week. Fantasy takeaway: keep him in lineups as the 49ers' Week 3 outlook brightens slightly.
- **49ers signed veteran WR Brandin Cooks to the practice squad**, addressing a receiver room thinned by Evans, Ricky Pearsall, De'Zhaun Stribling and Demarcus Robinson injuries. Fantasy takeaway: not roster-worthy yet, but a name to watch if San Francisco's injuries pile up further.
- **Nico Collins' hamstring is a confirmed Grade 1 strain and he's not expected back for Week 3** vs. the Colts, extending his absence to a second straight game. Fantasy takeaway: keep starting Dalton Schultz and Houston's other pass-catchers over Collins.
- **Ladd McConkey (rib) was a full practice participant Thursday**, an upgrade after managing the injury since Week 1. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward a normal workload Sunday vs. Buffalo.
- **J.K. Dobbins was a full participant in Thursday's practice**, an upgrade from Wednesday's limited session. Fantasy takeaway: further cements him as Denver's clear lead back Sunday vs. the Rams over Jonah Coleman and RJ Harvey.
- **Michael Penix Jr.'s 2026 debut is underway on Thursday Night Football vs. Green Bay** — Penix hit Austin Hooper for an early TD and Bijan Robinson answered with a rushing score as the two traded blows early (10-7 Falcons after the first quarter). Fantasy takeaway: too early to draw conclusions — check the final box score for early-season trust in Atlanta's passing game.
