# Training Camp Report — September 24, 2026 (Midday)

_A quiet overnight news cycle — the main action is Thursday practice-status deltas ahead of Week 3, plus tonight's Penix Jr. return on TNF. Ravens' Zay Flowers and Rams' Puka Nacua both trended the wrong way; Vikings' Aaron Jones and Broncos' J.K. Dobbins both trended the right way._

## Top Storylines

- **Michael Penix Jr. makes his 2026 debut tonight vs. Green Bay.** Falcons QB gets his first start since tearing his ACL last November; Tua Tagovailoa is active as the backup. Fantasy takeaway: Penix is startable with Drake London and Kyle Pitts in a prime-time spot — worth checking the box score tonight for early-season trust.
- **Zay Flowers (hamstring) didn't practice again Thursday.** Second straight missed session ahead of Baltimore's Week 3 trip to face Dallas; Ravens coach Jesse Minter still calls it a "good shot" to play Sunday. Fantasy takeaway: treat as a game-time call — have Rashod Bateman/DeAndre Hopkins ready as the hedge.
- **Puka Nacua (hip/groin) has missed every practice this week.** Sean McVay says he's "not sure" Nacua will play Sunday vs. Denver, though the team isn't worried about it being long-term. Fantasy takeaway: Davante Adams is the safer WR1 bet in LA's offense until Nacua is confirmed active.
- **Aaron Jones upgraded to limited practice Thursday** after sitting out Wednesday with a knee injury. Fantasy takeaway: a real step in the right direction for Sunday vs. Tampa Bay — still confirm Friday's report before locking him into lineups over the Vikings' veteran RB depth (Dallas/Ford/Patterson).
- **J.K. Dobbins trending toward playing Sunday vs. the Rams**, with Denver calling his hamstring "good" and putting him on track to practice this week. Fantasy takeaway: firms up as the lead Broncos back play over Jonah Coleman (ankle) and RJ Harvey (hamstring), pending Friday's final tag.
- **Bills WRs split ways on Wednesday's practice report.** DJ Moore (AC joint/shoulder) was limited but said he felt "great" afterward; Keon Coleman was a full DNP with an ankle injury. Fantasy takeaway: Moore trending toward playing Sunday vs. the Chargers; Coleman's practice reports Friday are worth tracking before starting him.
- **Marvin Harrison Jr. trade rumors got more specific.** ESPN's Dan Graziano reported the Raiders, Packers and Giants are among teams watching Harrison ahead of the Nov. 10 deadline, with Arizona reportedly open to a change-of-scenery deal given his 1-catch start to the season. Fantasy takeaway: still bench him in all formats — a trade wouldn't change his outlook mid-season, but it's a dynasty watch item.
