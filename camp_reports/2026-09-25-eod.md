# Training Camp Report — September 25, 2026 (End of Day)

_Friday's final injury reports reshape several Week 3 backfields and receiving corps: Rico Dowdle is out in Pittsburgh (locking in Jaylen Warren, who is himself questionable), Jayden Daniels' elbow saga gets a resolution (no surgery, but still out), and Brock Bowers/Zay Flowers/Mike Evans all trend toward playing despite late-week questionable tags._

## Top Storylines

- **Rico Dowdle is out, and Jaylen Warren is questionable but expected to play** — Pittsburgh's backfield picture for Sunday vs. Cincinnati is now clear: Warren (shoulder, limited 3 straight days) is "seemingly expected to carry the load" even with the tag, and rookie Eli Heidenreich backs him up. Fantasy takeaway: Warren is a lock-in RB2/flex with true bell-cow volume if he suits up; have a contingency only if he's scratched pregame.
- **Jayden Daniels won't need surgery, but he's still out and week-to-week** — Commanders QB will not go on IR after his second left elbow dislocation in 11 months, but there's no firm return timeline; Marcus Mariota starts again. Fantasy takeaway: keep Daniels benched and treat Washington's pass-catchers (Terry McLaurin, Deebo Samuel) as touchdown-dependent with Mariota under center.
- **Brock Bowers is questionable for his 2026 debut, but the DNP was by design** — Raiders coach Klint Kubiak said sitting Bowers out Friday was a planned rest day, not a setback; models give him a 58% chance to play Sunday vs. New Orleans. Fantasy takeaway: a coin-flip TE1 stash — check pregame inactives before locking your lineup.
- **Zay Flowers "took a step forward" in Brazil, listed questionable** — He returned to limited practice Friday, and Ravens coach Jesse Minter said he has "a real opportunity" to play Sunday vs. Dallas. Fantasy takeaway: trending in the right direction after a lost week — a game-time call, lean toward available.
- **Mike Evans returns to practice, trending to play vs. Arizona** — After sitting out with a hip injury, Evans practiced Friday and looks on track for Sunday, though final word won't come until ~90 minutes before kickoff. Fantasy takeaway: keep him in lineups with a survivable pivot plan given the late-decision risk.
- **George Kittle's Friday designation is confirmed doubtful** per the 49ers' own injury report (Hufanga was cleared) — no change from the midday report, but now official. Fantasy takeaway: plan to bench him; monitor the final pregame report.
- **Colts WR corps thins further**: Adonai Mitchell (finger) is questionable vs. Detroit, on top of Alec Pierce already on IR. Fantasy takeaway: Mitchell's snap share matters more with Pierce sidelined — check inactives before trusting him as more than a flex.
