# Training Camp Report — September 25, 2026 (Midday)

_Friday brings clarity on two season-altering QB injuries — Jaxson Dart's year is officially over and Caleb Williams is locked out of Week 3 — while George Kittle's Sunday outlook worsens to doubtful and Puka Nacua/Zay Flowers remain up in the air ahead of the final Week 3 injury reports._

## Top Storylines

- **Jaxson Dart is done for the regular season.** John Harbaugh confirmed Wednesday that Dart will have surgery for MCL, PCL and meniscus damage (ACL intact); Jameis Winston is locked in as the Giants' full-time starter for the remaining 15 games. Fantasy takeaway: bench Dart in all formats and reassess Giants pass-catchers (Malik Nabers, etc.) around Winston's skill set.
- **Caleb Williams will sit Week 3, and it's Case Keenum — not Tyson Bagent — getting the start** for Chicago on Monday night vs. Philadelphia, since Bagent remains in concussion protocol. Fantasy takeaway: significant fantasy downgrade for Bears pass-catchers (DJ Moore, Rome Odunze, Colston Loveland) with Keenum under center.
- **George Kittle is now doubtful for Sunday's game vs. the Rams**, an escalation from Thursday's DNP. Fantasy takeaway: plan for Kittle to sit — a real problem for 49ers streamers given Mike Evans' own uncertain hip.
- **Puka Nacua's Friday status is still murky.** McVay: "I'm not sure what his status is... I'm hopeful that he'll play," after Nacua missed every practice this week. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a game-time coin flip at best; Davante Adams remains the safer LA start.
- **Zay Flowers hasn't been ruled out for Brazil, but time is running out.** He's missed every practice this week ahead of the Ravens' trip to face the Cowboys; Friday's session is the last real chance to get on track. Fantasy takeaway: lean toward a second straight missed game — DeAndre Hopkins/Rashod Bateman absorb more Baltimore looks if he sits.
- **Travis Etienne (hamstring) was limited Wednesday** for New Orleans; models still give him a ~75% chance to play Sunday vs. the Raiders. Fantasy takeaway: monitor Friday's report, but treat him as a low-end RB2 given he's still splitting the Saints backfield even healthy.
- **Michael Penix Jr.'s season debut was a hit.** He went 18/25 for 256 yards, a TD and an INT as Atlanta blew out Green Bay 35-14 on Thursday Night Football; Bijan Robinson ran wild for 194 yards and 2 TDs. Fantasy takeaway: Penix looks like a streamable low-end QB1 going forward, and Bijan reasserted himself as a true RB1.
