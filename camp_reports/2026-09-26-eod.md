# Training Camp Report — September 26, 2026 (End of Day)

_Saturday's news cycle was about final Week 3 tune-ups rather than new bombshells: Saquon Barkley and Travis Etienne both shed their injury designations entirely, Zay Flowers looks like a capped-snap gamble if he suits up, and two more players (Jonah Coleman, Andrei Iosivas) landed on IR with clear return timelines. The Steelers also kept adding running back depth after Rico Dowdle's toe injury._

## Top Storylines

- **Saquon Barkley is fully cleared, off the injury report, and playing Monday night vs. the Bears** — full practice participant Thursday and Friday after the Week 3 stinger scare. Fantasy takeaway: no more hedging — start him as a clear RB1.
- **Travis Etienne is off the injury report and ready to go vs. the Raiders** — hamstring maintenance days are behind him. Fantasy takeaway: health is a non-issue, but the Kamara/Donaldson timeshare still caps his weekly ceiling.
- **Zay Flowers will test his hamstring pregame and, if active, plays on a snap count vs. Dallas** — did not practice Wednesday/Thursday, limited Friday. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a capped-volume flex at best, not a full-workload starter.
- **Jonah Coleman is officially on IR (high-ankle sprain), out until at least Week 7** — confirms last week's "ruled out" is now a multi-week absence. Fantasy takeaway: J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey remain the only backs to roster in Denver's backfield.
- **Andrei Iosivas had thumb surgery and is on IR, out 4-6 weeks** — torn ligament suffered in Week 2. Fantasy takeaway: minimal fantasy loss; deep-league Bengals WR options get marginally more run.
- **Steelers keep adding RB depth as Sunday's Bengals game approaches** — signed Travis Homer to the active roster and elevated Lew Nichols from the practice squad, with Rico Dowdle out (toe) and Jaylen Warren questionable (shoulder). Fantasy takeaway: Warren remains the lead-work bet if active; Homer/Nichols are only relevant as a last resort.
