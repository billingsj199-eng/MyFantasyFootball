# Training Camp Report — September 26, 2026 (Midday)

_Saturday's final Week 3 injury picture is largely settled: Sam Darnold and Kyler Murray are both officially back under center, Nico Collins and Puka Nacua are trending toward second straight missed games, and two rookie WRs (Caleb Douglas, Barion Brown) are ruled out for Sunday._

## Top Storylines

- **Sam Darnold is officially cleared and starting Sunday vs. Washington** — full practice participant Thursday and Friday; Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald confirmed "Sam's going to play." Fantasy takeaway: reinstate him as a streaming QB option; Drew Lock reverts to the bench.
- **Kyler Murray clears concussion protocol and reclaims the Cardinals' starting job vs. Tampa Bay** — Carson Wentz, who filled in for two games, is back to backup duty. Fantasy takeaway: Murray is startable immediately with no snap-count concerns reported.
- **Nico Collins is officially OUT for a second straight week (hamstring) vs. the Colts** — Grade 1 strain hasn't healed in time. Fantasy takeaway: keep riding Dalton Schultz and Xavier Hutchinson as Houston's top pass-catching options.
- **Puka Nacua upgraded to doubtful, trending to miss a second straight game vs. Denver** — no practice all week with a hip/groin issue; McVay says it isn't considered long-term. Fantasy takeaway: plan as if he's out and have a bench option ready.
- **Caleb Douglas (rookie WR, MIA) ruled out vs. Chiefs** — ankle injury from Week 2 didn't clear in time despite not being considered serious. Fantasy takeaway: Malik Washington and the rest of Miami's thin WR room absorb the extra targets.
- **Barion Brown (rookie WR, NO) ruled out vs. Raiders** — hamstring kept him out of practice all week. Fantasy takeaway: negligible offensive fantasy loss (9 combined snaps through two weeks), but Saints lose their kick/punt returner — Jayden Price next up.
- **DJ Moore (BUF) is a game-time call vs. the Chargers** — AC joint sprain, not considered serious, limited Wednesday/Friday. Fantasy takeaway: have a flex contingency ready in case he's scratched.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane officially lands on IR** — wrist surgery is done and Baltimore is targeting a Week 6 return. Fantasy takeaway: confirms thin Ravens WR2 depth behind Zay Flowers for at least the next month.
- **RJ Harvey (DEN) is cleared to play Sunday night vs. the Rams** — upgraded to full practice Thursday after missing Week 2 with a hamstring injury. Fantasy takeaway: crowded backfield with J.K. Dobbins (also full practice) caps him as deep-league flex only.
- **Tre Tucker is emerging as the Raiders' clear top target earner** — 18.6% target share and back-to-back strong games while Brock Bowers ramps back up. Fantasy takeaway: priority Week 3 waiver add against a Saints defense he should carve up again.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Broncos backfield**: RJ Harvey and J.K. Dobbins both logged full practices Thursday, while Jonah Coleman (ankle) remains out — a two-way timeshare likely caps both backs' Week 3 ceiling.
- **Raiders WR room**: Tre Tucker has firmly established himself as the top target earner even as Brock Bowers works back into the rotation; expect his volume to normalize once Bowers is fully healthy.
- **Dolphins WR corps**: With Douglas out and Jaylen Wright doubtful in the backfield, Miami's offensive depth is thin for Sunday's home opener vs. Kansas City — watch Malik Washington's role.
- **QB situations diverge across three teams**: Arizona gets Murray back as starter, while Chicago (Case Keenum) and Washington (Marcus Mariota) remain in backup mode for at least one more week.
