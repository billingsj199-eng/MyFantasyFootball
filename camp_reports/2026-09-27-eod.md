# Training Camp Report — September 27, 2026 (End of Day)

_Sunday's Week 3 slate delivered brutal injury news at the skill positions — De'Von Achane and Justin Jefferson both left games and didn't return, and Baker Mayfield dislocated his throwing-hand thumb — while Brock Bowers made an emphatic season debut and this morning's game-time decisions (Zay Flowers, DJ Moore, Keon Coleman) all resolved to "played."_

## Top Storylines

- **De'Von Achane's knee injury "does not sound very optimistic,"** per Dolphins coach Jeff Hafley, after a non-contact plant-and-cut injury in the first quarter of a 24-10 loss to Kansas City. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a likely multi-week (or worse) absence until Monday's MRI; Jaylen Wright and Alexander Mattison become must-adds in all Miami backfield shares.
- **Justin Jefferson exited with a left ankle injury and was ruled out** in the Vikings' 23-16 win over Tampa Bay. Fantasy takeaway: monitor Monday's imaging closely — this is the single biggest name hurt today; Jordan Addison becomes the clear early-down alpha if Jefferson misses time.
- **Baker Mayfield dislocated his right (throwing) thumb** late in the loss to Minnesota; X-rays negative for fracture, MRI Monday. Fantasy takeaway: undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels is in line to start vs. Green Bay if Mayfield can't go — treat all Bucs pass-catchers as lower-confidence starts until his status clears.
- **Brock Bowers made his 2026 debut a memorable one**: 10 catches on 13 targets for 116 yards and the game-winning 4-yard TD in Las Vegas's 35-27 win over New Orleans, pushing the Raiders to 3-0. Fantasy takeaway: he's back to must-start TE1 territory immediately.
- **Zay Flowers played through the hamstring injury and delivered**, including the clutch 27-yard catch that set up Baltimore's walk-off, 56-yard field goal in a 34-31 win over Dallas in Rio. Fantasy takeaway: workload may still be capped short-term, but tonight's usage in the biggest moment is a strong signal he's trending back to full-time.
- **DJ Moore and Keon Coleman were both active and played** for Buffalo vs. the Chargers after being game-time decisions. Fantasy takeaway: both clear for lineups, though snap counts are worth checking Monday for signs of in-game management.
- **Jalen McMillan left early with a re-aggravated left knee** — same knee that cost him camp time — on just the third offensive snap of the Bucs-Vikings game. Fantasy takeaway: another injury-prone game for a player already on a short leash for target share; downgrade his floor.
- **Jack Bech (LV) fractured his forearm** and is expected to land on IR, sidelining him at least four weeks. Fantasy takeaway: modest bump for Tre Tucker and Jakobi Meyers in the Raiders' passing-game pecking order behind Bowers.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Bucs QB room on alert**: if Mayfield's thumb sidelines him, UDFA Jalon Daniels would make his first career start vs. Green Bay — a shaky Week 4 outlook for Tampa Bay's pass-catchers (Egbuka, Godwin) until this resolves.
- **Raiders WR/TE pecking order resets** with Bech headed to IR and Bowers now fully back: Bowers is the clear No. 1 target, with Tucker and Meyers next in line on early downs.
- **Bills WRs both played through injuries** (Moore's shoulder, Coleman's ankle) rather than sitting — a good early-season signal the team trusts both to handle a game-day workload despite the injury reports.

## Rookie Watch
