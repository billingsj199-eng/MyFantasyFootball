# Training Camp Report — September 28, 2026 (End of Day)

_Monday's three biggest morning injuries got resolved by evening: Barkley and Jefferson both caught breaks on their MRIs, while Mayfield's is a mixed bag (no structural damage, but still 3+ weeks out). The Vikings took a real hit at TE with Josh Oliver. Monday Night Football (Bears-Eagles) was still in progress at file time, so tonight's full recap rolls into tomorrow's midday report._

## Top Storylines

- **Justin Jefferson's ankle MRI came back clean** — a sprain with no long-term damage. Fantasy takeaway: comfortably projectable as a Week 4 starter vs. Miami, not just a game-time gamble.
- **Saquon Barkley got good MRI results on his neck stinger** and was active for Monday Night Football vs. Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: full workload expected, no lingering Week 4 concern from tonight's game barring a new issue.
- **Baker Mayfield's MRI showed no ligament or tendon damage** on his dislocated throwing thumb, but coach Todd Bowles confirmed he's out at least three weeks anyway. Fantasy takeaway: Bucs pass-catchers (Evans, Godwin, Egbuka) downgrade for a few weeks under a backup QB.
- **Vikings TE Josh Oliver is likely done for the season** after an MRI confirmed a torn biceps; surgery is expected. Fantasy takeaway: he was playing over half of Minnesota's offensive snaps — check the wire for whoever inherits those TE reps.
- **Giants OLB Brian Burns is believed to have a season-ending torn ACL**, per Schefter/Rapoport, a week after losing Jaxson Dart. Fantasy takeaway: no offensive impact, but a real blow to the Giants' defense/DST outlook.
- **Case Keenum (not Tyson Bagent) got the Week 3 start for the Bears** with Caleb Williams out — Bagent cleared concussion protocol but hadn't practiced enough during the week. Fantasy takeaway: expect Bagent to reclaim the backup gig once fully back into the rotation if Williams is still out past his 3-4 week hamstring timeline.
- **Jalen Hurts was briefly evaluated for a concussion** on MNF after a hard out-of-bounds hit, but was cleared and returned the same drive. Fantasy takeaway: no ongoing concern, but Andy Dalton's replacement series ended in a red-zone interception — a reminder of Philly's thin QB2 plan if Hurts ever missed time.
