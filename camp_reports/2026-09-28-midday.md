# Training Camp Report — September 28, 2026 (Midday)

_Monday brought confirmation on Sunday's scariest injuries — De'Von Achane and Jaxson Dart are both out for the season — plus a genuine trade: the Giants added J.J. McCarthy for depth behind new starter Jameis Winston. Several Week 3 game-day questions (Barkley, Jefferson, Mayfield) remain in limbo pending MRIs._

## Top Storylines

- **De'Von Achane's knee injury is confirmed as a torn ACL** — he's out for the rest of the 2026 season, per Schefter and Rapoport. Fantasy takeaway: Ollie Gordon II is now the clear lead back in Miami; he's a top-priority waiver add off his 17-41-1 rushing line plus 3 catches in relief.
- **Jaxson Dart will have season-ending knee surgery** for a torn meniscus and MCL (his ACL is intact). Fantasy takeaway: Jameis Winston is the Giants' starter going forward — a mild bump for Cam Skattebo and Malik Nabers as steady offensive touches.
- **The Giants traded a 2027 fifth-round pick to the Vikings for J.J. McCarthy**, adding QB depth behind Winston. Fantasy takeaway: not a near-term factor, but a name worth stashing in deep dynasty leagues in case Winston struggles.
- **Cam Skattebo remained the clear lead back** in the Giants' Week 3 win over Tennessee (20 carries/60 yards, 3 catches/40 yards). Fantasy takeaway: stays a comfortable RB2 for Week 4 vs. Arizona even with the QB shuffle behind him.
- **Kenyon Sadiq broke out for the Jets**: 7 catches, 105 yards, and a TD on 8 targets vs. Detroit. Fantasy takeaway: with Adonai Mitchell and Mason Taylor banged up, Sadiq is a streaming-TE add worth grabbing now.
- **Breece Hall was ruled out vs. the Lions with a thigh injury**; head coach Aaron Glenn says there's no update until Wednesday. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as questionable for Week 4 at Chicago and handcuff with Braelon Allen.
- **Saquon Barkley suffered a stinger vs. the Titans**, returned after halftime, and is getting an MRI. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward playing tonight vs. the Bears, but confirm pregame inactives before locking him in.
- **Justin Jefferson's ankle "got close" to returning Sunday**, per Kevin O'Connell, with a Monday re-evaluation to follow. Fantasy takeaway: hopeful signs of a lower-ankle sprain, but don't pencil him into Week 4 lineups until the exam confirms it.
- **Caleb Williams (hamstring, Grade 2) is officially out for tonight's Monday-nighter vs. the Eagles**, on a 3-4 week timeline. Fantasy takeaway: Tyson Bagent starts, which drags down DJ Moore/Rome Odunze floors for the next several weeks.
- **Jayden Daniels is "unlikely" for Week 4** per Ian Rapoport after his dislocated elbow (same joint as a prior injury) kept him out of Week 3. Fantasy takeaway: keep Commanders pass-catchers as lower-confidence plays until he's back on the field.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Miami backfield reshuffled**: with Achane out for the year, Ollie Gordon II is the presumptive lead back; Jaylen Wright's role/health is the swing factor to watch.
- **Giants QB room**: Jameis Winston starts now, with newly acquired J.J. McCarthy as the primary backup — Dart is done for the year.
- **Jets backfield**: Braelon Allen is next up if Breece Hall (thigh) sits in Week 4, per beat reporting.
- **Bears QB room**: Tyson Bagent gets the start tonight vs. Philadelphia with Caleb Williams sidelined; Case Keenum is the other option in the room.
