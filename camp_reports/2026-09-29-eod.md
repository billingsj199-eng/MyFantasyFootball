# Training Camp Report — September 29, 2026 (End of Day)

_Tuesday cleanup on the Week 3 injury wave: Achane's ACL is now fully official and season-ending, Baker Mayfield's replacement is locked in, and the waiver-wire winners/losers from Sunday's carnage are coming into focus._

## Top Storylines

- **De'Von Achane's season is over** — Monday's diagnosis confirmed a torn ACL from Sunday's Chiefs loss. Fantasy takeaway: Ollie Gordon II is the clear early-down/goal-line pickup, with Jaylen Wright also in the mix; neither profiles as a true 1-for-1 Achane replacement.
- **Jalon Daniels (undrafted rookie) is officially the Bucs' starting QB** for Oct. 4 vs. Green Bay with Mayfield out. Fantasy takeaway: streaming caution — first career start for a UDFA behind an offense that just lost its steady QB.
- **Nico Collins has a "decent chance" to play Week 4 vs. Dallas**, per Adam Schefter, after two weeks out with a hamstring strain. Fantasy takeaway: monitor Wednesday's practice designation before trusting him; his return also caps Xavier Hutchinson's short-term waiver spike.
- **Michael Wilson is the clear WR1 in Arizona** — 17 targets, 11 catches, 89 yards, 1 TD in Week 3, out-producing Marvin Harrison Jr. in all three games. Fantasy takeaway: startable WR2/3 as long as the Cardinals are playing from behind.
- **David Montgomery's role is shrinking in Houston** — Woody Marks took over short-yardage and goal-line work in the fourth quarter Sunday. Fantasy takeaway: Montgomery becomes touchdown-dependent; Marks gains standalone flex appeal.
- **Jaguars are rolling with a shared backfield** — coach Liam Coen confirmed Bhayshul Tuten and Chris Rodriguez Jr. will keep splitting work rather than naming a clear-cut lead back. Fantasy takeaway: caps Tuten's ceiling as a must-start; treat both as flex-only for now.
- **Packers' WR2 job is up for grabs with Jayden Reed still out indefinitely** — Christian Watson and Matthew Golden are the top options, with Jonnu Smith and Mark Redman also soaking up meaningful snaps at TE. Fantasy takeaway: Golden gets a real look at weekly volume; low-end streamer appeal for both WRs.
- **Odell Beckham Jr. released by the Giants** after zero catches on 20 snaps through three games. Fantasy takeaway: no roster relevance in redraft; watch for a landing spot if desperate for WR depth later.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Bucs QB room**: Jalon Daniels takes over for Mayfield; first career NFL start comes against a Packers defense that's given up points in bunches.
- **Texans backfield**: Montgomery and Woody Marks are now close to an even snap split, with Marks winning the goal-line touches Sunday.
- **Jaguars backfield**: Coen explicitly framed it as a "shared workload" between Tuten and Rodriguez rather than a hierarchy — no lead back to trust yet.
- **Packers pass-catchers**: With Reed's timeline still unresolved, Watson and Golden are the top two options alongside Tucker Kraft; Jonnu Smith (35% snaps) and Mark Redman (32%) are absorbing extra work at TE.
