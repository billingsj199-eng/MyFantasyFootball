# Training Camp Report — September 29, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet overnight news cycle -- this run mostly cleans up Sunday/Monday fallout that hadn't made it into the feed yet: fresh clarity on Travis Etienne, Breece Hall, Bucky Irving and Mike Evans, plus a couple of Week 3 stat-line risers._

## Top Storylines

- **Travis Etienne Jr. is a confirmed OUT for Week 4 vs. Atlanta** after re-aggravating his hamstring against the Raiders. Fantasy takeaway: Alvin Kamara is the clear waiver priority in New Orleans, with CJ Donaldson and Kendre Miller also in the mix.
- **Breece Hall's Sunday quad injury is now officially "week-to-week,"** upgraded from the vague MRI-pending report. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a bench/IR-stash for at least this week; Braelon Allen is the handcuff to roster.
- **Bucky Irving was NOT injured** despite getting parked on the sideline late in Sunday's loss -- Todd Bowles confirmed he's full-go. Fantasy takeaway: with Baker Mayfield out, Irving (and Kenny Gainwell) profile for a bigger role in Tampa Bay's offense vs. Green Bay.
- **Geno Smith torched Detroit for 321 yards and 3 TDs** in Sunday's loss, connecting with rookie TE Kenyon Sadiq for one of the scores. Fantasy takeaway: a top streaming QB option for Week 4.
- **Mike Evans left Sunday's win over Arizona with a rib injury** stacked on top of an existing hip issue. Fantasy takeaway: day-to-day, "good chance" to play Week 4 vs. Denver, but check Wednesday's practice report before locking him in.
- **Jaycee Horn (Panthers CB) is headed to IR with a torn quad** and could miss the rest of the season. Fantasy takeaway: no offensive impact, but a real hit to Carolina's defense/DST outlook.
- **Tre' Harris quietly led the Chargers' receiving corps** with 6 catches on 7 targets for 76 yards. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward more Week 4 volume, a viable WR3/FLEX streamer.
