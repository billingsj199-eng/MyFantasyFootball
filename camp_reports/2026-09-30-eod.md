# Training Camp Report — September 30, 2026 (End of Day)

_Quieter Wednesday evening, highlighted by Jayden Reed's season-ending neck surgery and a round of Wednesday practice-report injury news. Puka Nacua's outlook brightened since the midday report. Thursday Night Football (Steelers @ Browns) locked in its injury list._

## Top Storylines

- **Jayden Reed (GB) is out for the rest of the 2026 season** and will undergo neck surgery, per HC Matt LaFleur. Fantasy takeaway: a full drop/IR-stash in all formats — Christian Watson and Matthew Golden inherit his target share in Green Bay's passing game.
- **Puka Nacua's (LAR) Week 4 outlook improved** — McVay now says he *expects* Nacua to play Sunday vs. Philadelphia after a limited (not DNP) Wednesday practice, an upgrade from the "targeting/TBD" language in today's midday report. Fantasy takeaway: trending back toward a locked-in LAR WR1 start.
- **Chris Godwin (TB) did not practice Wednesday** (ankle), adding to an already Mayfield-less Bucs passing attack. Fantasy takeaway: watch Thursday/Friday tags before trusting him vs. Green Bay.
- **Josh Allen (BUF) was a full practice participant Wednesday** with no brace, days after a hard hit to his knee Sunday. Fantasy takeaway: team signals he's fine and trending to start vs. NE, though outside medical opinions are split — confirm the final injury tag.
- **Thursday Night Football (Steelers @ Browns) injury list is set**: Rico Dowdle (toe) and Tylan Wallace (knee) are both OUT. Fantasy takeaway: Jaylen Warren remains Pittsburgh's clear workhorse; Isaiah Bond and Malachi Corley see a snap bump for Cleveland.
- **Lamar Jackson (BAL) and Drake Maye (NE)** both showed up on Wednesday injury reports (back; throwing shoulder) but each stayed on the field as full/limited participants with no real availability concern yet. Fantasy takeaway: non-issues for now, but worth a glance at Thursday/Friday tags.
