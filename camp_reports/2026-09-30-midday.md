- **Jayden Daniels (WAS) returns to practice (limited) in London**, with coach Dan Quinn saying there's "a chance" he plays Sunday vs. the Colts. Fantasy takeaway: real but unconfirmed uptick — treat as a game-time-decision-in-waiting rather than a lock, and don't bench him from waivers yet.
- **Jalen McMillan officially lands on IR** with a PCL sprain, expected out 6-8 weeks. Fantasy takeaway: closes the book on a short-term McMillan bounce-back; Egbuka/Godwin absorb more of Tampa Bay's target share for at least a month and a half.

Fantasy takeaway: keep a bench plan ready; Davante Adams remains the safer WR1 bet in LA until Nacua is confirmed active.
- **Mike Evans trending toward practice later this week** per Kyle Shanahan, who sounds optimistic about a Week 4 return from his rib injury vs. Denver. Fantasy takeaway: lean toward rostering him for Sunday, but confirm Thursday/Friday practice status before locking him into lineups.
