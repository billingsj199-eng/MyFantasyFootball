# Training Camp Report — October 1, 2026 (End of Day)

_Thursday's practice reports and TNF fallout: three Jets skill players (Breece Hall, Mason Taylor, Adonai Mitchell) are all "week-to-week" ahead of Sunday's Bears game, Nico Collins and Puka Nacua both keep trending toward Week 4 returns, Chig Okonkwo inches closer to a London return, and Zach Charbonnet opens his practice window in Seattle as Jadarian Price keeps fading._

## Top Storylines

- **Jets' offense takes a Thursday hit**: RB Breece Hall (quad), TE Mason Taylor (thumb) and WR Adonai Mitchell (finger) all missed practice for a 2nd straight day, with HC Aaron Glenn labeling the trio "week-to-week" ahead of Sunday at Chicago. Fantasy takeaway: Braelon Allen and Kenyon Sadiq are the clear beneficiaries if the group sits — check Friday's tags before trusting any of the three.
- **Nico Collins (HOU) and Puka Nacua (LAR) both keep trending toward Week 4 returns** after a 2nd straight limited practice apiece; both need a Friday upgrade to shed their injury tags. Fantasy takeaway: both playable gambles for Sunday, but confirm status before locking in lineups.
- **Chig Okonkwo (WAS) limited for a 2nd straight day** (hamstring) ahead of Sunday's London game vs. the Colts. Fantasy takeaway: trending the right direction but not yet confirmed — a streaming TE option at best until Friday's tag.
- **Zach Charbonnet (SEA) designated to return to practice**, opening a 21-day activation window, but he's not available for Week 4. Fantasy takeaway: Jadarian Price remains Seattle's lead back for now, but he's barely hanging onto flex value (under 8 PPR points in 3 straight games, 35% snap share) in a crowded committee that only gets more crowded once Charbonnet is back.
- **Jalen Ramsey played through a fractured wrist** for the Steelers on Thursday night vs. Cleveland, active despite the injury. Not a fantasy-relevant position, but worth knowing for both teams' secondary math when projecting opposing WRs down the stretch.
