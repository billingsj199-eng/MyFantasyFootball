# Training Camp Report — October 1, 2026 (Midday)

_Overnight/morning news ahead of tonight's Steelers @ Browns TNF: the Joey Porter Jr. trade, Thursday practice reports for several Week 4 questionables (DeVonta Smith, Rachaad White, Mike Evans), Austin Ekeler's signing in Washington, and a round of Week 3 stock-watch risers at RB/WR._

## Top Storylines

- **Steelers trade CB Joey Porter Jr. to the Cowboys** for a 2027 sixth-round pick and a 2028 second-rounder, ending a contract standoff. Fantasy takeaway: not fantasy-relevant directly, but weakens Pittsburgh's secondary and bolsters Dallas's — worth a glance for opposing-WR matchup plays in both cities going forward.
- **DeVonta Smith (PHI) did not practice Thursday** (hamstring), a repeat of last week's pattern before he ramped up Friday/Saturday. Fantasy takeaway: don't panic yet, but check Friday's tag before locking him in vs. the Rams.
- **Rachaad White (WAS) sat out practice for a 2nd straight day** (shoulder) in London. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward sitting Week 4 vs. the Colts — Jacory Croskey-Merritt stays the lead back, with newly-signed Austin Ekeler now a depth option.
- **Austin Ekeler officially signed with Washington's active roster**, with Leo Chenal going to IR to clear the spot. Fantasy takeaway: speculative add given White's shaky status, but no clear role yet.
- **Mike Evans (SF) missed his 2nd straight practice** (ribs) ahead of Sunday vs. Denver; GM John Lynch says Evans will "make every attempt" to play. Fantasy takeaway: trending toward a Friday game-time-decision tag — have a backup plan.
- **Week 3 stock watch**: Cardinals rookie RB Jeremiyah Love (21 carries, 90 yards, 5 catches, TD) looks like a true bell-cow; Dolphins WR Chris Bell (4/7 targets, 67 yards, 71% snaps) and Jaguars WR Josh Cameron (37% snap share, 2 TD on the season) are both trending up.
- **Ollie Gordon II's outlook firms up** now that De'Von Achane's season-ending ACL tear is officially confirmed — he projects as Miami's clear early-down back and a top Week 4 waiver add.
