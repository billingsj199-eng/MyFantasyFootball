# Training Camp Report — October 2, 2026 (End of Day)

_Final Week 4 tags are in: Justin Jefferson, Rachaad White, Hollywood Brown, DeVonta Smith, Dallas Goedert and Xavier Legette are all officially OUT; D'Andre Swift and Brock Bowers are confirmed good to go; and three new Friday-report questionables — Terry McLaurin, Zay Flowers and Ladd McConkey — join Mike Evans and Kenyon Sadiq as true game-time decisions. Travis Etienne lands on IR (earliest return Week 9), and Tampa Bay hands undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels his first career start._

## Top Storylines

- **Justin Jefferson (MIN) officially ruled out for a 2nd time** vs. Dolphins (ankle) — Kevin O'Connell confirms Jordan Addison and Jauan Jennings again lead the WR room Sunday. Fantasy takeaway: treat Addison/Jennings as the Week 4 Vikings WR plays, Jefferson as a bench.
- **Travis Etienne (NO) placed on IR** with the hamstring injury suffered vs. the Raiders — earliest eligible return is Week 9 at Cleveland. Fantasy takeaway: Alvin Kamara (lead role) and Kendre Miller (change-of-pace) own the Saints backfield for at least a month.
- **Three new Friday-report questionables emerge**: Terry McLaurin (WAS, hamstring — surprise addition), Zay Flowers (BAL, hamstring) and Ladd McConkey (LAC, new foot issue) are all legitimate game-time decisions for Sunday. Fantasy takeaway: have backup plans ready for all three; confirm actives before locking lineups.
- **Philadelphia's injury list balloons**: DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Hollywood Brown (ankle) and Dallas Goedert (knee) are all officially OUT vs. the Rams. Fantasy takeaway: Jalen Hurts' passing game runs through Dontayvion Wicks, Makai Lemon, Zach Ertz and Johnny Mundt this week — low-confidence streams at best.
- **D'Andre Swift (CHI) and Brock Bowers (LV) both clear up** — Swift practiced fully Friday and is off the injury report; Bowers carries no Week 4 designation after Friday's rest day. Fantasy takeaway: both are full-green-light Week 4 starts.
- **Rachaad White (WAS) and Xavier Legette (CAR) both officially OUT**, continuing absences with no firm return timeline from either team. Fantasy takeaway: Austin Ekeler/Jacory Croskey-Merritt (WAS) and Carolina's remaining WRs behind Legette gain work.
- **Tampa Bay turns to undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels** at QB vs. Green Bay with Baker Mayfield out at least 3 weeks (thumb) — first undrafted-rookie start in franchise history. Fantasy takeaway: downgrade confidence in all Bucs pass-catchers until Daniels shows something.
- **Cleveland backfield/TE depth flashes in Thursday's win over Pittsburgh**: Jaleel McLaughlin scored on a 28-yard TD run and Harold Fannin Jr. caught his 3rd TD in 2 games. Fantasy takeaway: both are speculative adds in a run-heavy, red-zone-efficient Browns offense.

## Injury Analysts

- **Dr. Deepak Chona** on Jayden Daniels' elbow: surgery is often recommended for this injury but is optional here, and reports suggest Washington is leaning nonoperative — if so, he projects a return timeline of roughly 4 weeks, which lines up with the team's own "door open for Week 5" guidance.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Buccaneers QB**: Undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels gets his first career start vs. Green Bay with Baker Mayfield (thumb) out through at least Week 6. Daniels won the backup job in camp on physical tools and poise.
- **Browns backfield**: Quinshon Judkins remains the clear lead back, but Jaleel McLaughlin (signed off the practice squad after Dylan Sampson's IR move) is carving out real change-of-pace touches and goal-line work.
- **Eagles passing game**: With Smith, Brown and Goedert all out, Dontayvion Wicks and Makai Lemon become Jalen Hurts' top outside options, and Zach Ertz/Johnny Mundt split the TE work vs. the Rams.

## Rookie Watch

- **Harold Fannin Jr. (CLE, TE)** — Caught his 3rd TD in his last 2 games Thursday night, a clutch 2-yard score with 1:22 left to beat the Steelers. Growing into a real streaming option at a shallow position.
- **Jalon Daniels (TB, QB)** — Undrafted rookie gets the start vs. Packers, the first undrafted-rookie QB start in Buccaneers history.

## Risers / Fallers

- **Riser: D'Andre Swift (CHI, RB) / Brock Bowers (LV, TE)** — Both clear the injury report entirely and are full-green Week 4 plays.
- **Riser: Jaleel McLaughlin (CLE, RB) / Harold Fannin Jr. (CLE, TE)** — Both flashed in Thursday's win and are speculative adds.
- **Faller: Justin Jefferson (MIN, WR) / Travis Etienne (NO, RB)** — Jefferson sits a 2nd straight week; Etienne is now on IR into November.
- **Faller: Philadelphia's passing-game stock (DeVonta Smith, Hollywood Brown, Dallas Goedert)** — All three officially out vs. the Rams.
