# Training Camp Report — October 2, 2026 (Midday)

_Friday is final-tag day for Week 4: the Jets officially rule out Breece Hall, Mason Taylor and Adonai Mitchell vs. Chicago; Jayden Daniels sits a 2nd straight week; DeVonta Smith trends toward missing Sunday while Puka Nacua, Nico Collins and Chig Okonkwo all trend toward playing; and D'Andre Swift becomes a late, unexpected Week 4 question mark in Chicago._

## Top Storylines

- **Jets officially rule out Breece Hall (quad), Mason Taylor (thumb) and Adonai Mitchell (finger)** for Sunday at Chicago — confirms Thursday's "week-to-week" tags. Fantasy takeaway: Braelon Allen and Kenyon Sadiq are locked-in Week 4 plays; no clear beneficiary emerges at WR behind Garrett Wilson.
- **Jayden Daniels (WAS, elbow) ruled out for a 2nd straight week** vs. Colts in London; Marcus Mariota starts again. Fantasy takeaway: Commanders' passing volume stays capped — treat WAS pass-catchers as lower-confidence plays until Daniels is back (door reportedly open for Week 5).
- **DeVonta Smith (PHI, hamstring) trending toward missing Sunday vs. Rams** — "not much optimism" per ESPN's Tim McManus and NFL Media's Mike Garafolo — and fellow WR Hollywood Brown (ankle) is right there with him after a 2nd straight DNP. Fantasy takeaway: Philly's passing game could be without both outside receivers Sunday.
- **Puka Nacua (LAR) and Nico Collins (HOU) both trend the right way** — Nacua practiced fully Thursday with McVay expecting him to play vs. the Eagles; Collins is probable after a 3rd straight limited session vs. Dallas. Fantasy takeaway: both playable again, but confirm the final tag before kickoff.
- **Chig Okonkwo (WAS) and Josh Allen (BUF) both clear up** — Dan Quinn confirms Okonkwo plays vs. Colts despite still being limited in practice, and Allen isn't even on Buffalo's injury report after last week's knee hit vs. the Chargers. Fantasy takeaway: both are full-strength Week 4 plays.
- **D'Andre Swift (CHI, knee) is a brand-new, late addition** to the injury report — DNP Thursday after a full Wednesday practice, with Friday's session now critical for Sunday vs. the Jets.
- **Rachaad White (WAS, shoulder) didn't practice all week** and sounds unlikely to play in London; newly signed Austin Ekeler is the top handcuff alongside Jacory Croskey-Merritt.
- **Keenan Allen (IND) reportedly facing a minimum 3-game suspension** stemming from an Aug. 30 DUI arrest (.224 BAC) — no immediate lineup impact since the NFL is expected to wait out his legal case (next court date Oct. 26), but worth monitoring.

## Injury Analysts

- **Dr. David Chao** on Josh Allen's knee: concluded Allen "did not suffer an injury" on last week's helmet shot — "more hurt than injured." Supports treating him as a normal Week 4 workload.
- **Dr. Jesse Morse** on the same play: disagrees, flagging a possible MCL injury despite Allen's clean practice week. Worth a note of caution even though Allen has no game-status tag.

## Depth Chart / Usage Notes

- **Saints backfield (post-Etienne IR)**: Alvin Kamara played 46.7% of Week 3's snaps (10 touches) once Travis Etienne left, with Kendre Miller at 31.1% (6 touches). Coach Kellen Moore expects a similar Kamara/Miller split Monday vs. Atlanta — Kamara projects as the better PPR floor play with the passing-down role.
- **Commanders backfield**: Austin Ekeler (newly signed) and Jacory Croskey-Merritt are the next men up if Rachaad White sits in London.
- **Eagles WR corps**: With DeVonta Smith and Hollywood Brown both trending out, keep an eye on snap-share shifts for the remaining PHI pass-catchers once Friday's report is final.
