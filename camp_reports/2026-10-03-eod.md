# Training Camp Report — October 3, 2026 (End of Day)

_Saturday night brings two new IR moves (Xavier Legette, Jadarian Price -- both out at least four games) and a round of final-week injury-report tightening ahead of Sunday's Week 4 slate: Terry McLaurin was downgraded to doubtful for the London game, while Puka Nacua got the best possible final word -- no game-status designation at all. Noah Fant and Jalen Coker round out the questionable list for their Sunday/Monday games, and Dr. David Chao backs up Buffalo's clean injury report on Josh Allen's knee._

## Top Storylines

- **Xavier Legette (CAR) placed on IR** with the knee injury that's kept him out two straight games; Carolina's Week 5 bye pushes his earliest return to Week 8 vs. Denver. Fantasy takeaway: drop in shallow leagues -- no near-term value.
- **Jadarian Price (SEA) placed on IR** after re-aggravating a chest injury in Wednesday practice. Fantasy takeaway: Emanuel Wilson and George Holani split Seattle's backfield for at least the next month.
- **Terry McLaurin (WAS) downgraded to doubtful** for Sunday's London game vs. Colts (hamstring), a step down from Friday's questionable tag. Fantasy takeaway: have Stefon Diggs and Antonio Williams ready as the top Commanders pass-catchers if he sits.
- **Puka Nacua (LAR) gets a clean bill of health** -- no injury designation at all for Sunday vs. Philadelphia, the strongest version of "he'll play" all week. Fantasy takeaway: locked-in WR1 play alongside Davante Adams, though watch for an early snap-count cap in his first game back.
- **Noah Fant (NO) and Jalen Coker (CAR) land on Saturday's questionable lists** -- Fant (abdomen) for Monday vs. Atlanta, Coker (quad) for Sunday vs. Detroit. Fantasy takeaway: both trending toward playing but confirm before kickoff.
- **Dr. David Chao says Josh Allen's knee is "hurt, not injured"** after Sunday's hit from the Chargers game. Fantasy takeaway: independent confirmation Allen is a normal-workload Week 4 QB1 vs. New England.
