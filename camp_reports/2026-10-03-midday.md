# Training Camp Report — October 3, 2026 (Midday)

_Saturday's Week 4 news is mostly about the London game and Saturday elevations: Keenan Allen (groin) is a surprise OUT for Colts-Commanders, while Tennessee gets good injury news with Tony Pollard fully cleared and Tyjae Spears trending toward playing. Lamar Jackson's early-week back scare is a non-issue -- he's off the injury report. Joe Mixon remains sidelined through Week 4 on the NFI list. Tyson Bagent is confirmed starting for the Bears, and two injury analysts flagged real re-injury risk for Nico Collins and Puka Nacua even though both are playing Sunday._

## Top Storylines

- **Keenan Allen (IND) ruled OUT for the London game** vs. Commanders (groin) after a questionable tag turned into a scratch. Fantasy takeaway: Josh Downs is the clear top Colts receiver, with Darius Slayton, Laquon Treadwell and TE Tyler Warren picking up extra targets.
- **Tennessee's backfield clears up**: Tony Pollard is off the injury report entirely (full go), and Tyjae Spears (ankle) is questionable after a full Friday practice. Fantasy takeaway: Pollard is a confident RB1 play vs. Baltimore; Spears trending toward active but confirm before locking a flex spot.
- **Lamar Jackson (BAL) fully clears the injury report** after an early-week back scare that the team called "minor." Fantasy takeaway: no lingering concern -- treat him as a normal-workload Week 4 QB1 vs. Tennessee.
- **Joe Mixon (HOU) remains out through Week 4** -- he wasn't activated off the NFI list by the Tuesday deadline for his ankle injury. Fantasy takeaway: Texans backfield stays a committee situation; Mixon's earliest eligible return is Week 5 vs. Baltimore.
- **Tyson Bagent confirmed as Bears starting QB** vs. Jets with Caleb Williams (hamstring, 3-4 week timeline) still out. Fantasy takeaway: streaming value only in deep two-QB leagues.
- **Injury analysts flag real risk on two plays**: Dr. Deepak Chona pegs Nico Collins' re-injury risk at 15-20% on his Week 4 return, and Dr. Jesse Morse says Puka Nacua is cleared to play but at moderately elevated re-injury risk with his groin/hip issue. Fantasy takeaway: both are starts, but neither is a lock for a full workload/day.
