# Training Camp Report — October 4, 2026 (End of Day)

_Sunday's injury carnage escalated into the afternoon and evening windows: Lamar Jackson left the Ravens' win with an ankle injury and was seen in a walking boot, Saquon Barkley exited early with a hamstring strain, and Ja'Marr Chase was knocked out on a facemask pull that put his head into the turf. Mike Evans gutted out a win on broken ribs, and the morning's Washington/Tampa Bay QB chaos resolved with Kaliakmanis and Jalon Daniels both throwing TDs in losses._

## Top Storylines

- **Lamar Jackson (BAL) exits at halftime with an ankle injury**, replaced by Tyler Huntley in a 24-18 win over Tennessee. Fantasy takeaway: Rapoport says "day-to-day," but a walking-boot sighting postgame makes his Week 5 status a real watch item.
- **Saquon Barkley (PHI) ruled out in the 1st quarter** of a 24-20 loss to the Rams with a hamstring injury. Fantasy takeaway: Tank Bigsby/Will Shipley handled the backfield in relief — treat Barkley as unconfirmed for Week 5 until a timeline emerges.
- **Ja'Marr Chase (CIN) ruled out with a concussion** after a facemask pull drove his head into the turf vs. Jacksonville. Fantasy takeaway: needs to clear protocol — watch Wednesday's practice window.
- **Mike Evans (SF) plays through fractured ribs** in a protective pad, catching 5-of-8 for 76 yards in a 24-14 win over Denver. Fantasy takeaway: full-confidence Week 5 WR if the ribs hold up through practice week.
- **Athan Kaliakmanis (WAS) throws his first NFL TD** in relief of the injured Marcus Mariota, finishing 15-33-186-1-1 in a 30-13 loss at Indianapolis. Fantasy takeaway: real flashes, but speculative-only given the interception and Washington's shaky offensive line.
- **Jalon Daniels (TB) throws a TD but tosses two picks** in his first career start, a 17-14 loss to Green Bay. Fantasy takeaway: streamer-only floor until Baker Mayfield (thumb) returns, possibly as early as Week 7.
- **Mack Hollins (NE) exits with a non-contact calf injury** vs. Buffalo after 3 catches for 53 yards. Fantasy takeaway: watch early-week reports — a thin Patriots WR room can't absorb another multi-week loss.
- **Braelon Allen (NYJ) handles every Jets carry** (14-59, plus 2 catches) in a loss to Chicago with Breece Hall (quad) out. Fantasy takeaway: confirmed lead-back role as long as Hall is sidelined.
