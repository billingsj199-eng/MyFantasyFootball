# Training Camp Report — October 4, 2026 (Midday)

_Sunday's early-slate storyline is Washington losing a second quarterback: Marcus Mariota re-aggravated his MCL in London and was ruled out after halftime, pushing rookie seventh-rounder Athan Kaliakmanis into his pro debut behind an already-Jayden-Daniels-less offense. Elsewhere, the Week 4 inactive lists mostly confirmed Saturday's reports (Nacua and Nico Collins in, Jefferson/Smith/Hall/McLaurin out), while a wave of secondary pass-catchers were cleared to play through questionable tags._

## Top Storylines

- **Marcus Mariota (WAS) exits with a re-aggravated right MCL**, ruled out after halftime of the London game vs. Colts. Fantasy takeaway: Washington is down to rookie Athan Kaliakmanis at QB — avoid Commanders skill players as streaming plays until there's clarity on both Daniels and Mariota.
- **Jalon Daniels makes his first career NFL start** for Tampa Bay vs. Green Bay with Baker Mayfield (thumb) still out. Fantasy takeaway: speculative streamer only; Bucs have simplified the playbook for him, capping upside for Mike Evans/Bucky Irving in a run-first gameplan.
- **Kenyon Sadiq (NYJ) active** despite a limited practice week (back), and set up for a huge target share with Mason Taylor (thumb), Adonai Mitchell (finger) and Omar Cooper (ankle) all out vs. Bears. Fantasy takeaway: strong streaming/waiver TE play off last week's 7-105-1 breakout.
- **Ladd McConkey (LAC) and Zay Flowers (BAL) both confirmed active** after carrying questionable tags into Sunday (foot, hamstring respectively). Fantasy takeaway: full-workload clearance for both WR1s, no expected snap caps.
- **Ja'Seem Reed elevated from Carolina's practice squad** as Jalen Coker (quad) trends toward sitting out Sunday night vs. Detroit. Fantasy takeaway: deep-league dart throw only if Coker is a late scratch.
- **Week 4 inactive lists mostly confirm Saturday's news**: Justin Jefferson, DeVonta Smith, Breece Hall, Aaron Donald, Terry McLaurin, Keenan Allen and Jadarian Price are all out; Puka Nacua and Nico Collins are both active and expected to play full workloads.
