# Training Camp Report — October 5, 2026 (Midday)

_Monday's news cycle is all about Sunday's injury carnage settling into firmer (if still uncertain) diagnoses: Lamar Jackson heads for an ankle MRI, Saquon Barkley's hamstring is "not considered overly serious," and the Chiefs absorbed a second ugly injury in Tyquan Thornton's dislocated ankle on top of Rashee Rice's hamstring. Washington lost returner/WR Jaylin Lane for the season on a gruesome ankle injury in London. Tonight's lone game is Falcons-Saints._

## Top Storylines

- **Lamar Jackson (BAL) scheduled for an ankle MRI Monday**, per Schefter. Fantasy takeaway: Florio called him "week-to-week" pending results — don't lock in a Week 5 Falcons start yet.
- **Saquon Barkley's hamstring "not considered overly serious"** after Monday tests, with a Week 6-8 return window being floated. Fantasy takeaway: Tank Bigsby is the lead-back bet in Philly for at least a couple weeks.
- **Chiefs lose Tyquan Thornton to a dislocated ankle** (out indefinitely, IR likely) on top of Rashee Rice's hamstring "tweak." Fantasy takeaway: both are bye-week news for KC — Rice projects fine after the break, Thornton's a depth-chart loss more than a must-replace fantasy piece.
- **Jaylin Lane (WAS) likely out for the season** with a fractured, dislocated ankle suffered returning a punt in London. Fantasy takeaway: marginal fantasy value, but Washington's receiver room keeps thinning out.
- **Terry McLaurin (WAS) officially inactive for Week 4** with the hamstring issue that popped up Friday. Fantasy takeaway: confirms the sit — watch Wednesday's report for his Week 5 trajectory.
- **Keon Coleman (BUF) breaks out (6-116-1) filling in for an injured DJ Moore.** Fantasy takeaway: priority waiver add, and a real flex play if Moore's shoulder lingers.
- **Tee Higgins (CIN) exits with an adductor injury**, leaving Cincinnati's WR room (already without Chase) depleted. Fantasy takeaway: monitor practice reports closely before trusting any Bengals passing-game piece in Week 5.
- **Baker Mayfield's thumb timeline widens to 3-6 weeks** per Schefter, though Pelissero notes a Week 6 return is possible if he beats the grip-strength benchmark. Fantasy takeaway: Jalon Daniels/streamers stay relevant into at least Week 6.
- **Puka Nacua's groin issue confirmed as a "pre-hernia"** by Dr. Jesse Morse. Fantasy takeaway: he's playing, but carries real re-aggravation risk the rest of the way — a notch of risk to price in even in good matchups.
