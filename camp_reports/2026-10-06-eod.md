# Training Camp Report — October 6, 2026 (End of Day)

_A genuinely quiet Tuesday -- no games, and teams don't release practice-participation reports until Wednesday. The day's real news was off the field: Lane Johnson's shock retirement reshapes the Eagles' line, and the Joe Mixon-to-Seattle deal that broke yesterday fell apart after his physical. Tank Bigsby got a clearer (if lengthy) runway back from his core-muscle surgery. Lamar Jackson's Week 5 outlook is still murky -- nothing has changed since this morning's report, so treat him as a game-time question until Wednesday's injury report._

## Top Storylines

- **Eagles RT Lane Johnson abruptly announces his retirement**, four games into his 14th season, citing a need to focus on his mental health. Fantasy takeaway: no direct fantasy value himself, but losing a 6-time Pro Bowl right tackle is a real downgrade to the Eagles' pass protection and running lanes -- worth watching how Jalen Hurts' pressure rate trends in Weeks 5-6.
- **Joe Mixon's deal with the Seahawks is off.** Schefter reports Seattle backed out after Mixon failed his physical, re-raising concerns about the foot injury that cost him all of 2025. Fantasy takeaway: take him off add/stash lists -- Seattle is instead signing depth back Robert Henry Jr. off Washington's practice squad.
- **Tank Bigsby's timeline comes into focus:** he's set for core-muscle surgery at the Vincera Institute, with the Eagles hopeful for a return in six weeks or sooner. Fantasy takeaway: with Barkley (hamstring) and now Bigsby both out, Will Shipley is PHI's lead back for the foreseeable future -- Bigsby is a Week 11-12 stash at best, not an imminent fix.
- **No new word on Lamar Jackson (ankle) since this morning** -- still framed as no better than a long-shot for Sunday at Atlanta, with Tyler Huntley the next man up. Wednesday's official injury report should bring real clarity.
