# Training Camp Report — October 6, 2026 (Midday)

_Quiet Tuesday as the league digests Monday night: the Bengals' Zac Taylor softened his stance on Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins to "day-to-day" rather than firm sits, Rashee Rice's hamstring got an encouraging downgrade in severity, and the Falcons' ground game buried New Orleans behind a monster Bijan Robinson game. No new hard practice-report news yet -- that starts Wednesday._

## Top Storylines

- **Zac Taylor backs off hard "out" framing on Chase and Higgins, calling both day-to-day.** Fantasy takeaway: neither is ruled out for Sunday at Miami yet, but Chase's concussion protocol clock makes him the bigger risk of the two.
- **Rashee Rice's hamstring now sounds "relatively minor,"** per Rapoport, with the Chiefs' bye putting a Week 6 return in play. Fantasy takeaway: treat him as a likely Week 6 starter, not a multi-week loss.
- **Bijan Robinson torches the Saints for 145 yards and 2 TDs** (including a 59-yard house call) in Atlanta's 45-24 Monday-night rout; Brian Robinson Jr. added 3 short scores and Michael Penix Jr. was efficient (15/20, 223 yards, 1 TD). Fantasy takeaway: Bijan is a weekly RB1, Penix a streamable QB2 as long as Atlanta keeps controlling games on the ground.
- **Baker Mayfield had stitches removed Monday**, with James Palmer reporting he's pushing to return after missing just two games instead of three-plus. Fantasy takeaway: don't give up on him as a Week 6 streamer option if grip strength cooperates.
- **Joe Mixon signs with the Seahawks** on a practice-squad deal after clearing a physical. Fantasy takeaway: speculative taxi-squad stash only, buried behind Seattle's active backfield for now.
- **Lamar Jackson's Monday MRI results still haven't been made public**, with Rapoport describing only an "outside" chance he plays Week 5 vs. Atlanta. Fantasy takeaway: still treat him as a legitimate question mark; no official Week 5 designation yet.
