## Top Storylines

- **Saquon Barkley and DeVonta Smith are both officially OUT** for Philadelphia's London game vs. Jacksonville. Fantasy takeaway: Will Shipley is the clear-cut lead back; A.J. Brown and a returning Dallas Goedert absorb the receiving work.
- **Dallas Goedert is back** — full practice participant Friday after missing two games with a knee injury. Fantasy takeaway: streamable TE1 upside Sunday with Smith out and Barkley drawing extra attention underneath.
- **Lamar Jackson is officially OUT** at Atlanta (ankle); Tyler Huntley confirmed as the starter. Fantasy takeaway: bench Jackson, stream Huntley only in deep two-QB formats.
- **Justin Jefferson and Jordan Addison both landed on Minnesota's final report as questionable** (ankle, hamstring) vs. New Orleans. Fantasy takeaway: both trending to play after practicing all week, but treat as true game-time decisions — Jefferson says the plan is to go.
- **Christian Darrisaw (concussion) was ruled OUT** at left tackle for Minnesota, Ryan Van Demark stepping in. Fantasy takeaway: added pass-protection risk if Jefferson/Addison do suit up.
- **Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins both closed the week as full practice participants**, questionable (concussion protocol / adductor) for Cincinnati at Miami. Fantasy takeaway: Chase still needs to clear protocol Saturday; both trending toward playing after a cleaner finish to the week than Wednesday suggested.
- **Jayden Daniels is on track to start** for Washington vs. the Giants (elbow) — full practice all week, with Garafolo and Glazer both saying he's trending to play. Fantasy takeaway: locks in as a starter if he's active Sunday.
- **Marcus Mariota is OUT** (knee MCL) for Washington — moot with Daniels trending to play, but confirms Athan Kaliakmanis as the emergency option if Daniels is downgraded.
