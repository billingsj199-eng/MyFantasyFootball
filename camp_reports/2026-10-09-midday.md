- **Justin Jefferson (ankle) was limited for a 2nd straight day Thursday**; he says the plan is to play Sunday vs. the Saints, but Minnesota hasn't issued its official tag yet. Fantasy takeaway: trending positive, not locked in — confirm Friday's designation before full WR1 trust.
- **Lamar Jackson (ankle) still hasn't practiced**; Tyler Huntley took first-team reps all week. Fantasy takeaway: no change from Wednesday's doubtful read — plan around Huntley at Atlanta.
- **Saquon Barkley (hamstring) missed practice again** with no official word yet on the London trip vs. Jacksonville. Fantasy takeaway: Will Shipley remains the top hedge/streamer if Barkley can't travel.
- **DeVonta Smith's outlook got worse**: ESPN's Garafolo called him "at very best questionable," and NBC Sports Philadelphia leans toward him sitting a 2nd straight game. Fantasy takeaway: bench/drop candidate in London until Friday's tag says otherwise.
- **Ja'Marr Chase (concussion protocol) still hasn't had a full practice**, and Cincinnati's Week 6 bye gives the Bengals incentive to be cautious. Fantasy takeaway: real doubt for Sunday at Miami — have a contingency plan.
- **DJ Moore's shoulder is "not considered a long-term issue"** per Adam Schefter after the Week 4 scare. Fantasy takeaway: encouraging sign for Monday night vs. the Rams.
- **Vikings G Donovan Jackson (knee) is likely out for the season** — a confirmed torn meniscus sent him to IR. No direct fantasy value, but it's a hit to Minnesota's run-blocking as Joe Huber steps in.
