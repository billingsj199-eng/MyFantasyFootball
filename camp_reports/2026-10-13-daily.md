# Daily News Scan — October 13, 2026

_Covers the aftermath of Monday night's Bills @ Rams overtime thriller plus Tuesday's early-week injury/insider notes heading into Week 6. Quiet transaction day otherwise — no fresh fantasy-relevant trades, signings, or releases found._

## Top Storylines

- **Bills escape Rams 37-34 in overtime** — Josh Allen passed for 3 TDs and ran for a 4th, adding 121 rush yards; Buffalo improves to 4-1 after Greg Rousseau blocked a potential game-tying field goal in OT, setting up Tyler Bass's walk-off.
- **Rams fall to 2-3 despite a huge night from Stafford** (40/54, 383 yds, 3 TD) and two Davante Adams touchdowns — Kyren Williams tied the game late in regulation but the defense couldn't close it out.
- **Caleb Williams' Week 6 status is a genuine insider split** — Rapoport says "all signs point" to him playing at Atlanta; Pelissero is more cautious and floats Week 7. Don't bench Bagent streamers until Friday.
- **Lamar Jackson has a "real chance" to return Week 6 vs. Cleveland** per Rapoport, after missing Week 5 with his ankle sprain — still unconfirmed.
- **Michael Penix Jr.'s rib injury (suffered vs. Baltimore) is "not considered serious,"** per Schefter — cautiously optimistic for Week 6 vs. Chicago, more clarity expected Wednesday.
- **TreVeyon Henderson (ankle) out "multiple weeks"** after exiting New England's blowout of the Raiders — no surgery needed, but a real near-term loss for the Patriots' backfield.
- **Tyquan Thornton's Week 4 ankle injury is worse than first described** — AP reports a dislocation and torn ligaments requiring surgery, 12-16 weeks out; effectively a lost season for the Chiefs WR.
