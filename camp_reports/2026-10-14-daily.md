## Top Storylines

- **Deebo Samuel (hamstring) will miss "at least a couple of weeks,"** per Kyle Shanahan — he's out for Monday night's trip to Washington, opening up more low-end WR2 volume for Ricky Pearsall and Jauan Jennings.
- **Will Shipley (plantar fascia, partial tear) could miss multiple weeks**, per Schefter — with Saquon Barkley (hamstring) also out, Philly's active backfield is down to Dameon Pierce and Carson Steele.
- **David Montgomery (groin) is "week-to-week"** and considered very likely to miss Houston's Week 6 London game vs. Jacksonville — Woody Marks is the clear waiver priority after a 19-touch workload Sunday.
- **CeeDee Lamb's quad bruise is "not considered to be serious,"** per Rapoport — Dallas expects him ready for the Oct. 18 trip to Green Bay.
- **Kyle Monangai's turf toe is "a bigger deal than we initially anticipated,"** per Bears coach Ben Johnson — D'Andre Swift is now the clear lead back in Chicago.
- **Quinshon Judkins (ankle) left Sunday's loss in a boot** but Browns coach Todd Monken calls it a day-to-day "tweak" and hasn't ruled him out for Week 6 vs. Baltimore.
- Caleb Williams (hamstring) and Lamar Jackson (ankle) remain the two biggest Week 6 swing votes league-wide — both still trending toward playing per Sunday's reporting, but no fresh Wednesday practice-report confirmation available yet; check Friday's injury report before locking lineups.
- Michael Penix Jr. (ribs) status for Sunday vs. Chicago was expected to firm up at Wednesday's team briefing — no update beyond "not considered serious" surfaced this cycle.
