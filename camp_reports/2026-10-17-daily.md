## Top Storylines

- **Saquon Barkley is officially OUT for Week 6 vs. Carolina** (hamstring) after missing every practice this week — and Will Shipley landed on IR too, so the Eagles' backfield behind him is thin.
- **DeVonta Smith is trending the other way** — full practice Friday and cleared to play vs. the Panthers, a real sign of life after two straight DNPs.
- **Jalen Hurts is formally on injured reserve** with the calf blood clot; there's still no return timetable, so Tanner McKee is the Eagles' starter for more than just this week.
- **Caleb Williams is a true game-time decision for Week 6 vs. Atlanta** — full practice Friday and officially Questionable, but Williams himself says the hamstring is actually torn (not the Grade 2 strain first reported) and he must clear a pregame stress test. Schefter/Rapoport still expect him to start.
- **Breece Hall is out again for the Jets** (quad) with no firm return week — Braelon Allen remains the entrenched every-down back.
- **Baker Mayfield (thumb) is out and expected to miss roughly 3–6 weeks** — Jalon Daniels gets a third straight start for Tampa Bay.
- **Tre' Harris will sit out Week 6** with the hamstring injury from Week 5; the Chargers' Week 7 bye pushes his realistic return to Week 8.
- **David Montgomery is officially out in London** (groin) — Woody Marks is a confirmed lead-back play, while Tank Dell still won't make his season debut.
- **Jeremiyah Love is officially ruled out** for Arizona (ankle) — Tyler Allgeier remains the early-down option.
