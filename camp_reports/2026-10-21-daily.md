# Daily News Scan — October 21, 2026

_Wednesday scan, Week 7. Yesterday's two open questions from Monday Night Football both resolved: Mike Evans re-aggravated his Achilles late in the first half and was ruled out, though Kyle Shanahan doesn't expect it to be long-term, while Terry McLaurin played through his hamstring issue with no designation — the "significant knee injury" rumor flagged (and discarded) yesterday did not happen. San Francisco held on to beat Washington 23-17 and improved to 6-0. Also confirmed: Caleb Williams' return from his hamstring injury was excellent — 30-of-36 for 305 yards and a touchdown in a 31-23 Bears win over Atlanta. Wednesday's practice reports bring the usual Week 7 injury-designation churn: Rashee Rice, Baker Mayfield, and Lamar Jackson are all notable QB/WR situations to track into Friday._

## Top Storylines

- **Mike Evans (SF) re-aggravated his Achilles and was ruled out of Monday night's win over Washington**, hurt on a 16-yard catch with 1:10 left in the first half. Shanahan said afterward he doesn't expect it to be a long-term issue, but there's no official timetable yet. **Fantasy: don't bench him preemptively, but have a Friday-report check built into your Week 7 plan.**
- **Caleb Williams (CHI) returned from his hamstring injury in style** — 305 passing yards, a touchdown, and a 111.2 rating in a 31-23 road win over Atlanta. **Fantasy: back in as a confident weekly starter; the hamstring is clearly no longer limiting him.**
- **Terry McLaurin (WAS) played Monday night with no injury designation**, closing the book on an uncorroborated rumor of a serious knee injury from this scan's prior report. **Fantasy: no new injury concern — treat him as a normal starter.**
- **Rashee Rice (KC) won't practice Wednesday** as he continues rehabbing the hamstring that's kept him out since Week 4; Andy Reid says he's making progress but gave no firm date. **Fantasy: trending toward a second straight missed game — Xavier Worthy and JuJu Smith-Schuster keep the extra work for Sunday night's trip to Seattle.**
- **Baker Mayfield (TB) took every rep in a full Wednesday practice** for the first time since his Week 3 thumb injury; Todd Bowles says he'll decide Friday between Mayfield and Jalon Daniels for the start at Carolina. **Fantasy: real streaming/matchup relevance returns if he's named the Week 7 starter — check back Friday.**
- **Lamar Jackson (BAL) got limited practice work Wednesday**, his first since spraining his ankle on Oct. 4. **Fantasy: no guarantee yet, but it's the most positive signal so far toward him suiting up Sunday against Cincinnati.**
- **Quinshon Judkins (CLE) was limited Wednesday with an ankle injury**, but the Browns' staff sounds optimistic about Week 7 vs. Tennessee. **Fantasy: trending toward active — a viable FLEX if he clears Friday's report.**
- **Christian Kirk (SF) is a "possibility" to return from his calf injury in Week 7**, per Shanahan. **Fantasy: worth a speculative stash given San Francisco just lost Mike Evans to the Achilles issue.**
