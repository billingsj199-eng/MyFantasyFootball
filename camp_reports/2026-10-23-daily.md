# Daily News Scan — October 23, 2026

_Friday scan, Week 7. - **Alvin Kamara (NO) came off New Orleans' injury report** after battling a back issue in practice this week, clearing him for Sunday's game in Paris vs. Pittsburgh. **Fantasy: locks back in as a full-workload RB2 play.**
- **Emeka Egbuka (TB) was ruled out** for Sunday vs. Carolina with a concussion/foot issue, his status unresolved from last week. **Fantasy: more volume for Tampa Bay's remaining healthy receivers.**
