# Daily NFL Fantasy News Scan — 2026-11-17

_Tuesday scan, ~6:10 PM ET, covering the window since Monday's scan (~6:18 PM ET) through today's Week 11 Tuesday practice reports. Includes Monday night's Ravens-Chargers recap, which hadn't kicked off as of yesterday's digest._

## Top Storylines

- **Chargers snap their 0-8 start, beating the Ravens 23-20 on Monday Night Football.** Jacoby Brissett went 33-for-49 for 340 yards in his Baltimore debut, but a late Zay Flowers fumble sealed it. **Fantasy: Ravens drop to 4-6 (five straight losses); Brissett's volume is a real floor for Mark Andrews, Flowers and Rashod Bateman with Lamar Jackson still out.**
- **Baker Mayfield (foot) is "trending toward sitting" Week 11 at Detroit, per Ian Rapoport.** **Fantasy: Jalon Daniels is the early favorite to start if Mayfield's out — he carries rushing upside but threw 4 INTs in his last three-start stretch.**
- **Marvin Harrison Jr. (hamstring) sat out of practice again Tuesday**, still without a clear Week 11 timeline after being ruled out for Week 10 vs. Cleveland. **Fantasy: monitor Wednesday/Thursday practice reports before trusting him in lineups.**
- **Wan'Dale Robinson landed on IR with an ankle injury** suffered in Tennessee's Week 10 loss to Jacksonville. **Fantasy: Elic Ayomanor and Chimere Dike step into the top two Titans WR roles for at least a month.**
- **Luther Burden III cleared concussion protocol** and is expected to play Sunday vs. New Orleans. **Fantasy: full green light for Bears lineups in Week 11.**
- **Houston's backfield flips: David Montgomery (groin) is trending to play Thursday vs. Indianapolis, while Woody Marks is unlikely to clear concussion protocol in time.** **Fantasy: Montgomery looks like a start if Marks sits, on a short week.**
- **Colts dealing with a mini injury wave two days before a short week at Houston** — Sauce Gardner (calf), Bernhard Raimann (concussion) and Will Mallory (hamstring) all missed Tuesday's practice; Charvarius Ward (knee) also sat out. **Fantasy: watch Wednesday's practice report for availability before Thursday's game.**

## Injury Report

- **Baker Mayfield (TB, QB)** — foot strain suffered late in the Week 9 loss to Chicago; has been in and out of a walking boot through the bye. Per Ian Rapoport, he's trending toward sitting out Sunday's game at Detroit, though Tampa Bay hasn't made it official and may wait until Friday. *Fantasy: plan for Jalon Daniels as the likely Week 11 starter; confirm Friday.*
- **Marvin Harrison Jr. (HOU, WR)** — hamstring; missed Thursday and Friday practice last week, was ruled out for Week 10 at Cleveland, and sat out again Tuesday. No outlet has given a firm return date. *Fantasy: hold off until a practice-participation change shows up.*
- **Wan'Dale Robinson (TEN, WR)** — ankle, hurt in the second half of Sunday's 40-23 loss to Jacksonville; placed on IR Tuesday per the team, out at least four games. *Fantasy: Elic Ayomanor and Chimere Dike are the direct beneficiaries; Xavier Restrepo was promoted off the practice squad for depth.*
- **Luther Burden III (CHI, WR)** — concussion suffered in the Week 9 win over Tampa Bay; Bears coach Ben Johnson confirmed Tuesday he's cleared all five protocol steps and worked out with teammates Monday. *Fantasy: expect a full practice week and no limitations Sunday vs. New Orleans.*
- **Woody Marks (HOU, RB)** — concussion from Sunday's loss to Cleveland; missed Tuesday's practice, and coach DeMeco Ryans said he's unlikely to clear the five-step protocol before Thursday's game at Indianapolis. *Fantasy: treat as a likely out pending Wednesday's report; David Montgomery would take the lead role.*
- **David Montgomery (HOU, RB)** — groin injury that cost him the last four games; was a full practice participant Monday and Tuesday and is trending to play Thursday. *Fantasy: clear path to a lead role if Marks sits, on a short week with extra rest value.*
- **Kyle Monangai (CHI, RB)** — turf toe/thumb, has missed five games; back in full pads for a second straight unofficial practice Tuesday and looks poised to return Sunday vs. New Orleans. *Fantasy: workload uncertain on return — watch Wednesday's official report and Friday's designation.*
- **Jacory Croskey-Merritt (WAS, RB)** — groin; designated to return from IR Tuesday, opening a 21-day window to be added to the 53-man roster. *Fantasy: would reclaim the lead Washington backfield role over Rachaad White if activated in time for Monday's game vs. Cincinnati.*
- **Ladd McConkey (LAC, WR)** — back spasms; didn't play a snap in Week 9 and sat out Monday's win over Baltimore entirely. Coach Jim Harbaugh called him day-to-day. *Fantasy: Tre' Harris is the direct beneficiary in the interim.*
- **Azeez Al-Shaair (HOU, LB)** — groin, needed surgery; placed on IR Tuesday, out at least four games. Not a fantasy-relevant position, but notable for Houston's defense (he was the team's leading tackler).
- **James Conner (ARI, RB)** — a correction worth flagging: some outlets this week conflated an "encouraging" LaFleur quote about guard/center Jon Gaines with Conner's situation. Conner remains on IR from his Week 3, 2025 foot/ankle surgery and has not practiced all season; per Arizona Sports, he's not expected back for Week 11 vs. Kansas City and still has no confirmed return date.
