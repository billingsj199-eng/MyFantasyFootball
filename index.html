How the JM Score has performed against actual NFL outcomes for the 2020-2024 rookie classes. Higher tiers should produce more studs and fewer busts.
Class Position Metric how this is computed
Outcome window: Best season in years 1-3 (1-4 for QB) of NFL career. Minimum 8 GP per season to qualify.
Elite: any qualifying season with PPG ≥ 15 (RB/WR), 11 (TE), 20 (QB) — a true top-6 rookie-window outcome. Validation lens only; does not feed the JM weights.
Stud: top-12 PPG at position (top-6 for QB/TE) in any qualifying season.
Hit: top-24 PPG (top-12 for QB/TE) in any qualifying season.
Contributor: avg PPG ≥ 8 (RB/WR), 12 (QB), 5 (TE) across qualifying seasons.
Bust: below those thresholds across the full window.
Pending: window not complete and no Hit/Stud yet (mostly 2024 class).
Eligible universe: all skill-position rookies from HISTORICAL_DRAFT_PICKS 2020-2024 (~80/yr).
Calibration: Hit Rate by JM Tier

HOW TO USE







📋


MY RANKINGS


Your personal custom rankings. Click a name for the full player card. Drag rows to reorder. Hover between rows to add a tier break. Hit Save to sync to the cloud. Drag and drop players to reorder them, add and remove tiers to organize by position group, export your rankings to a spreadsheet, use the trade calculator powered by your custom values, and mock draft using your personal board.

Position Sync: Toggle the SYNC switch next to the mode tabs to keep your positional rankings (RB1, RB2, WR1, etc.) and position-specific tiers identical between paired formats — Redraft ↔ Superflex and Dynasty 1QB ↔ Dynasty SF. Only the overall interleaving changes (QBs rank higher in SF). Move a player or add a tier in either mode and the other updates automatically.







⭐


JACK'S RANKINGS


Jack's official rankings for the 2026 fantasy football season. View his player valuations, tier breakdowns, and use them as a reference when building your own board. Free users can see the top 36 overall (or top 12 per position).







🏷️


TIERS


Hover between any two players and click "+ Add Tier" to create a tier break. Tiers are separate for each position filter — your WR tiers won't affect your ALL tiers. Click the tier name to rename it, or the ✕ to remove it.







🔄


TRADE CALCULATOR


Evaluate trades using your custom rankings or Jack's rankings as the value source. Add players and draft picks to each side to see which team wins the deal.







🎯


MOCK DRAFT


Practice drafting against AI opponents using your personal rankings as the ADP board. Pick your draft slot, number of teams, and rounds to simulate a real draft experience. Use Rookie mode to practice dynasty rookie drafts with only the 2025 class, sorted by KTC dynasty values.







📊


EXPORT


Download your current rankings view as a CSV file that opens in Excel. It exports whatever you're viewing — filter to a specific position to export just those players.







💾


SAVING


Sign in to save your rankings to the cloud. After making changes, hit the Save button in the toolbar. Your rankings sync across devices and persist between sessions.

Privacy Policy














MOCK DRAFT






⚙️


FORMAT


Redraft — standard 1-year leagues. Superflex — second QB slot, QBs spike up the board. Dynasty 1QB / SF — long-term values (rookies + youth weighted). Rookie — rookie-only mocks against the 2026 class.







📋


RANKINGS SOURCE


Which board the CPUs use when picking. Consensus — community aggregate. Jack's — Jack's official board (premium). My Ranks — your custom rankings from the Rankings page.







🎯


ADP SOURCE


Market ADP the CPUs reference for reach/value decisions. Underdog and Sleeper are most accurate for best-ball and dynasty respectively. KTC = KeepTradeCut dynasty values.







🤖


CPU STYLE


Mixed — weighted blend of all archetypes (default, most realistic). BPA — every CPU drafts Best Player Available; predictable, less variance. Aggressive — CPUs run hard archetypes; more reaches + position runs. Zero-RB — CPUs lean Zero/Hero-RB or WR-heavy, so RB value falls to you.







🪑


DRAFT SLOT & LINEUP


Number of Teams sets the field size. Your Pick chooses your draft slot (1 = first overall). Starting Lineup spinners define how many QB/RB/WR/TE/FLEX/SFLEX/K/D-ST slots and how many bench spots — affects roster size and total rounds.







⏱️


ON THE CLOCK


When it's your turn, search/filter available players or sort by RANK vs ADP. The board log on the right shows every pick. After the draft completes, you get a Grade Card + analytics (positional spend, build shape, value vs ADP).







🔧


LIVE DRAFT HELPER


For real Underdog drafts: install the Chrome extension (from the orange banner above) to get live BPA recommendations powered by Jack's rankings during your actual drafts. Free.

KPI STRIP


DRAFTS — count across the selected phase + contest filter. INVESTED — total $ across drafts, with avg entry fee below. PLAYERS — unique players you own, with total picks below. AVG BUILD — mean QB/RB/WR/TE count per roster across your portfolio.

EXPOSURE TAB


Every player you own, ranked by ownership %. Click any row to expand and see every draft you got them, the pick number, your slot, plus your top 5 most-common co-drafts. Click a tournament link to jump to that exact team on the Teams tab.







🏆


TEAMS TAB


Every draft with all 12 teams ranked by build grade — the Roster Construction score from historical BBM data (Superflex pools rank by projected season points instead). Click any team for the full roster (picks, projections, VOR, vs-ADP). Sort the list by rank, BB Proj, season total, build score, ADP value, or entry fee.







💎


VOR & ADP VAL


VOR (Value Over Replacement) — season pts above the BBM starter cutoff (QB12/RB30/WR42/TE13). Positive = above replacement. ADP val — sum of (ADP − pick number) across the roster. Positive = stole players late vs. market. Negative = reached.







🧱


BUILD SCORE & PATH CODES


BUILD % = implied BBM advance rate (baseline 16.7%) based on roster shape, position counts, round pattern, stacking, and structural fit. Hover the score for the breakdown. Path codes: RB — ZRO=Zero, HER=Hero, BAL=Balanced, HVY=Heavy, ROB=Robust. QB — EQB=Early, MQB=Mid, LQB=Late, VLQB=Very Late.







🔍


FILTERS


Phase — limit to a particular best-ball phase (regular, quarterfinal, etc.). Contest — check chips to keep specific tournaments (right-click a chip to view that contest only). All KPIs and tabs update live.

















PROSPECT MODEL






🎯


JM SCORE (0-100)


Jack's Model — a single number predicting a prospect's chance of becoming a fantasy contributor in their first 3 NFL years. Blends draft age, breakout age, college production, conference quality, RAS, draft capital, size, and teammate strength. Position-specific weights — what drives a QB hit is different from what drives a WR hit.







🏷️


TIERS & HIT RATES


Each JM range maps to a position-specific tier (Generational → Top Prospect → Starter → Contributor → Depth → Lottery Ticket → Long Shot). The % next to each tier is the historical 3-year hit rate based on 2017-2024 draft classes. "Hit" means clearing a position-specific fantasy threshold (QB 200+ fpts, RB/WR 170+, TE 120+, half-PPR).







📋


DC (DRAFT CAPITAL)


NFL overall pick number. Lower = earlier = more team investment. UDFA = undrafted free agent. DC heavily shifts JM upward for 2026 prospects once drafts happen — a Day 1 pick gets a meaningful boost, a UDFA gets a meaningful penalty.







🏃


RAS, FILM, TEAMMATE


RAS (Relative Athletic Score, 0-10) — composite combine/pro-day athleticism vs. position peers. FILM — PFF tape grade for the player's final college season. TM (Teammate score) — quality of the college supporting cast (line, QB play, surrounding skill players) which adjusts production credit.







🎂


DRAFT AGE & BREAKOUT


Draft Age — age on draft day. Younger declarations historically hit at higher rates. Breakout Age — youngest college season where the player crossed the position's dominator threshold (early breakouts predict NFL success better than late ones).







📊


RISK INDICATOR


Adjustments to raw JM based on bust/sleeper signals (extreme age, low breakout, athletic outliers). Red ▼ means the model is fading the player versus their components alone; green ▲ means it's boosting. Hover the cell for the specific drivers.







🧪


BACKTEST


Every component weight was tuned on 2017-2024 actual NFL outcomes — see the Backtest page for tier-by-tier hit rates, calibration, and how the current weights compare to alternatives. Free to access.







💡


HOVER ANY COLUMN HEADER


JM, TIER, RISK, DC, DRAFT AGE, BREAKOUT, RAS, TM, FILM all show a tooltip on hover with a one-line definition. Look for the dotted-orange underline.
