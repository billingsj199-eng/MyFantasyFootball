Model: Clay season projections rescored to the chosen format, split per-week, shifted by the DraftKings implied team total and the opponent's position-specific defense (Clay unit grades — CB/S/ED vs passing, DI/LB/ED vs the run, S/LB vs TEs; capped ±8%), then Monte-Carlo'd with each player's own 3-year weekly volatility (gamma distribution, teammates correlated through a shared game-environment factor). In-season, every sim on every tab samples around the JS Weekly blend (Clay prior shrunk toward actual 2026 PPG, prior worth ~5 games — beat pure Clay at every 2019–25 mid-season checkpoint); preseason the blend equals Clay identically.
Full season outcome: every player's entire schedule simulated week by week — byes, Vegas implied totals, opponent defense, QB/injury windows and rookie ramps all shape each week, and game environments are shared league-wide within each simulated season, so booms line up with shootouts. You get season-total distributions (p10 / median / p90) plus positional finish odds — how often each player ends the range as the #1 / top-3 / top-12 / top-24 at his position within the same sim. Narrow the week range for a stretch run (e.g. 15–17 = fantasy playoffs). Calibrated on a 2019–25 backtest (backtest_sim_calibration.py): each simulated season draws a per-player season shock — a projection-scaled chance the season busts to 5–50% of projection (~1-in-8 for elite projections, up to ~1-in-4 for deep-roster flyers — matching the real wreck rates of top-projected players), otherwise mild drift that's also narrower for elites (a 350-pt stud can't 1.6× like a bench flyer can) — and players Clay projects for <17 games miss weeks at that rate. Without these layers only 31% of real season totals landed in the sim's p10–p90 band; with them it's 80.4% (target 80), below-p10 10.6% vs 10, above-p90 8.9% vs 10 — calibrated per projection tier, not just pooled — and the best top-12 finish-odds Brier score.
Real league: paste your Sleeper league ID (the long number in the league URL) — or an ESPN or Yahoo league ID/URL (publicly viewable leagues only: private ones must be set viewable-to-public in the platform's league settings; ESPN/Yahoo players are matched by name into the projection pool). Rosters, scoring, lineup slots and playoff settings all come from the league API; Sleeper and ESPN bring real weekly matchups too, while Yahoo exposes no matchup feed so its schedule is a synthesized round-robin (records stay real). PRIVATE leagues: import them on the MFF site's My Teams tab (the ESPN/Yahoo extensions bridge private leagues there), then LOAD FROM MFF here — signs into your MFF account and pulls every league you've saved (roster snapshot as-of the last site sync; round-robin schedule, playoffs assumed wk15/6). Every sim replays the remaining schedule with correlated player scores — league-wide, so two teams starting players from the same NFL game share that game's script. Each simulated season also draws a per-player season shock (wrecked-season mixture — same layer as SEASON SIM) that persists through that sim's remaining weeks and playoffs: the mid-season backtest (weeks 5/9/13, 2019–25) showed rest-of-season outcomes need the full shock all year, so playoff/title odds here run cooler and honester than before.

Team pace & play-calling tracker: season-to-date tendencies vs a coach-aware baseline — same coach = the team's own 2025 identity; new coach = his prior pace/no-huddle/2-TE identity (those travel with coaches) but the roster's pass mix (the QB decides that). Validated 2019–25: drift a team shows by week 8 persists the rest of the season (no-huddle r .78, 2+TE .75, PROE .61, neutral pass .57, plays .44). Intel only: converting drift into projection multipliers was backtested (weeks 5/9/13, 2019–25) and made player means worse — Vegas totals and realized PPG already price it at player level — so the sims don't use it. What it's for: spotting a new coordinator's real identity by ~week 3, weeks before Clay/consensus react. Refresh: python pull_pace_tracker.py (in the daily deploy chain).
Target-area zones (intel only): where each defense gets targeted (share of targets faced by depth and side vs league = the funnel) and what it allows per target there, next to each receiver's own target mix (behind LOS / 0–9 / 10–19 / 20+ air yards, left / middle / right) and aDOT. Backtested 2019–25 (backtest_target_area.py): receiver mix is a real, sticky profile (deep share YoY r .75, behind-LOS .88); a defense's funnel is a soft scheme identity (early→late r .2–.5); a defense's per-zone efficiency beyond its overall rate is noise (r ~0 in-season and YoY) — quote it as "so far", never as an identity. The matchup multiplier graded flat, so the sims do not use any of this. Season-to-date numbers are shrunk toward the 2025 profile (receivers 40 targets, defenses 60) so Week 1–3 reads are not pure noise. Scheme & alignment (PFF Premium weekly facets, build_scheme.py): man rate, blitz, pressure, rusher win rate, safety-in-box, lanes a defense gets run at and its run-D results, next to each player's profile (man/zone YPRR, slot share, gap vs zone, edge share, yards after contact, QB under pressure / vs blitz). Identities (man rate, blitz, lanes, run style) persist; per-lane and per-zone efficiency graded as noise — intel only. Coaches: the prior each card compares against follows the playcaller (PFR staffs via pull_coaches.py; HC playcallers in coach_overrides.json). The Scheme backtest block at the bottom of the league view shows every interaction's LOYO verdict and each metric's persistence (backtest_scheme.py).
Video prep notes: a TD-luck leaderboard (who is due to regress up or down, from the shipped RB / WR-TE / QB luck layers: expected TDs from every touch's field position and depth vs actual, season-to-date) and a one-page sheet per game that pulls together everything the engine knows: line and implied totals, weather, pace drift, the opposing defense's points allowed / pressure / CB1 / OL status, the zone funnel, and every player's projection with the layers that moved it (luck, weather, pressure, CB, OL, snap and route trends, injury docks) plus his zone read.
