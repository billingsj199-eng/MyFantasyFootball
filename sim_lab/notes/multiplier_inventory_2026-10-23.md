# Sim engine conditional multiplier inventory - 2026-10-23

Every player-level multiplier / dock / boost / floor / cap in `sim_lab/engine.js` (+ `overrides.js`; `export_site_proj.js` adds no
player multipliers of its own - only the boom/bust display thresholds 1.5 / 0.5 and the debut label) that fires on a SITUATION.
Base-model pieces that always run (ADP market prior, ridge prior, position-mean shrink, usage evidence, prop anchor weights, Vegas
elasticity, sigma) are listed at the end as "always-on context", not as situational layers.

Columns: FLAT = the same number no matter who the replacement / backup / opponent / lineman is; CTX = already scales with a measured
quantity. RATING? = could a player quality rating or a quality GAP (Jack 10-23: "Josh Allen to an above-average backup is a bigger
downgrade than a bad starter to a mid backup") drive it, and which rating. Weeks: cur = current (per-team, `cwOf`) week only,
later = weeks after it, all = every projected week. Evidence = the backtest that put the number there ("none recorded" = judgment).
